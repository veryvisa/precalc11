🌿 Calm, stuck, wrong平静 · 卡住 · 做错

Three short cards for the moments math feels hard. Timers on this site only run if you start them.三张小卡片，给数学变难的时刻。本站的计时器只有你自己点开始才会走。

😮‍💨 Two minutes before a test考前两分钟

Nerves are normal and they do not mean you are bad at math. This routine takes two minutes. Do it at your desk or in the hallway.紧张很正常，并不代表你数学不好。这套小练习只要两分钟，在座位上或走廊里都能做。

  1. 0:00–0:45 Breathe slowly: in for 4 counts, out for 6. Five times. Let the long breath out do the work.慢慢呼吸：吸气数 4 下，呼气数 6 下，做五次。把注意力放在长长的呼气上。
  2. 0:45–1:30 Write the worry down in one sentence on scrap paper ("I'm scared I'll blank on trig"). Putting it on paper frees up working memory.在草稿纸上用一句话写下担心的事（「我怕三角题脑子一片空白」）。写出来，大脑就腾出了地方。
  3. 1:30–2:00 Say to yourself: "My heart is fast because my body is getting ready." Then find the first question you are sure about and start there.对自己说：「心跳快，是身体在做准备。」然后找到第一道有把握的题，从它开始。

🧱 When you are stuck卡住的时候

Being stuck is part of doing math, not a sign you can't. Work down this list; stop as soon as you can move.卡住是做数学的一部分，不代表你不行。按顺序往下试，能往前走了就停。

  1. Given: read it again and underline every number and condition.已知：再读一遍，把每个数字和条件划出来。
  2. Asked: circle what the question wants. Write "Find: …" at the top of your work.求什么：圈出题目要的东西，在草稿最上面写「求：……」。
  3. Picture: sketch it — a number line, a parabola, a triangle, a table.画出来：画个草图——数轴、抛物线、三角形或表格。
  4. Tool: which topic does this look like? Open that topic's "How to get started" card.用什么工具：它像哪个知识点？打开那个知识点的「怎么入手」卡片。
  5. Make it smaller: do only the first step (a restriction, a formula), or try easier numbers.把它变小：只做第一步（写限制条件、写公式），或者先换成简单的数试试。
  6. Still stuck after 3 minutes? Mark it, move on, come back later. Coming back works more often than you expect.3 分钟还卡着？做个记号，先做别的，回头再来。回头再做，常常就通了。

🔁 When you get it wrong做错的时候

A wrong answer is information, not a verdict. Fixing one mistake properly is worth more than doing three new questions.错题是信息，不是判决。认真改对一道，比多做三道新题更有用。

  1. Find the FIRST line that is wrong. Compare your work with the solution, line by line.找到「第一行」出错的地方，和答案逐行对照。
  2. Name it: slip (copying, sign, arithmetic) · rule (used a rule the wrong way) · idea (did not know why) · reading (misread the question).给它起名：手误（抄错、符号、计算）· 规则（规则用错）· 概念（不懂为什么）· 读题（看错题目）。
  3. Fix only that line, then finish the question again from there.只改那一行，然后从那里重新做完。
  4. Within a day, do ONE similar question. The site's mistake list brings it back for you.一天之内再做「一道」类似的题。本站的错题本会把它送回来。
  5. Slips are normal for everyone. If the same "rule" or "idea" mistake comes back, open the topic's prerequisite check.手误人人都有。如果同一个「规则」或「概念」错误反复出现，去做这个知识点的先修检查。
