Angles in standard position标准位置角

  • Standard position: vertex at the origin, initial arm on the positive x-axis. Counterclockwise rotation is positive, clockwise is negative. Where the rotation stops is the terminal arm.标准位置：顶点在原点，始边在 x 轴正半轴。逆时针旋转为正角，顺时针为负角。旋转停下的那条边叫终边。
  • The reference angle is the acute angle between the terminal arm and the x-axis (never the y-axis). Q I: θ; Q II: 180∘−θ; Q III: θ−180∘; Q IV: 360∘−θ.参考角是终边与 x 轴（不是 y 轴）所夹的锐角。第一象限：θ；第二象限：180∘−θ；第三象限：θ−180∘；第四象限：360∘−θ。
  • Coterminal angles share a terminal arm: add or subtract 360∘ any number of times, θ+360∘n.终边相同的角（同终边角）：加或减任意次 360∘，即 θ+360∘n。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Measuring the reference angle to the y-axis. For 120∘ the reference angle is 60∘ (to the x-axis), not 30∘.把参考角量到 y 轴。120∘ 的参考角是 60∘（量到 x 轴），不是 30∘。
  • Using 180∘−θ in every quadrant. Sketch first, then pick the rule for that quadrant.每个象限都用 180∘−θ。先画草图，再按所在象限选公式。
  • Thinking −60∘ is coterminal with 60∘. Negative goes clockwise: −60∘ is coterminal with 300∘.以为 −60∘ 与 60∘ 终边相同。负角顺时针转：−60∘ 与 300∘ 终边相同。

🎯 Always draw a 3-second sketch: axes, terminal arm, and a little arc to the x-axis. Almost every reference-angle error comes from skipping the sketch.每次都花 3 秒画草图：坐标轴、终边、一小段到 x 轴的弧。参考角出错几乎都是因为没画图。

Special angles & the unit circle特殊角与单位圆

  • 45-45-90 triangle: sides 1,1,2​. 30-60-90 triangle: sides 1,3​,2 (short leg opposite 30∘, hypotenuse 2). Draw them; do not memorize tables.45-45-90 三角形：边长 1,1,2​。30-60-90 三角形：边长 1,3​,2（30∘ 对短直角边，斜边为 2）。画出来，不用背表。
  • On the unit circle (radius 1), the point on the terminal arm of θ is (cosθ, sinθ), and tanθ=cosθsinθ​.在单位圆（半径为 1）上，角 θ 终边上的点是 (cosθ, sinθ)，tanθ=cosθsinθ​。
  • For any special angle: (1) find the reference angle; (2) take the value from the triangle; (3) attach the sign from the quadrant (CAST: All positive in Q I, Sin in Q II, Tan in Q III, Cos in Q IV).任何特殊角三步走：(1) 求参考角；(2) 从三角形读出数值；(3) 按象限定符号（CAST：第一象限全正，第二象限 sin 正，第三象限 tan 正，第四象限 cos 正）。
  • Quadrantal angles come straight from the unit circle: 0∘→(1,0), 90∘→(0,1), 180∘→(−1,0), 270∘→(0,−1). tan90∘ and tan270∘ are undefined.轴上角直接看单位圆：0∘→(1,0)，90∘→(0,1)，180∘→(−1,0)，270∘→(0,−1)。tan90∘、tan270∘ 无定义。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Mixing up the 30-60-90 sides: the side 1 is opposite 30∘, 3​ is opposite 60∘. The biggest side (2) is the hypotenuse, opposite 90∘.30-60-90 的边弄混：边 1 对 30∘，3​ 对 60∘。最长边 2 是斜边，对 90∘。
  • Getting the value right but the sign wrong: cos135∘ is −22​​, because cosine is negative in Q II.数值对了符号错了：cos135∘=−22​​，因为第二象限 cos 为负。
  • Reading tan2θ as tan(θ2). It means (tanθ)2.把 tan2θ 理解成 tan(θ2)。它表示 (tanθ)2。

🎯 At the start of a trig test, draw both special triangles and the CAST letters in the margin — 20 seconds that prevents every exact-value slip. If a question says "exact", a decimal answer gets no marks.三角测验一开始就在空白处画好两个特殊三角形和 CAST 字母，20 秒能避免所有精确值失误。题目说「精确值」时，写小数不得分。

Trig ratios of any angle任意角的三角比

  • Take any point P(x,y) on the terminal arm. Its distance from the origin is r=x2+y2​, always positive.在终边上任取一点 P(x,y)，它到原点的距离 r=x2+y2​，永远为正。
  • sinθ=ry​, cosθ=rx​, tanθ=xy​. The signs of x and y make the ratios positive or negative — that is where CAST comes from.sinθ=ry​，cosθ=rx​，tanθ=xy​。x、y 的符号决定比值的正负——CAST 就是这样来的。
  • Given one ratio and the quadrant: draw the point, fill in the missing coordinate with x2+y2=r2 (pick its sign from the quadrant), then write the other ratios.已知一个比值和象限：画出点，用 x2+y2=r2 补出缺的坐标（符号由象限决定），再写其他比值。
  • To find θ: get the reference angle from the POSITIVE ratio (e.g. tan−1​xy​​), then place it in the right quadrant.求 θ：用比值的绝对值求参考角（如 tan−1​xy​​），再放到正确的象限。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Giving r a negative sign. r is a distance, always positive; only x and y carry signs.给 r 加负号。r 是距离，永远为正；只有 x、y 带符号。
  • Typing tan−1(−32​) and writing −33.7∘ as the final answer when the question wants 0∘≤θ<360∘. Convert with the reference angle.题目要求 0∘≤θ<360∘，却直接写计算器给的 −33.7∘。要用参考角换算。
  • Choosing the wrong sign for the missing coordinate. Decide the quadrant first, then the sign of x or y.缺的坐标符号选错。先确定象限，再定 x 或 y 的符号。

🎯 Sketch the point and a right triangle down to the x-axis every time — the triangle gives the numbers, the quadrant gives the signs. For "find θ" questions, list ALL angles in the stated range, not just the calculator one.每次都画出这个点，并向 x 轴画一个直角三角形——三角形给数值，象限给符号。「求 θ」时，要写出规定范围内的所有角，不只是计算器给的那一个。

Simple trig equations简单三角方程

  • Step 1: isolate the trig ratio, e.g. 2sinθ+1=0⇒sinθ=−21​. If sin or cos ends up bigger than 1 or less than −1, there is no solution.第一步：把三角比单独留下，如 2sinθ+1=0⇒sinθ=−21​。若 sin 或 cos 大于 1 或小于 −1，无解。
  • Step 2: reference angle from the POSITIVE value: special triangle if exact, or the inverse key for decimals.第二步：用正值求参考角：特殊值看特殊三角形，小数用反函数键。
  • Step 3: the sign tells you which two quadrants (CAST). Step 4: build each angle from the reference angle with the quadrant rules.第三步：由符号和 CAST 确定是哪两个象限。第四步：在每个象限里用参考角写出对应的角。
  • Values of 0, ±1 are quadrantal: read them off the unit circle (sinθ=1⇒θ=90∘ only).0、±1 对应轴上角，直接看单位圆（sinθ=1⇒θ=90∘，只有一个）。
QI: θR​QII: 180∘−θR​QIII: 180∘+θR​QIV: 360∘−θR​
Angles from the reference angle由参考角写出各象限的角
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Stopping at the calculator answer. Inverse keys give one angle, but in 0∘–360∘ every tangent value, and every sine or cosine value strictly between −1 and 1, has two answers.停在计算器给的答案上。反函数键只给一个角；而在 0∘–360∘ 内，任何 tan 值、以及严格介于 −1 和 1 之间的 sin、cos 值都有两个解。
  • Putting a negative number into the reference-angle step: use cos−132​, not cos−1(−32​), when you then apply quadrant rules.求参考角时代了负数：要用 cos−132​，而不是 cos−1(−32​)，再套象限公式。
  • Calculator in radian mode. Check: sin30 should give 0.5.计算器在弧度模式。检查：sin30 应该得 0.5。

🎯 Write the two quadrants next to the equation before you touch the calculator ("sin < 0 → Q III, Q IV"). Round only the final angles; use the unrounded reference angle in your subtractions.按计算器之前，先在方程旁写下两个象限（如「sin < 0 → 第三、四象限」）。只对最后的角四舍五入；做减法时用未取整的参考角。

The sine law正弦定理

  • In any triangle, each side divided by the sine of its opposite angle gives the same number: sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​. Lowercase a is the side opposite angle A.在任何三角形中，每条边除以它对角的正弦，得数都相同：sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​。小写 a 是角 A 的对边。
  • Use it when you know a complete pair (a side AND its opposite angle) plus one more side or angle: AAS, ASA, or SSA.当你知道一组完整的「边—对角」，再加一条边或一个角时使用：AAS、ASA 或 SSA。
  • Finding a side: put the unknown side on top, sinBb​=sinAa​. Finding an angle: put sines on top, bsinB​=asinA​. The angles always add to 180∘ — use that for the third angle.求边：未知边放分子，sinBb​=sinAa​。求角：正弦放分子，bsinB​=asinA​。三角之和为 180∘，第三个角用它求。
sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​
Sine law (finding sides)正弦定理（求边）
asinA​=bsinB​=csinC​
Sine law (finding angles)正弦定理（求角）
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Pairing a side with the wrong angle. Label first: side a sits across from angle A, never touching it.边和角配错。先标好：边 a 在角 A 的对面，不和它相接。
  • Trying the sine law with SAS or SSS. Without a complete side–angle pair it cannot start — use the cosine law.已知 SAS 或 SSS 还用正弦定理。没有完整的「边—对角」就开不了头——要用余弦定理。
  • Rounding sinB early (e.g. to 0.7) and getting 44.4∘ instead of 44.8∘. Keep the full value in the calculator.过早把 sinB 取整（如取 0.7），结果得 44.4∘ 而不是 44.8∘。计算器里保留完整数值。

🎯 Always sketch and label the triangle, and circle the complete side–angle pair before writing any equation. In context questions, the sine law usually finds a slanted side first; a right-triangle step then gives the height or width.先画图标字母，在列式前圈出那一组完整的「边—对角」。实际问题中，正弦定理通常先求出一条斜边，再用直角三角形求高度或宽度。

The ambiguous case (SSA)两解情形（SSA）

  • The ambiguous case is SSA: you know ∠A, its opposite side a, and another side b. Compute the height h=bsinA — the shortest distance the swinging side a must reach.两解情形就是 SSA：已知 ∠A、它的对边 a 和另一边 b。先算高 h=bsinA——摆动边 a 至少要这么长才够得着。
  • If ∠A is acute: a<h → no triangle; a=h → one right triangle; h<a<b → TWO triangles; a≥b → one triangle.若 ∠A 为锐角：a<h → 无三角形；a=h → 一个直角三角形；h<a<b → 两个三角形；a≥b → 一个三角形。
  • If ∠A is obtuse: a>b → one triangle; a≤b → none.若 ∠A 为钝角：a>b → 一个三角形；a≤b → 无三角形。
  • When there are two triangles, the sine law gives B1​; the second is B2​=180∘−B1​ (same sine). Finish each triangle separately.有两个三角形时，正弦定理先给出 B1​；另一个是 B2​=180∘−B1​（正弦值相同）。两个三角形分别解完。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Stopping at B1​ from the calculator and missing the second triangle. In SSA, always test 180∘−B1​.只用计算器得出的 B1​，漏掉第二个三角形。SSA 一定要检验 180∘−B1​。
  • Comparing a with bsinB or asinA. The height is h=bsinA: the known angle with the side that is NOT opposite it.拿 a 和 bsinB 或 asinA 比较。高是 h=bsinA：已知角配上「不是」它对边的那条边。
  • Reusing the first triangle's C and c for the second. Each triangle has its own third angle and third side.把第一个三角形的 C、c 照搬给第二个。每个三角形都有自己的第三个角和第三条边。

🎯 Every SSA question: compute h=bsinA first and write the comparison ("h<a<b, so two triangles"). Teachers mark the classification separately — and it tells you whether to look for a second answer.每道 SSA 题都先算 h=bsinA，并写出比较（「h<a<b，所以有两个三角形」）。老师会单独给分类分——它也告诉你要不要找第二个解。

The cosine law余弦定理

  • c2=a2+b2−2abcosC. It is Pythagoras with a correction term; when C=90∘, cosC=0 and it becomes c2=a2+b2.c2=a2+b2−2abcosC。它是带修正项的勾股定理；当 C=90∘ 时 cosC=0，就变回 c2=a2+b2。
  • Use it for SAS (two sides and the angle BETWEEN them → find the third side) and SSS (three sides → find an angle).用于 SAS（两边及其「夹角」→ 求第三边）和 SSS（三边 → 求角）。
  • For an angle, rearrange first: cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​. A negative value means C is obtuse — the inverse cosine handles that correctly, no ambiguity.求角先变形：cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​。值为负说明 C 是钝角——反余弦能正确给出，没有歧义。
c2=a2+b2−2abcosC
Cosine law (find a side)余弦定理（求边）

Cosine law (find an angle)余弦定理（求角）
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Doing the order of operations wrong: 64+121−176cos37∘ is NOT (64+121−176)cos37∘. Multiply 2abcosC first, then subtract.运算顺序错：64+121−176cos37∘ 不是 (64+121−176)cos37∘。先算 2abcosC，再相减。
  • Matching the wrong side with the angle: in c2=a2+b2−2abcosC, c must be the side OPPOSITE C.边与角配错：c2=a2+b2−2abcosC 中，c 必须是 C 的对边。
  • Forgetting the square root at the end and reporting c2 as c.最后忘了开平方，把 c2 当成 c。
  • In bearing problems, using the bearing itself as the triangle angle. Draw north lines at every point and find the interior angle.方位角题直接把方位角当作三角形内角。要在每个点画指北线，再求内角。

🎯 Decide the law in 5 seconds: complete side–angle pair → sine law; SAS or SSS → cosine law. When solving a whole triangle from SSS, find the LARGEST angle first with the cosine law — then any sine-law step afterwards cannot hit the ambiguous case.5 秒内选定理：有完整的「边—对角」→ 正弦定理；SAS 或 SSS → 余弦定理。由 SSS 解整个三角形时，先用余弦定理求「最大」的角，之后再用正弦定理就不会碰到两解情形。
