📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • A2−B2=(A−B)(A+B): two squares with a MINUS.A2−B2=(A−B)(A+B)：两个平方数，中间是减号。
  • A2±2AB+B2=(A±B)2: check the middle is exactly 2AB.A2±2AB+B2=(A±B)2：一定要验证中间项正好是 2AB。
  • A2+B2 does not factor over the reals; keep going on x4−16.A2+B2 在实数范围内不能分解；x4−16 要继续分解。

💡 What is 51×49 in your head? It is (50+1)(50−1)=2500−1=2499 — the same pattern that factors 49x2−25y2 in one line.心算 51×49 等于多少？就是 (50+1)(50−1)=2500−1=2499——同一个规律能一行分解 49x2−25y2。

  1. Difference of squares: two perfect squares with a MINUS between them. a2x2−b2y2=(ax−by)(ax+by).平方差：两个完全平方之间是减号。a2x2−b2y2=(ax−by)(ax+by)。
  2. Perfect square trinomial: first and last terms are squares, and the middle is twice their product. 9x2−30x+25=(3x−5)2 because 2(3x)(5)=30x.完全平方式：首末两项是平方，中间项是它们乘积的两倍。9x2−30x+25=(3x−5)2，因为 2(3x)(5)=30x。
  3. A SUM of squares like x2+25 does not factor over the real numbers.平方和（如 x2+25）在实数范围内不能分解。
  4. GCF first, then keep going: x2−16 hiding inside x4−16 factors again.先提公因式，再继续分：x4−16 里藏着的 x2−4 还能再分解。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Count the terms. Is there a common factor? Write each square term as (something)2, and note the sign between them.数一数有几项。有没有公因式？把每个平方项写成 (某个式子)2，再看它们之间是什么符号。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    A product of brackets that cannot be split any further over the integers ("factor fully").写成乘积，而且每个括号在整数范围内都不能再分（「完全分解」）。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    A big square of side A with a small square of side B cut from the corner: the leftover L-shape rearranges into an (A−B) by (A+B) rectangle. A trinomial (A+B)2 is the big square split into A2, B2 and two AB strips.边长 A 的大正方形，从角上剪掉边长 B 的小正方形：剩下的 L 形能拼成 (A−B)×(A+B) 的长方形。三项式 (A+B)2 就是大正方形分成 A2、B2 和两条 AB。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Common factor first. Two squares with a minus: (A−B)(A+B). Three terms with square ends: test whether the middle is exactly 2AB; if yes (A±B)2, if not factor it as an ordinary trinomial.先提公因式。两个平方相减：(A−B)(A+B)。三项且首末是平方：检验中间项是不是正好 2AB；是就写 (A±B)2，不是就按普通三项式分解。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Expand your answer and get the original back. Then look at every bracket once more: is any of them still a difference of squares?把答案展开，要能还原成原式。再把每个括号看一遍：还有没有平方差可以分？

1Multiplying binomials, (a+b)2二项式相乘与 (a+b)2
Expand (2x+5)2.展开 (2x+5)2。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Multiplying binomials, (a+b)2二项式相乘与 (a+b)2 (Grade年级 9–10)
Every term times every term (FOIL): (x+3)(x−5)=x2−5x+3x−15=x2−2x−15. A square has a middle term: (x+3)2=x2+6x+9, never x2+9.每一项乘每一项：(x+3)(x−5)=x2−5x+3x−15=x2−2x−15。平方会有中间项：(x+3)2=x2+6x+9，绝不是 x2+9。
  1. (x−4)2=(x−4)(x−4)=x2−4x−4x+16=x2−8x+16.(x−4)2=(x−4)(x−4)=x2−4x−4x+16=x2−8x+16。
  2. Shortcut: (a±b)2=a2±2ab+b2 — middle term =2× first × last.捷径：(a±b)2=a2±2ab+b2——中间项＝2×首项×末项。
Try one:试一道：
Expand (2x−3)2.展开 (2x−3)2。

2Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号）
Expand (x−6)(x+6).展开 (x−6)(x+6)。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号） (Grade年级 8–9)
Multiply EVERY term inside the bracket: 3(x−4)=3x−12. A minus in front changes every sign inside: −(x−5)=−x+5, and 2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1.括号里的每一项都要乘到：3(x−4)=3x−12。前面是负号时，括号里每一项都变号：−(x−5)=−x+5，2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1。
  1. −2(3x−1)=−6x+2 (the −1 becomes +2).−2(3x−1)=−6x+2（−1 变成 +2）。
  2. x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6.x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6。
Try one:试一道：
Expand and simplify 4x−(2x−7).去括号并化简 4x−(2x−7)。

4Exponent rules (product, quotient, power)指数运算法则（同底乘、除、乘方）
Simplify (3x2)2.化简 (3x2)2。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Exponent rules (product, quotient, power)指数运算法则（同底乘、除、乘方） (Grade年级 9)
Same base: multiply → ADD exponents (x3x4=x7); divide → SUBTRACT (x7÷x2=x5); power of a power → MULTIPLY ((x3)2=x6). A power of a product hits every factor: (2x)3=8x3.同底数：相乘→指数相加（x3x4=x7）；相除→指数相减（x7÷x2=x5）；幂的乘方→指数相乘（(x3)2=x6）。积的乘方每个因数都要乘方：(2x)3=8x3。
  1. (3x2)2⋅x=9x4⋅x=9x5.(3x2)2⋅x=9x4⋅x=9x5。
  2. 2x26x5​=3x3 (divide the numbers, subtract the exponents).2x26x5​=3x3（系数相除，指数相减）。

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

Someone made each of these. Tap the line where it first goes wrong.每道题都有人这样做过。点出第一处出错的那一行。

1Factoring a sum of squares把平方和也分解了
Factor fully: 16x2+25.完全分解：16x2+25。

🧠 The pattern (A−B)(A+B) needs a MINUS between the squares. Expanding (4x−5)(4x+5) gives 16x2−25, not +25.公式 (A−B)(A+B) 要求两个平方之间是减号。展开 (4x−5)(4x+5) 得 16x2−25，不是 +25。

🔧 A sum of two squares has no real factors, and 16 and 25 share no common factor, so it stays as it is.两个平方的和没有实数因式，16 和 25 也没有公因数，所以保持原样。

✔ It does not factor over the real numbers: 16x2+25.在实数范围内不能分解：16x2+25。

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 C, Practice练习 7

2Stopping before it is fully factored没分解完就停了
Factor fully: 16x4−1.完全分解：16x4−1。

🧠 The first round is right, but "factor fully" means checking each bracket again. 4x2−1=(2x)2−12 is another difference of squares.第一轮是对的，但「完全分解」要把每个括号再查一遍。4x2−1=(2x)2−12 还是平方差。

🔧 4x2−1=(2x−1)(2x+1); 4x2+1 is a sum and stays. So (2x−1)(2x+1)(4x2+1).4x2−1=(2x−1)(2x+1)；4x2+1 是平方和，保留。所以是 (2x−1)(2x+1)(4x2+1)。

✔ (2x−1)(2x+1)(4x2+1)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 A, Practice练习 5

3Writing a difference of squares as a perfect square把平方差写成了完全平方
Factor 9x2−49.分解因式 9x2−49。

🧠 The minus sign got copied into both brackets. (3x−7)2=9x2−42x+49 has a middle term and ends in +49; a difference of squares needs one minus bracket and one plus bracket.把减号抄进了两个括号。(3x−7)2=9x2−42x+49 有中间项，末项是 +49；平方差要一个减号括号、一个加号括号。

🔧 9x2−49=(3x−7)(3x+7). Expand to check: 9x2+21x−21x−49 ✓.9x2−49=(3x−7)(3x+7)。展开检验：9x2+21x−21x−49 ✓。

✔ (3x−7)(3x+7)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 A, Practice练习 1

4Calling it a perfect square without checking the middle没验中间项就当成完全平方
Factor x2+13x+36.分解因式 x2+13x+36。

🧠 Square ends are not enough. For (x+6)2 the middle would be 2⋅x⋅6=12x, but here it is 13x.首末是平方还不够。(x+6)2 的中间项应是 2⋅x⋅6=12x，这里却是 13x。

🔧 Treat it as an ordinary trinomial: product 36, sum 13 gives 4 and 9, so (x+4)(x+9).按普通三项式分解：积 36、和 13，得 4 和 9，所以是 (x+4)(x+9)。

✔ (x+4)(x+9)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 D

5Not looking for a common factor first没先找公因式
Factor fully: 12x2−75.完全分解：12x2−75。

🧠 The squares are hiding behind a common factor. Both 12 and 75 are multiples of 3, and after taking it out you get 4x2−25.平方藏在公因式后面。12 和 75 都是 3 的倍数，提出 3 后得到 4x2−25。

🔧 12x2−75=3(4x2−25)=3(2x−5)(2x+5).12x2−75=3(4x2−25)=3(2x−5)(2x+5)。

✔ 3(2x−5)(2x+5)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Practice练习 3

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Factoring a sum: x2+9=(x+3)(x+3) (that gives x2+6x+9). A sum of squares does not factor.把平方和也分解：x2+9=(x+3)(x+3)（那等于 x2+6x+9）。平方和不能分解。
  • Calling x2+10x+16 a perfect square because 16 is a square. The middle must be 2⋅x⋅4=8x; here it is 10x, so it is (x+2)(x+8).因为 16 是平方数就把 x2+10x+16 当完全平方。中间项须为 2⋅x⋅4=8x，这里是 10x，所以应为 (x+2)(x+8)。
  • Stopping too early: x4−81=(x2−9)(x2+9) still has x2−9=(x−3)(x+3) inside.分解得太早停：x4−81=(x2−9)(x2+9) 里的 x2−9=(x−3)(x+3) 还能分。

How can you tell x2+10x+25 is a perfect square, but x2+10x+16 is not?怎样看出 x2+10x+25 是完全平方式，而 x2+10x+16 不是？

For x2+10x+25 the ends are squares (x2, 52) and the middle 10x equals 2⋅x⋅5, so it is (x+5)2. In x2+10x+16 the middle would have to be 2⋅x⋅4=8x; it is 10x, so it factors as (x+2)(x+8) instead.x2+10x+25 首尾是平方（x2、52），中间 10x=2⋅x⋅5，所以是 (x+5)2。x2+10x+16 的中间项应是 2⋅x⋅4=8x，实际是 10x，所以要分成 (x+2)(x+8)。

A square lawn has side s metres. A 2 m wide gravel path is laid around all four sides, outside the lawn, so the lawn and path together form a larger square. The gravel path covers 336 m2. (a) Write the path area as a difference of squares and factor it. (b) Find s. (c) Verify using a mental-math difference of squares.一块正方形草坪边长 s 米。在草坪外侧四周铺一条 2 m 宽的碎石小路，草坪和小路合起来是一个更大的正方形。碎石路面积为 336 m2。(a) 把路面积写成平方差并分解。(b) 求 s。(c) 用平方差心算验证。
The big square has side s+4, not s+2: the path is on both sides.大正方形边长是 s+4，不是 s+2：两侧都有小路。
(a) (s+4)2−s2=4(2s+4)=8s+16; (b) s=40 m; (c) 442−402=4⋅84=336.(a) (s+4)2−s2=4(2s+4)=8s+16；(b) s=40 m；(c) 442−402=4⋅84=336。
  1. Path area =(s+4)2−s2=((s+4)−s)((s+4)+s)=4(2s+4)=8s+16.路面积 =(s+4)2−s2=((s+4)−s)((s+4)+s)=4(2s+4)=8s+16。
  2. 8s+16=336 gives 8s=320, so s=40 m.8s+16=336 得 8s=320，所以 s=40 m。
