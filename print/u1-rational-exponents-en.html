🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Circle three things: the base, the denominator of the exponent (the root) and the numerator (the power). Note any minus sign in the exponent.圈出三样东西：底数、指数的分母（开几次方）、指数的分子（几次幂）。指数上有负号也标出来。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Is it "evaluate" (a number), "simplify" (one power of each variable, positive exponents) or "write as a radical"? The answer form depends on it.题目要的是「求值」（一个数）、「化简」（每个字母只剩一个幂，指数为正），还是「写成根式」？答案形式由它决定。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Write the base as a power of a small number: 64=26=43=82. Seeing that list tells you which root comes out clean.把底数写成小数字的幂：64=26=43=82。看着这串式子，就知道开几次方能开得干净。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Flip first if the exponent is negative, then take the root, then the power. For variables, use the exponent laws and add/subtract the fractions.指数是负的就先翻转（取倒数），再开方，再乘方。字母部分用指数律，对分数指数做加减。
  5. ✅ Check检验 A negative exponent never makes the answer negative. Check backwards: raise your answer to the reciprocal power and see if you get the start again.负指数不会让结果变负。倒推检验：把答案取倒数次幂，看能不能回到原来的数。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • am/n=(na​)m: bottom of the exponent = root, top = power.am/n=(na​)m：指数的分母是开方次数，分子是乘方次数。
  • By hand, take the root first: 272/3=32=9.手算先开方，数字小：272/3=32=9。
  • Negative exponent = reciprocal, never a negative answer: 16−1/2=41​.负指数＝倒数，结果不会变负：16−1/2=41​。

  3. Multiplying the base by the exponent把底数和指数相乘

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 1 — The 2 on top looked like "square root". But the DENOMINATOR is the root: 32​ means cube root, then square.分子上的 2 看着像「开平方」。其实分母才是开方次数：32​ 表示先开立方，再平方。 364​=4, then 42=16.364​=4，再 42=16。
  2. line第 2 — The minus sign in an exponent means "take the reciprocal", not "make it negative". A positive base always gives a positive power.指数上的负号表示「取倒数」，不是「变成负数」。底数为正，幂永远为正。 25−1/2=251/21​=51​.25−1/2=251/21​=51​。
  3. line第 1 — An exponent is not a multiplier. 31​ as an exponent asks "what number cubed gives 27?"指数不是乘数。指数 31​ 问的是「哪个数立方等于 27」。 271/3=327​=3, since 33=27.271/3=327​=3，因为 33=27。
  4. line第 1 — Fractions made the familiar rule feel new. Multiplying powers of the same base still means ADD the exponents; you multiply only for a power of a power.一碰到分数，熟悉的法则就忘了。同底数幂相乘仍然是指数「相加」；只有幂的乘方才是指数相乘。 21​+41​=42​+41​=43​, so x3/4.21​+41​=42​+41​=43​，所以是 x3/4。
