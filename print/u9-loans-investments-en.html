🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 List the numbers in the words: the loan or savings amount, the payment, the yearly rate, how often interest is compounded (C/Y), how often you pay (P/Y), and how many years.把题目里的数列出来：贷款额或储蓄额、每期付款、年利率、每年计息几次（C/Y）、每年付款几次（P/Y）、多少年。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Which one is blank: the payment PMT, the loan size PV, or the savings total FV? Does the question also want the total paid or the interest?空着的是哪一个：每期付款 PMT、贷款额 PV，还是储蓄终值 FV？题目是否还要还款总额或利息？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Draw a timeline: one arrow for each payment at the end of each period. Money today sits at the left (PV); money at the end sits at the right (FV).画一条时间线：每期末画一个付款箭头。今天的钱在左端（PV），最后的钱在右端（FV）。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 i = rate per PAYMENT period, n = number of payments. Saving: FV formula. Loan size: PV formula. Loan payment: PMT formula. Or fill the TVM list, money out negative.i＝每个「付款期」的利率，n＝付款次数。储蓄用 FV 公式；求能借多少用 PV 公式；求月供用 PMT 公式。也可以填 TVM 清单，付出的钱为负。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Payments times n must be MORE than the loan (you pay interest) and LESS than the savings total (interest is added). The difference is the interest.每期付款乘以 n：贷款题要「大于」贷款额（你付了利息），储蓄题要「小于」终值（利息加进来了）。两者的差就是利息。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Saving with equal deposits → FV. Loan → amount borrowed is PV; solve for PMT.定期等额存款 → 求终值 FV。贷款 → 借款额是现值 PV，求每期还款 PMT。
  • i = rate per PAYMENT period, n = number of payments (monthly, 5 years → n=60).i＝每个还款期的利率，n＝还款次数（按月 5 年 → n=60）。
  • Interest = total paid − amount borrowed (or − total deposited).利息＝总付款 − 借款额（储蓄则 − 总存入）。
FV=PMT⋅i(1+i)n−1​
PV=PMT⋅i1−(1+i)−n​

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Using the yearly rate as the monthly rate把年利率当成月利率
  2. Counting years instead of payments数了年数而不是付款次数
  3. Calling the total paid the interest把还款总额当成利息
  4. Using r over 12 for a Canadian mortgage加拿大房贷直接用 r 除以 12
  5. Using the savings formula to find a loan求贷款额却用了储蓄公式

  2. line第 1 — "6 years" is the number in the question, so it becomes n. But n counts deposits, and there is one every month.题目里的数是「6 年」，于是就成了 n。可是 n 数的是存款次数，每月存一次。 n=12×6=72: FV=50⋅0.00251.002572−1​≈$3938.97.
