Factoring by substitution换元法分解

Thinking map — keep it next to you while you work思路图——做题时放在手边

🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Look for ONE chunk that shows up twice: once squared, once to the first power (x4 and x2, or (x+2)2 and (x+2)). Write "Let m=" that chunk.找出出现了两次的「同一块」：一次是平方，一次是一次方（x4 和 x2，或 (x+2)2 和 (x+2)）。写下「设 m=」这一块。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 A full factorization in x, not in m, where no bracket can be factored again.用 x 写的完全分解（不是用 m），每个括号都不能再分解。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Rewrite the whole expression with m, e.g. m2−5m+4. It should now look like an ordinary trinomial you already know how to factor.用 m 把整个式子重写一遍，例如 m2−5m+4。它应该变成一个你已经会分解的普通三项式。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Factor in m (product and sum, or decomposition when a=1). Then put the chunk back in place of m and simplify each bracket.按 m 分解（积与和；a=1 时用拆项法）。再把这一块代回 m 的位置，化简每个括号。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Look at each bracket again: is it a difference of squares or does it have a common factor? Then put x=1 (or 2) into the original and into your answer.再看一遍每个括号：是不是平方差？有没有公因数？然后把 x=1（或 2）分别代入原式和答案。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Spot a(stuff)2+b(stuff)+c; let m= the stuff.认出 a(整体)2+b(整体)+c；令 m= 这个整体。
  • Factor in m, substitute back, simplify each bracket.按 m 分解，代回，再化简每个括号。
  • Re-check every bracket: x2−4 and friends factor again.每个括号再查一遍：x2−4 这类还能继续分解。
a[f(x)]2+bf(x)+c  m=f(x) ​ am2+bm+c

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Leaving the answer in m答案停在 m 上
  2. Stopping before the difference of squares没分解平方差就停了
  3. Putting x back instead of the whole chunk代回时只写了 x，没代回整块
  4. Dropping the bracket when working out A minus B算 A−B 时丢了括号
  5. Distributing only part of the way when substituting back代回时分配律只做了一半

5. Distributing only part of the way when substituting back代回时分配律只做了一半

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 3 — The factoring felt like the finish line. But m was only a helper letter; the question is about x.分解完就觉得到终点了。可 m 只是临时的帮手，题目问的是 x。 Put x2 back: (x2−1)(x2−5)=(x−1)(x+1)(x2−5). (x2−5 does not factor over the integers.)把 x2 代回：(x2−1)(x2−5)=(x−1)(x+1)(x2−5)。（x2−5 在整数范围内不能再分。）
  2. line第 3 — After the substitution the work looks finished. But "fully" means checking every bracket again, and x2−4, x2−16 are both differences of squares.换元做完，看起来就结束了。可是「完全」分解要求每个括号再查一遍，x2−4 和 x2−16 都是平方差。 (x−2)(x+2)(x−4)(x+4).(x−2)(x+2)(x−4)(x+4)。
  3. line第 2 — In the quartic questions m was "x-something" and it is easy to just write x. Here m stands for the whole x−1.做四次式时习惯把 m 当成「和 x 差不多」，顺手就写成了 x。这里的 m 代表整个 x−1。 ((x−1)+5)((x−1)−2)=(x+4)(x−3).((x−1)+5)((x−1)−2)=(x+4)(x−3)。
  4. line第 1 — The minus sign was given to x only. B is the whole (x+5), so the minus hits the 5 too.减号只给了 x。B 是整个 (x+5)，所以减号也要作用到 5。 A−B=(3x−1)−(x+5)=2x−6, A+B=4x+4, so (2x−6)(4x+4)=8(x−3)(x+1).A−B=(3x−1)−(x+5)=2x−6，A+B=4x+4，所以 (2x−6)(4x+4)=8(x−3)(x+1)。
  5. line第 2 — 3m became 3x−2 because the bracket was not written. 3m=3(x−2)=3x−6.没写括号，3m 就成了 3x−2。其实 3m=3(x−2)=3x−6。 (3(x−2)+1)((x−2)+2)=(3x−5)x, so x(3x−5).(3(x−2)+1)((x−2)+2)=(3x−5)x，即 x(3x−5)。
