🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Factor every denominator first and write the NPVs from them (every value that makes any denominator 0).先把每个分母分解，并据此写出不允许值（凡是让任何一个分母为 0 的值）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 ONE fraction, numerator and denominator factored, with nothing left to cancel, plus the NPVs.一个分式：分子、分母都分解好，不能再约分，再加上不允许值。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Line the factored denominators up in a row. Under each one, write the factors it is missing from the LCD.把分解后的分母排成一行，在每个分母下面写出它比公分母少了哪些因式。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Multiply each numerator by its missing factors. Write the whole new numerator on one line with brackets, then expand and collect.每个分子乘上自己缺的因式。先在一行写出带括号的整个新分子，再展开、合并同类项。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Factor the numerator and cancel common FACTORS only. Then put one allowed value (like x=0) into the question and your answer — they must match.把分子分解，只约公「因式」。再取一个允许值（比如 x=0）代入原题和答案——两边必须相等。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Factor every denominator; LCD = each different factor at its highest power.把每个分母分解；最简公分母＝每个不同因式取最高次幂。
  • Rewrite over the LCD, then add or subtract the NUMERATORS only.化成同分母，只对分子加减。
  • Subtracting: bracket the whole numerator, −(x−2)=−x+2; finish by factoring and cancelling.相减时整个分子加括号，−(x−2)=−x+2；最后分解分子看能否约分。

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Adding the denominators too连分母也相加
  2. The minus sign only hits the first term减号只作用到第一项
  3. Changing the bottom but not the top只改了分母，没改分子
  4. Treating opposite denominators as the same把相反的分母当成相同的
  5. Cancelling terms instead of factors约掉了「项」而不是「因式」
