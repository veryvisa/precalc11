🧭 Catch-up path: if you missed about 4 weeks补课路线：落下约 4 周怎么办

A calm, time-boxed way back into class. You do not have to learn everything you missed — only what the class is using now, in the order it needs it. Each topic is one 25-minute block: a 5-question check first, study only what you missed, then check again.一条平静、有时间框的回班路线。你不用补完落下的所有内容，只补班里现在要用的，并按需要的先后顺序来。每个知识点是一个 25 分钟的小段：先做 5 道题的小测，只学错了的部分，再测一次。

How one 25-minute block works一个 25 分钟小段怎么走

  1. Check · 5 min小测 · 5 分钟 — 5 questions. Score 4 or 5 and you already have this topic: tick it and move on.5 道题。答对 4 道或 5 道，说明这个知识点你已经会了：打勾，往下走。
  2. Learn · 15 min学习 · 15 分钟 — Only if you scored 3 or less. Flashcards first, then the slides. Open the full lesson only if you are still stuck.只在答对 3 道或更少时才学。先看闪卡，再看幻灯。还是卡住，再打开完整讲解。
  3. Re-check · 5 min再测 · 5 分钟 — 5 fresh questions. Score 4 or 5 and you are done. Lower? No penalty: repeat the Learn step tomorrow, sleep helps.5 道新题。答对 4 道或 5 道就完成了。低于这个？没关系：明天再学一遍，睡一觉有帮助。

Two minutes with the teacher和老师聊两分钟

  • Which topic is the class on this week, and which are next?班里这周在讲哪个知识点，接下来是哪些？
  • Which unit tests did the class write while I was away? Can I write or rewrite them, and when?我不在时班里考了哪些单元测？我能补考或重考吗，什么时候？
  • Is there work to hand in, and what is the one thing I should do first?有没有要交的作业？我最该先做的一件事是什么？

📅 Three weeks, day by day三周，一天一天来

At most two 25-minute blocks a day, one weekly check-in, and a rest day every week. If a day goes badly, do one block or none: the plan slides, nothing is lost. Keep doing your regular class work as usual.每天最多两个 25 分钟的小段，每周一次小结测，每周一个休息日。哪天状态不好，做一段或不做都行：计划顺延，什么都不会丢。班里的日常作业照常做。
