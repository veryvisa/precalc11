🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Label the triangle: side a across from angle A, and so on. Find a complete pair (a side AND its opposite angle). If two angles are known, the third is 180∘ minus them.先标三角形：边 a 在角 A 的对面，依此类推。找一组完整的「边—对角」。若已知两个角，第三个角是 180∘ 减去它们。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Which side or angle, and how to round. A side (cm, m, km) or an angle (degrees)?求哪条边或哪个角？保留几位？求的是边（cm、m、km）还是角（度）？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Sketch it roughly to scale. The biggest angle faces the longest side: use that to predict your answer before you calculate.大致按比例画草图。最大的角对着最长的边：计算之前先用它预估答案。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Unknown on top: sinBb​=sinAa​ for a side, bsinB​=asinA​ for an angle. Calculator in degrees; keep full values until the end.未知量放分子：求边用 sinBb​=sinAa​，求角用 bsinB​=asinA​。计算器用角度制，中间值全部保留到最后。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Do the sides come in the same order as their opposite angles? Do the angles add to 180∘? Any negative length means radian mode.边的大小顺序和对角的顺序一致吗？三角之和是 180∘ 吗？出现负的边长，说明计算器在弧度制。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​; side a is opposite angle A.sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​；边 a 对角 A。
  • Needs a complete pair (side + opposite angle): AAS, ASA, SSA. SAS or SSS → cosine law.需要一组完整的「边＋对角」：AAS、ASA、SSA 可用。SAS、SSS → 用余弦定理。
  • Unknown on top: sides on top for a side, sines on top for an angle.未知量放分子：求边时边在上，求角时正弦在上。

  2. line第 2 — To get a alone, multiply both sides by sin30∘: the angle under a moves to the top. The answer 35.5 is also too big: 30∘ is the smallest angle.要把 a 单独留下，两边乘 sin30∘：a 下面的那个角要移到分子。而且 35.5 太大了：30∘ 是最小的角。 a=sin100∘18sin30∘​≈9.1.a=sin100∘18sin30∘​≈9.1。
