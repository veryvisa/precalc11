🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Read it twice and underline every number with what it describes. Let the variable be the thing asked for, then write every other quantity using it (width w, length w+5).读两遍，把每个数字和它描述的东西划出来。设所求的量为未知数，再用它表示其他量（宽 w，长 w+5）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Which quantity, in which unit? One value, a pair of numbers, or every time something happens (a ball can pass a height twice)?求哪个量、什么单位？要一个值、一对数，还是「所有时刻」（球可能两次经过同一高度）？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Sketch it: a rectangle with the border labelled x on BOTH sides, a right triangle with its three sides labelled, or a height-vs-time arc with a line at the target height.画个图：矩形四周的边框「两侧」都标上 x；直角三角形三边都标好；或者画高度—时间的弧线，再画一条目标高度的水平线。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Write ONE equation from ONE fact: area = length × width, a2+b2=c2, or h(t)= the height. Move everything to one side; if D is a perfect square, factor, otherwise use the formula.用「一个事实」列「一个方程」：面积 = 长 × 宽、a2+b2=c2，或 h(t)= 某高度。全部移到一边；D 是完全平方数就分解，否则用公式。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Test each root against the WORDS: negative lengths and times go, with a reason. Put the kept answer back into the story, then finish with a sentence and units.每个根都拿回「题目原文」检验：负的长度、时间要写理由舍去。把留下的答案代回情境验算，最后写一句带单位的话。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Variable = what is asked; equation from area, Pythagoras or "height = value".设所求为未知数；由面积、勾股定理或「高度 = 某值」列方程。
  • A frame or path of width x adds 2x to each dimension.宽为 x 的边框或小路，使每个方向的长度都增加 2x。
  • Solve fully, then reject roots that make no sense (say why); keep both if both fit.先完整解出，再舍去不合理的根（写出理由）；两个都合理就都保留。

  2. line第 2 — "One factor must be 0" only works when the product IS 0. A product of 143 tells you nothing about each factor on its own.「必有一个因式为 0」只在乘积等于 0 时成立。乘积是 143，并不能单独确定每个因式。 Expand and move: n2+2n−143=0, (n+13)(n−11)=0, n=11 or n=−13. But both times are positive: the ball passes 26 m going up and again coming down.「应用题只有一个答案」的习惯在作怪。两个时间都为正：球上升时经过 26 m，下落时又经过一次。 Keep every root that fits the story: t=1 s and t=5 s (the axis t=3 sits halfway between them ✓).保留所有符合情境的根：t=1 秒和 t=5 秒（对称轴 t=3 正好在中间 ✓）。
