🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Read the index (2 or 3) and the radicand. With letters, note which exponents are odd and whether you are told x≥0.读出根指数（2 还是 3）和被开方数。有字母时，看哪些指数是奇数，题目有没有说 x≥0。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Simplest form: no perfect-square factor left inside (perfect cube for 3 ​), plus a restriction if an even root holds a letter. For ordering: a list from least to greatest.最简形式：根号里不再有完全平方因数（立方根就是完全立方因数）；偶次根号里有字母时还要写限制条件。排大小时：从小到大的一串。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A factor tree of the radicand. Circle each PAIR of equal primes (each triple for a cube root); every circled group comes out as one number.给被开方数画因数树。把相同质数「两个一圈」（立方根就三个一圈）；每圈出一组，就有一个数出到根号外。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Try the perfect squares from big to small (100, 81, 64, 49, 36, 25, 16, 9, 4) until one divides the radicand; split and take its root out. To compare, square the outside number and move it in.从大到小试完全平方数（100、81、64、49、36、25、16、9、4），直到有一个能整除被开方数；拆开，把它的平方根提出去。比较大小时，把外面的数平方后乘进去。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Multiply back: (outside)2× inside must give the original radicand. Then ask: can the leftover still be divided by 4, 9 or 25?乘回去：（外面的数）2× 里面的数必须等于原来的被开方数。再问：剩下的还能被 4、9 或 25 整除吗？

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Pull out the LARGEST perfect square (perfect cube for 3 ​): 72​=62​.提出最大的完全平方因数（立方根提完全立方数）：72​=62​。
  • Mixed → entire: square the outside number and multiply it in: 35​=45​.最简根式化为整根式：把外面的数平方后乘进去：35​=45​。
  • To compare radicals, rewrite all as entire radicals and compare the insides.比较根式大小：都化成整根式，比较根号里面的数。

  1. Stopping before the largest square is out没提出最大的平方因数就停了
  2. Not squaring the outside number when moving it in把外面的数移进根号时没有平方
  3. Comparing only the outside numbers比较大小只看外面的数
  4. Taking a whole odd power out of the root把奇次幂整个提出根号
  5. Splitting a root over a plus sign把根号对加法拆开
