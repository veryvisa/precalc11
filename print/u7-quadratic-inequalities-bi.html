🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Is one side 0? If not, move everything over first. Note the sign (<,≤,>,≥) and whether the x2 coefficient is positive or negative.有一边是 0 吗？不是就先全部移过去。记下不等号（<,≤,>,≥），以及 x2 的系数是正还是负。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Every x that makes it true. Usually one interval or two pieces joined by "or"; sometimes all real numbers, one point, or nothing.使它成立的所有 x。通常是一个区间，或用「或」连接的两段；有时是全体实数、一个点，或无解。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A tiny parabola through the zeros (up if a>0, down if a<0), or a number line with the zeros and +/− in each region.过零点画个小抛物线（a>0 开口向上，a<0 向下），或者画一条数轴，标出零点和每一段的 +/−。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Factor (or use the formula) to get the zeros. Opens up: <0 BETWEEN the zeros, >0 OUTSIDE. Opens down: the other way round.分解（或用公式）求零点。开口向上：<0 在零点「之间」，>0 在零点「两侧」。开口向下：反过来。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Test one number inside your answer and one outside in the ORIGINAL inequality. Square brackets only for ≤ or ≥.在「原」不等式里代一个答案内的数和一个答案外的数。只有 ≤、≥ 才用方括号。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Everything to one side, find the zeros, then decide the sign between and outside them.移到一边，求零点，再判断零点之间和两侧的符号。
  • Opens up: negative BETWEEN the zeros, positive OUTSIDE.开口向上：零点之间为负，两侧为正。
  • ≤,≥ include the zeros; outside answers are joined by "or" (∪).≤,≥ 包括零点；两侧的答案用「或」(∪) 连接。
a(x−r)(x−s)<0, a>0, r<s ⇒ r<x<s
a(x−r)(x−s)>0, a>0 ⇒ x<r or x>s

🩹 If you missed it如果做错了 — Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）: Take out a common factor first. Then find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b: x2−x−12 → −4 and 3 → (x−4)(x+3). Check by expanding.先提公因式。再找两个数，相乘等于 c、相加等于 b：x2−x−12 → −4 和 3 → (x−4)(x+3)。展开检验。

🩹 If you missed it如果做错了 — Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘: Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。

  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 3 — The answer "between the zeros" feels natural because the zeros are what you just found. But an upward parabola is ABOVE the axis outside its zeros.刚求出零点，「取中间」感觉最自然。但开口向上的抛物线在零点两侧才位于 x 轴上方。 Sketch or test x=0: −21>0 ✗, so the middle is wrong. Answer: x<−3 or x>7.画草图或代 x=0：−21>0 ✗，所以中间不对。答案：x<−3 或 x>7。
  2. line第 1 — It copies the equation habit x2=25⇒x=±5. With ≥, the −5 side runs the other way: x=−10 works but is not ≥−5.照搬了方程的习惯 x2=25⇒x=±5。换成 ≥ 时，−5 这一边方向相反：x=−10 满足，却不 ≥−5。 x2−25≥0, (x−5)(x+5)≥0, opens up, outside: x≤−5 or x≥5.x2−25≥0，(x−5)(x+5)≥0，开口向上，取两侧：x≤−5 或 x≥5。
  3. line第 2 — The sign rules for a product only work against 0. Knowing x(x+2) is below 24 says nothing about each factor.乘积的符号规律只在和 0 比较时才成立。知道 x(x+2)<24，并不能推出每个因式的情况。 x2+2x−24<0, (x+6)(x−4)<0, opens up, between: −6<x<4.x2+2x−24<0，(x+6)(x−4)<0，开口向上，取中间：−6<x<4。
  4. line第 1 — Making a positive is a good move, but it changes the direction of every inequality, the same rule as −2x>6⇒x<−3.把 a 变正是好办法，但乘负数会让不等号反向，和 −2x>6⇒x<−3 是同一条规则。 x2−x−12<0, so between the zeros: −3<x<4. (Or keep the original: it opens down and is positive between its zeros.)x2−x−12<0，取零点之间：−3<x<4。（或者不乘：原式开口向下，在零点之间为正。）
  5. line第 3 — The zeros are the points where the expression equals 0, and ≥ asks for "0 or more", so they belong in the answer.零点处式子正好等于 0，而 ≥ 要的是「大于或等于 0」，所以零点要包含。 Square brackets at both zeros: (−∞,−2]∪[4,∞). Check x=4: 16−8−8=0≥0 ✓.两个零点都用方括号：(−∞,−2]∪[4,∞)。验算 x=4：16−8−8=0≥0 ✓。
