🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Write down a, b and c with their signs. The sign of a already tells you: up (minimum) or down (maximum).先写下带符号的 a、b、c。a 的符号已经告诉你：开口向上（有最小值）还是向下（有最大值）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Which of the six facts are wanted? Remember that a max or min VALUE is a y-value, and the range is about y.题目要六项特征中的哪几项？记住：最大值或最小值是一个 y 值，值域讲的也是 y。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A quick U or ∩ with a dashed vertical line for the axis. The vertex sits on that line, and the two x-intercepts are mirror images across it.随手画个 U 或 ∩，再画一条竖直虚线当对称轴。顶点在这条线上，两个 x 截距关于它对称。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Axis first: x=−2ab​ (or the midpoint of the zeros). Put that x back into f to get the vertex height.先求对称轴：x=−2ab​（或两个零点的中点）。把这个 x 代回 f，得到顶点的高度。
  5. ✅ Check检验 The zeros must be equally far from the axis. The range must use the vertex y-value, with ≥ for up and ≤ for down.两个零点到对称轴的距离必须相等。值域要用顶点的纵坐标：开口向上用 ≥，向下用 ≤。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • a>0 opens up (minimum), a<0 opens down (maximum); the vertex sits on the axis x=−2ab​.a>0 开口向上（有最小值），a<0 开口向下（有最大值）；顶点在对称轴 x=−2ab​ 上。
  • Range uses the vertex y-value: y≥q (up) or y≤q (down). Domain is always all real numbers.值域看顶点的纵坐标：开口向上 y≥q，向下 y≤q。定义域永远是全体实数。
  • Axis = midpoint of the zeros, or of any two points at the same height.对称轴 = 两个零点的中点，或任意两个等高点的中点。

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 1 — The formula is x=−2ab​. With b positive the axis is on the negative side.公式是 x=−2ab​。b 为正时，对称轴在负的一侧。 x=−28​=−4, then y=16−32+7=−9.x=−28​=−4，再算 y=16−32+7=−9。
  2. line第 3 — The range lists y-values, so it must use the height of the vertex, not where it sits left or right.值域列的是 y 值，要用顶点的高度，而不是它在左右哪个位置。 The lowest y is −4, so the range is y≥−4.最小的 y 是 −4，所以值域是 y≥−4。
  3. line第 3 — Habit: most parabolas you have drawn open up. Here a=−3<0, so the vertex is the TOP and every other y is below it.习惯作怪：画过的抛物线大多开口向上。这里 a=−3<0，顶点是「最高点」，其他 y 都比它低。 Opens down, so the range is y≤4.开口向下，值域是 y≤4。
  4. line第 2 — The two minus signs looked like they cancel. But the square acts first: (−2)2=4, and then the minus in front makes it −4.两个负号看起来像能抵消。但平方先算：(−2)2=4，前面的负号再把它变成 −4。 −4−8+3=−9.−4−8+3=−9。
  5. line第 2 — The top is always on the axis. The point (2,6) only helps find a; it is not on the axis x=4.最高点一定在对称轴上。点 (2,6) 只用来求 a，它不在对称轴 x=4 上。 Axis x=20+8​=4; y=−21​(4)(−4)=8.对称轴 x=20+8​=4；y=−21​(4)(−4)=8。
