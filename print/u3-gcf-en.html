Greatest common factor & grouping提公因式与分组分解

Thinking map — keep it next to you while you work思路图——做题时放在手边

🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Count the terms. For each term, note the number and the power of each letter. Is the same number, letter or bracket in EVERY term?数一数有几项。每一项记下数字和每个字母的次数。有没有哪个数、字母或括号在「每一项」里都出现？
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 The expression written as a product, factored FULLY: nothing common is left and no bracket can be factored again.把式子写成乘积，而且要「完全」分解：没有公因式剩下，也没有哪个括号还能再分解。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A box: the GCF goes outside, and each term divided by the GCF goes inside, one per cell. For four terms, draw a line between the first two and the last two.画一个框：公因式写在框外，每一项除以公因式的结果写在框里，一项一格。四项式就在前两项和后两项之间画一条竖线。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 GCF = the biggest number that divides every coefficient × each letter to its LOWEST power; divide every term by it. Four terms: factor each pair so that the two brackets match (take out a negative if the third term is negative), then take the bracket out.公因式＝能整除所有系数的最大数 × 每个字母的「最低」次幂；每一项都除以它。四项式：两组各提公因式，让两个括号相同（第三项是负的就提负号），再把括号提出来。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Expand your answer: it must give back every original term, signs included. Then look at each bracket: is it a difference of squares?把答案展开：必须得回原来的每一项，符号也要一样。再看每个括号：是不是平方差？

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • GCF first: largest number dividing every coefficient × each variable to its LOWEST power.先提公因式：能整除所有系数的最大数 × 每个字母的最低次幂。
  • A bracket can be the common factor: 2x(x−3)+5(x−3)=(x−3)(2x+5).整个括号也能当公因式：2x(x−3)+5(x−3)=(x−3)(2x+5)。
  • Four terms → group in pairs; opposite brackets: (2−x)=−(x−2).四项式 → 两两分组；括号互为相反数时用 (2−x)=−(x−2)。
ab+ac=a(b+c)
ax+ay+bx+by=a(x+y)+b(x+y)=(x+y)(a+b)

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Taking out a factor that is not the greatest提出的不是最大公因式
  2. Losing the 1 left by a term equal to the GCF和公因式相同的项丢了 1
  3. A sign slip in the second pair第二组符号出错
  4. Stopping before a difference of squares平方差没分解就停了
  5. Treating opposite brackets as the same把相反的括号当成相同

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 1 — Both terms have at least x2, so x2 (the LOWEST power) belongs in the GCF. The bracket 2x2−3x still has a common x, which shows you stopped too early.两项都至少含 x2，所以公因式里要放 x2（取「最低」次幂）。括号 2x2−3x 里还有公因式 x，说明停早了。 GCF =6x2: 12x3−18x2=6x2(2x−3).公因式 =6x2：12x3−18x2=6x2(2x−3)。
  2. line第 2 — 4xy÷4xy=1, not 0. Dropping it leaves one term where there were two; expanding 4xy(2x) gives only 8x2y.4xy÷4xy=1，不是 0。丢掉它就从两项变成了一项；展开 4xy(2x) 只得到 8x2y。 8x2y+4xy=4xy(2x+1).8x2y+4xy=4xy(2x+1)。
  3. line第 2 — −5×(−4)=+20, not −20. Dividing −20 by −5 gives +4, so the bracket is (x+4).−5×(−4)=+20，不是 −20。−20÷(−5)=+4，括号应是 (x+4)。 x2(x+4)−5(x+4)=(x+4)(x2−5). (x2−5 does not factor with whole numbers.)x2(x+4)−5(x+4)=(x+4)(x2−5)。（x2−5 不能用整数再分解。）
  4. line第 3 — The grouping is right, but "fully" means checking every bracket again. x2−16 is a difference of squares.分组没错，但「完全分解」要把每个括号再检查一遍。x2−16 是平方差。 x2−16=(x−4)(x+4), so the answer is (x−2)(x−4)(x+4).x2−16=(x−4)(x+4)，所以答案是 (x−2)(x−4)(x+4)。
  5. line第 2 — (3−x) is not (x−3); it is −(x−3). So +2(3−x)=−2(x−3), and the sign of the 2 changes.(3−x) 不是 (x−3)，而是 −(x−3)。所以 +2(3−x)=−2(x−3)，2 前面的符号要变。 5x(x−3)−2(x−3)=(x−3)(5x−2). Check with x=0: original =6, answer =(−3)(−2)=6 ✓.5x(x−3)−2(x−3)=(x−3)(5x−2)。用 x=0 检验：原式 =6，答案 =(−3)(−2)=6 ✓。
