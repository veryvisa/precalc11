Buy, lease or rent?买、租赁还是租用？

Thinking map — keep it next to you while you work思路图——做题时放在手边

🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 For each option, list every cash flow over the SAME number of years: down payment, each payment times the number of months, end fees, and what you own at the end (its resale value).对每种方案，列出「相同年数」内的每一笔钱：首付、每期付款乘以月数、到期费用，以及期末拥有的东西（转卖价值）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Which option has the lower NET cost, and by how much? Then give a one-sentence recommendation with a reason.哪种方案的「净成本」更低？低多少？最后用一句话给出建议并说明理由。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A two-column table, Buy | Lease (or Rent | Own). One line per cost; the buy column ends with "− resale value".一张两栏表：买｜租（或租房｜买房）。每项成本一行；「买」那一栏最后一行是「− 转卖价值」。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Resale value V=P(1−r)t; appreciation V=P(1+r)t. A loan payment comes from the payment formula or TVM. For a home, count only interest, tax, strata, maintenance and lost return; a Canadian fixed-rate mortgage is quoted compounded semi-annually (C/Y =2, P/Y =12).转卖价值 V=P(1−r)t；升值 V=P(1+r)t。贷款月供用月供公式或 TVM。房子只算利息、地税、物业费、维修和机会成本；加拿大固定利率房贷按半年复利标示（C/Y =2，P/Y =12）。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Same time span on both sides? Resale less than the price? A lower monthly payment does NOT mean a lower total.两边年数一样吗？转卖价低于原价吗？月供低「不等于」总成本低。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Buy: net cost = total paid − value at the end. Lease: down + payments + end fees; you own nothing.买：净成本＝总支出 − 期末残值。租：首付＋月供＋到期费用；到期什么都不归你。
  • Depreciation compounds: V=P(1−r)t; appreciation: V=P(1+r)t.贬值是复利式的：V=P(1−r)t；升值：V=P(1+r)t。
  • Rent vs buy: compare unrecoverable costs; mortgage principal is equity, not a cost.租房还是买房：比较收不回的成本；房贷本金是自己的资产，不算成本。
V=P(1−r)t
V=P(1+r)t

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Comparing only the monthly payments只比较月供
  2. Forgetting to subtract the resale value忘了减去转卖价值
  3. Taking the same amount off every year每年都减同样的钱
  4. Multiplying by the rate instead of what is kept乘的是贬值率而不是保留率
  5. Counting the whole mortgage payment as a cost把整笔房贷还款都算成本

🔍 Common-errors sheet常见错误单

1. Comparing only the monthly payments只比较月供
A $26000 car. Lease: $2000 down, $329 a month for 48 months, $500 in fees. Buy: no down payment and a loan of $580 a month for 48 months, then sell the car (it loses 15% a year). Which costs less over 4 years?一辆 $26000 的车。租：首付 $2000，每月 $329 共 48 个月，费用 $500。买：不付首付，贷款每月 $580 共 48 个月，之后卖掉（每年贬值 15%）。4 年里哪个花得少？
1 Lease: $329 a month. Loan: $580 a month.租：每月 $329。贷款：每月 $580。
2 The lease is $251 a month less, so leasing costs less.租每月少 $251，所以租更省钱。

5. Counting the whole mortgage payment as a cost把整笔房贷还款都算成本
Rent is $2000 a month. Owning a similar home this year: mortgage payments $24000 (of which $15000 is interest), property tax $2000, strata $3600, maintenance $1200, lost return on the down payment $2500. Which costs less in money you never get back?月租 $2000。买一套类似的房子，今年要付：房贷还款 $24000（其中利息 $15000）、地税 $2000、物业费 $3600、维修 $1200、首付的机会成本 $2500。按拿不回来的钱算，哪个更省？
1 Own: 24000+2000+3600+1200+2500=$33300买：24000+2000+3600+1200+2500=$33300
2 Rent: 2000×12=$24000租：2000×12=$24000
3 Renting is $9300 cheaper.租房便宜 $9300。

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 2 — A lease payment is low because you never own the car. Buying hands you a car worth thousands at the end, and the monthly numbers ignore that.租的月付低，是因为车永远不归你。买车到最后手里有一辆值好几千元的车，只看月供就把这一点漏掉了。 Lease: 2000+329×48+500=$18292. Buy: 580×48=27840, minus resale 26000(0.85)4≈13572.16, gives $14267.84. Buying is about $4024.16 cheaper.租：2000+329×48+500=$18292。买：580×48=27840，减去转卖价值 26000(0.85)4≈13572.16，得 $14267.84。买约便宜 $4024.16。
  2. line第 1 — After 3 years the buyer still owns a car and sells it. That money comes back, so it must be taken off the buy side.3 年后买车的人手里还有车，可以卖掉。这笔钱会回来，要从「买」那一栏减掉。 Resale 28000(0.85)3≈$17195.50, so the net cost of buying is $10804.50. Buying is cheaper by $5019.50.转卖价值 28000(0.85)3≈$17195.50，所以买的净成本是 $10804.50。买便宜 $5019.50。
  3. line第 1 — The 20% is taken from the value AT THAT TIME. After year 1 the car is worth less, so 20% of it is less than $4800.20% 是按「当时」的价值算的。第一年后车已经不值那么多了，它的 20% 就不到 $4800。 It keeps 80% each year: V=24000(0.8)3=$12288.每年保留 80%：V=24000(0.8)3=$12288。
  4. line第 1 — Losing 10% means keeping 90%. Multiplying by 0.1 keeps only 10% each year, which is why the answer is absurdly small.贬值 10% 就是保留 90%。乘 0.1 等于每年只留下 10%，所以答案小得离谱。 V=15000(0.9)2=$12150.V=15000(0.9)2=$12150。
  5. line第 1 — Only the $15000 of interest is gone. The other $9000 pays down the loan, so it stays yours as equity in the home.真正花掉的只有 $15000 利息。另外 $9000 用来还本金，变成你在房子里的净值，仍然是你的。 Own: 15000+2000+3600+1200+2500=$24300. Renting is cheaper by only $300, so a small rise in the home's value would tip it the other way.买：15000+2000+3600+1200+2500=$24300。租房只便宜 $300，房价稍微涨一点就会反过来。
