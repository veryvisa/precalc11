🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Get 0 on one side first. Then write a=, b=, c= on their own line, signs included.先让一边为 0，再单独写一行 a=、b=、c=，连同符号。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Exact answers (simplified radicals) or decimals? How many decimal places?要精确解（化简后的根式）还是小数？保留几位？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 The answers are the x-intercepts of y=ax2+bx+c. D=b2−4ac says how many there are: two, one or none.答案就是 y=ax2+bx+c 的 x 截距。D=b2−4ac 告诉你有几个：两个、一个或没有。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Work out D=b2−4ac first, with b in brackets. Then x=2a−b±D​​.先算 D=b2−4ac，b 要加括号。再用 x=2a−b±D​​。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Simplify the radical, then divide EVERY term on top by the bottom. The two answers should add to −ab​.先化简根式，再把分子的每一项都除以分母。两个答案之和应等于 −ab​。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • x=2a−b±b2−4ac​​, only after the equation is ax2+bx+c=0.x=2a−b±b2−4ac​​，先化成 ax2+bx+c=0 再用。
  • Read a,b,c with their signs; b2 is never negative.a,b,c 连符号一起读；b2 永远不是负数。
  • Simplify the radical, then divide EVERY term on top by 2a.先化简根式，再把分子每一项都除以 2a。
x=2a−b±b2−4ac​​
