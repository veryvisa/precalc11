Non-permissible values & simplifying不允许值与化简

Formulas公式
BCAC​=BA​,B=0, C=0 — Cancelling a common factor约去公因式
b−aa−b​=−1,a=b — Opposite factors相反因式
Remember记住
  • NPVs: values that make the DENOMINATOR zero — factor it, set each factor =0.不允许值：使分母为零的值——把分母分解，每个因式 =0。
  • State NPVs from the ORIGINAL expression; a cancelled factor still counts.从原式写不允许值；约掉的因式照样算。
Watch out当心
  • x+3x+6​=36​ ✗ → nothing cancels across +.x+3x+6​=36​ ✗ → 加号连接的不能约。
  • Setting the numerator =0 for NPVs ✗ → only the denominator matters.令分子为 0 求不允许值 ✗ → 只看分母。

Multiplying & dividing有理式乘除

Formulas公式
BA​⋅DC​=BDAC​ — Multiplying (B,D=0)分式相乘（B,D=0）
BA​÷DC​=BA​⋅CD​ — Dividing (B,C,D=0)分式相除（B,C,D=0）
Remember记住
  • Multiply: factor everything, cancel any top with any bottom, then multiply what is left.乘法：全部分解，任一分子可与任一分母约分，再把剩下的相乘。
  • Divide: multiply by the reciprocal of the SECOND fraction.除法：乘以第二个分式的倒数。
Watch out当心
  • In ÷x+1x−5​, forgetting x=5 ✗ — it lands on the bottom after flipping.÷x+1x−5​ 漏写 x=5 ✗——翻转后它到了分母。
  • Cancelling across +: in xx+3​ nothing cancels.跨加号约分：xx+3​ 什么都不能约。

Adding & subtracting有理式加减

Formulas公式
BA​±DC​=BDAD±BC​ — Adding/subtracting (use the LCD when the denominators share factors)分式加减（分母有公因式时用最简公分母）
Remember记住
  • Factor every denominator; LCD = each different factor at its highest power.把每个分母分解；最简公分母＝每个不同因式取最高次幂。
  • Rewrite over the LCD, then add or subtract the NUMERATORS only.化成同分母，只对分子加减。
Watch out当心
  • x1​+y1​=x+y2​ ✗ → xyx+y​.x1​+y1​=x+y2​ ✗ → xyx+y​。
  • x−(x−2)=−2 ✗ → +2: the minus hits every term.x−(x−2)=−2 ✗ → +2：减号作用于每一项。

Rational equations & applications分式方程与应用题

Formulas公式
t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​ — Work together (time alone t1​,t2​; together T)合作工程（单独用时 t1​,t2​；合作用时 T）
t=vd​ — Time = distance ÷ speed时间＝路程÷速度
Remember记住
  • NPVs first; then multiply EVERY term (whole numbers too) by the LCD.先写不允许值；再把每一项（包括整数项）都乘以公分母。
  • An answer equal to an NPV is extraneous — reject it; all rejected → no solution.等于不允许值的解是增根，舍去；全部舍去 → 无解。
Watch out当心
  • 1+x2−412​: the 1 stays 1 ✗ → it becomes x2−4.1+x2−412​ 里的 1 不乘 ✗ → 它要变成 x2−4。
  • Together time =t1​+t2​ ✗ → add the RATES; together is faster than either alone.合作时间 =t1​+t2​ ✗ → 应该把效率相加；合作比任何一个人单干都快。
