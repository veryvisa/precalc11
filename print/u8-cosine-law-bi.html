🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Mark what you know on a sketch and write each side next to the angle across from it (a faces A). Two sides and the angle BETWEEN them is SAS; three sides is SSS.在草图上标出已知量，把每条边写在它对面的角旁边（a 对着 A）。两边及其「夹角」是 SAS；三条边是 SSS。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 A side (SAS: the side across from the known angle) or an angle (SSS)? Give sides to the nearest tenth and angles in degrees.求边（SAS：已知角对面的那条边）还是求角（SSS）？边精确到 0.1，角用度。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A rough triangle drawn roughly to scale. The biggest angle faces the longest side, and an obtuse angle makes its opposite side longer than Pythagoras would give.大致按比例画个三角形。最大的角对最长的边；钝角对面的边比用勾股定理算出的还长。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 SAS: c2=a2+b2−2abcosC; work out 2abcosC first, subtract, then square-root. SSS: cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​, then cos−1.SAS：c2=a2+b2−2abcosC；先算 2abcosC，再相减，最后开方。SSS：cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​，再用 cos−1。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Calculator in DEGREE mode. Does the biggest angle face the longest side? Is the answer a length, not c2?计算器是「角度制」吗？最大的角对着最长的边吗？答案是边长，而不是 c2 吗？

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • c2=a2+b2−2abcosC (c opposite C); C=90∘ gives Pythagoras.c2=a2+b2−2abcosC（c 对角 C）；C=90∘ 时就是勾股定理。
  • SAS → third side; SSS → angle from cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​.SAS → 求第三边；SSS → 由 cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​ 求角。
  • cosC<0 → C is obtuse; cos−1 handles it, no ambiguous case.cosC<0 → C 为钝角；cos−1 直接给出，没有两解问题。

  1. line第 2 — 90cos40∘ is one product. Multiplication comes before subtraction, so it must be worked out before it is taken away from 106.90cos40∘ 是一个整体的乘积。先乘后减，要先把它算出来，再从 106 里减掉。 90cos40∘≈68.94, so c2≈106−68.94=37.06 and c≈6.1.90cos40∘≈68.94，所以 c2≈106−68.94=37.06，c≈6.1。
  2. line第 3 — The formula gives c2, not c. A side of 67 cannot sit across a 70∘ angle when the other two sides are only 6 and 8.公式算出的是 c2，不是 c。另外两边只有 6 和 8，70∘ 角对面不可能是 67。 Take the square root: c=67.17​≈8.2.开平方：c=67.17​≈8.2。
  3. line第 2 — cos120∘=−0.5, not 0.5. Then −108(−0.5)=+54: for an obtuse angle the last term ADDS.cos120∘=−0.5，不是 0.5。于是 −108(−0.5)=+54：钝角时最后一项是「加」。 c2=117+54=171, so c≈13.1.   4. line第 1 — That form finds the angle across from c, which is C.
