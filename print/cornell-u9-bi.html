Compound interest复利

Formulas公式
A=P(1+nr​)nt — Compound interest (future value of a lump sum)复利公式（一次性存入的终值）
P=(1+nr​)ntA​ — Present value: how much to invest now现值：现在需要投入多少
I=A−P — Interest earned利息＝终值－本金
Remember记住
  • A=P(1+nr​)nt: r as a decimal, n = periods per year, exponent nt.A=P(1+nr​)nt：r 用小数，n 是每年计息次数，指数是 nt。
  • Per period i=nr​; periods =nt (monthly for 6 years = 72).每期利率 i=nr​；期数 =nt（按月 6 年＝72 期）。
Watch out当心
  • r=4 ✗ → r=0.04.r=4 ✗ → r=0.04。
  • Exponent t ✗ → nt: quarterly for 5 years is 20 periods.指数用 t ✗ → 用 nt：按季 5 年是 20 期。

Loans & investments with regular payments定期付款的贷款与投资

Formulas公式
FV=PMT⋅i(1+i)n−1​ — Future value of regular deposits定期存款的终值
PV=PMT⋅i1−(1+i)−n​ — Present value (loan amount) of regular payments定期付款的现值（贷款额）
PMT=1−(1+i)−nPV⋅i​ — Loan payment贷款每期还款额
Remember记住
  • Saving with equal deposits → FV. Loan → amount borrowed is PV; solve for PMT.定期等额存款 → 求终值 FV。贷款 → 借款额是现值 PV，求每期还款 PMT。
  • i = rate per PAYMENT period, n = number of payments (monthly, 5 years → n=60).i＝每个还款期的利率，n＝还款次数（按月 5 年 → n=60）。
Watch out当心
  • i=0.069 for monthly payments ✗ → i=0.069/12, n in months.按月还款用 i=0.069 ✗ → i=0.069/12，n 按月计。
  • Total of payments = interest ✗ → subtract the amount borrowed.把还款总额当利息 ✗ → 要减去借款额。

Buy, lease or rent?买、租赁还是租用？

Formulas公式
V=P(1−r)t — Depreciation (value after t years)贬值（t 年后的价值）
V=P(1+r)t — Appreciation升值
net cost of buying=down+all payments−resale value — Net cost of buying买的净成本
Remember记住
  • Buy: net cost = total paid − value at the end. Lease: down + payments + end fees; you own nothing.买：净成本＝总支出 − 期末残值。租：首付＋月供＋到期费用；到期什么都不归你。
  • Depreciation compounds: V=P(1−r)t; appreciation: V=P(1+r)t.贬值是复利式的：V=P(1−r)t；升值：V=P(1+r)t。
Watch out当心
  • Taking 15% of the ORIGINAL price every year ✗ → 15% of that year’s value.每年都减原价的 15% ✗ → 每年减当年价值的 15%。
  • Comparing monthly payments only ✗ → compare total cost minus what you own at the end.只比月供 ✗ → 比总成本减去期末拥有的价值。
