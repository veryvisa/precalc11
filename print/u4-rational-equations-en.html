🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Factor every denominator and write the NPVs on line 1. In a word problem, define the variable in words and add the context limits (time and speed >0, people a whole number).把每个分母分解，第一行写出不允许值。应用题先用文字定义变量，再加上实际限制（时间、速度 >0，人数是整数）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Every value of x that makes the ORIGINAL equation true and is allowed. There may be one, two, or none.所有让「原」方程成立而且被允许的 x——可能一个、两个，也可能没有。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 For word problems, a small table: one row per person, pipe or trip; columns for rate (or speed) and time. The equation comes from one column.应用题画一个小表：每个人、每根管或每一段路一行；列是效率（或速度）和时间。方程来自其中一列。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Multiply EVERY term on both sides — whole numbers too — by the LCD. Solve the linear or quadratic equation left over.把两边「每一项」——包括整数项——都乘以最简公分母，再解剩下的一次或二次方程。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Cross out any answer that is an NPV or breaks the context. Put the survivors back into the original equation.划掉等于不允许值或不符合实际的答案，把剩下的代回原方程检验。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • NPVs first; then multiply EVERY term (whole numbers too) by the LCD.先写不允许值；再把每一项（包括整数项）都乘以公分母。
  • An answer equal to an NPV is extraneous — reject it; all rejected → no solution.等于不允许值的解是增根，舍去；全部舍去 → 无解。
  • Work: rates add, t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​. Travel: time =speeddistance​.工作问题：效率相加，t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​。行程问题：时间 =速度路程​。
