🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Write a, b, c with their signs. Note whether a is 1, another positive number, or negative.带符号写出 a、b、c。看清 a 是 1、别的正数，还是负数。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 The form y=a(x−p)2+q with the SAME a, plus the vertex (p,q) and, if asked, the max/min value or the range.要的是 y=a(x−p)2+q，a 和原来「一样」；再写出顶点 (p,q)，题目要的话还有最值或值域。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Sketch where the vertex should be: p=−2ab​ sets the axis, and the sign of a says whether q is a minimum or a maximum.先估顶点在哪：p=−2ab​ 定对称轴，a 的符号决定 q 是最小值还是最大值。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Factor a out of the x2 and x terms only. Inside, add and subtract (2b′​)2, where b′ is the new x-coefficient. Move the subtracted number out, multiplied by a.只从 x2 项和 x 项提出 a。括号里加上再减去 (2b′​)2（b′ 是括号里新的 x 系数）。把减掉的数乘以 a 再移出括号。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Your p must equal −2ab​, and f(p) in the ORIGINAL must equal your q. Expanding back is the full check.你的 p 必须等于 −2ab​，把 p 代入「原」式得到的值必须等于你的 q。展开回去是最彻底的检验。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • a=1: factor a from the x2 and x terms only; the subtracted square leaves the bracket times a.a=1：只从 x2 项和 x 项提出 a；减掉的平方数移出括号时要乘 a。
  • Check: vertex x=−2ab​, vertex y=f(−2ab​).检验：顶点横坐标 x=−2ab​，纵坐标 y=f(−2ab​)。
x2+bx=(x+2b​)2−(2b​)2

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Moving the number out without multiplying by a移出括号时没乘 a
  2. Wrong sign inside the bracket括号里的符号写反了
  3. Adding the square but never subtracting it只加了平方数，没有再减掉
  4. Factoring out a negative a but keeping the old sign提出负的 a 却没给 x 项变号
  5. Using b instead of half of b用了 b，而不是 b 的一半
