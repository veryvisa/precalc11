Factoring trinomials ax²+bx+c三项式 ax²+bx+c 的分解

Thinking map — keep it next to you while you work思路图——做题时放在手边

🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Write a, b, c with their signs. First look for a GCF (and a negative leading coefficient) and take it out.带符号写出 a、b、c。先看有没有公因式（首项为负也算），有就先提出来。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Two brackets that multiply back to the original. "Factor fully" means nothing inside can be factored again.要两个括号，相乘能还原原式。「完全分解」意味着括号里不能再分解。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A small sign table: product ac positive → same signs (the sign of b); negative → opposite signs, the bigger one takes the sign of b.画一个小符号表：积 ac 为正 → 同号（与 b 同号）；为负 → 异号，绝对值大的与 b 同号。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 If a=1, find two numbers with product c and sum b. If a=1, product ac and sum b, split the middle term, then group.a=1 时找积为 c、和为 b 的两个数；a=1 时找积为 ac、和为 b 的两个数，拆中间项，再分组。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Expand your brackets — at least the middle term (outer + inner). It must match bx exactly, sign included.把括号展开——至少算中间项（外项＋内项）。它必须和 bx 完全一样，包括符号。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • a=1: two numbers that multiply to c and add to b.a=1：找两个数，积为 c、和为 b。
  • a=1: two numbers that multiply to ac and add to b; split the middle, then group.a=1：找积为 ac、和为 b 的两个数，拆中间项再分组。
  • Signs: c>0 → both take the sign of b; c<0 → opposite signs, bigger one takes the sign of b.符号：c>0 → 两数同号，与 b 同号；c<0 → 两数异号，绝对值大的与 b 同号。
x2+(m+n)x+mn=(x+m)(x+n)
ax2+bx+c:  mn=ac,  m+n=b ⇒ ax2+mx+nx+c

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Signs swapped in the brackets括号里的符号放反了
  2. Searching on c instead of a times c用 c 找数，而不是用 a 乘 c
  3. Stopping before the GCF is out公因式没提完就停了
  4. Checking the product but not the sum只验了积，没验和
  5. Losing the minus sign when grouping分组时丢了负号
