🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Is it × or ÷? Factor every numerator and every denominator before anything else.是乘还是除？先把每个分子、每个分母都分解，再做别的。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 One fraction in lowest terms, followed by EVERY non-permissible value.一个最简分式，后面写上「全部」不允许值。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Draw three boxes for NPVs: bottom of fraction 1, bottom of fraction 2, and (for division only) top of the divisor. Fill all three before you flip.画三个格子装不允许值：第 1 个分式的分母、第 2 个分式的分母，以及（只在除法时）除式的分子。颠倒之前把三个格子都填好。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Division: keep the first fraction, change ÷ to ×, flip the SECOND fraction. Then cancel any top factor with any bottom factor and multiply what is left.除法：第一个分式不变，÷ 变 ×，把「第二个」分式颠倒。然后任一分子因式都可以和任一分母因式约分，再把剩下的乘起来。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Test an allowed value such as x=0 or x=1 in the original and in your answer. Count your NPVs against the three boxes.用一个允许的值（如 x=0 或 x=1）分别代入原式和答案。再对照三个格子数一数不允许值。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Multiply: factor everything, cancel any top with any bottom, then multiply what is left.乘法：全部分解，任一分子可与任一分母约分，再把剩下的相乘。
  • Divide: multiply by the reciprocal of the SECOND fraction.除法：乘以第二个分式的倒数。
  • Division NPVs: both denominators AND the numerator of the divisor.除法的不允许值：两个分母，再加上除式的分子。

Simplify x+2x−5​÷x−1x−5​ and state all NPVs.化简 x+2x−5​÷x−1x−5​，并写出全部不允许值。

  2. line第 1 — In x+5 the x is a term that is ADDED, not a factor of the whole top.
