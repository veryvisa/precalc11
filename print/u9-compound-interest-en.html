Thinking map — keep it next to you while you work思路图——做题时放在手边

🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 List the numbers: principal P, yearly rate r as a DECIMAL, times per year it compounds n, and years t. Read the words for n: annually 1, semi-annually 2, quarterly 4, monthly 12.列出数字：本金 P、年利率 r（写成「小数」）、每年计息次数 n、年数 t。从文字里读出 n：按年 1，半年 2，按季 4，按月 12。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Is the unknown the final amount A, the interest A−P, or the amount to invest now, P? Money is rounded to the cent, at the very end.要求的是终值 A、利息 A−P，还是现在要投入的 P？金额四舍五入到分，而且只在最后一步。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A growth table: each period the balance is multiplied by (1+i). After N periods it has been multiplied N times.一张增长表：每过一期，余额乘一次 (1+i)。过了 N 期就乘了 N 次。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 i=nr​ and N=nt, then A=P(1+i)N typed in ONE line with brackets. To find P, divide A by (1+i)N. ("Compounded semi-annually", the usual way a Canadian fixed-rate mortgage is quoted, means n=2.)i=nr​，N=nt，再把 A=P(1+i)N 带括号「一行」输入计算器。求 P 就用 A 除以 (1+i)N。（「半年复利」——加拿大固定利率房贷通常这样标——就是 n=2。）
  5. ✅ Check检验 A must be bigger than P, and a little bigger than simple interest P(1+rt). The Rule of 72 gives a rough size.A 一定比 P 大，而且比单利 P(1+rt) 稍大一点。72 法则可以估个大概。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • A=P(1+nr​)nt: r as a decimal, n = periods per year, exponent nt.A=P(1+nr​)nt：r 用小数，n 是每年计息次数，指数是 nt。
  • Per period i=nr​; periods =nt (monthly for 6 years = 72).每期利率 i=nr​；期数 =nt（按月 6 年＝72 期）。
  • Rule of 72: doubling time ≈rate in %72​ years — an estimate only.72 法则：翻倍时间 ≈年利率（%）72​ 年——只是估算。

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Using the percent as a whole number把百分数当整数用
  2. Using years instead of periods as the exponent指数用了年数而不是期数
  3. Forgetting to divide the rate by n忘了把利率除以 n
  4. Working out simple interest instead算成了单利
  5. Rounding the rate per period too early每期利率舍入太早

1. Using the percent as a whole number把百分数当整数用
$1000 is invested at 4% compounded annually for 3 years. Find the amount.$1000 以 4%、按年复利投资 3 年。求终值。
1 r=4
2 A=1000(1+4)3=125000

2. Using years instead of periods as the exponent指数用了年数而不是期数
$2000 is invested at 6% compounded monthly for 4 years. Find the amount.$2000 以 6%、按月复利投资 4 年。求终值。
1 i=0.06÷12=0.005
2 A=2000(1.005)4≈2040.30

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

3. Forgetting to divide the rate by n忘了把利率除以 n
$1500 is invested at 8% compounded quarterly for 3 years. Find the amount.$1500 以 8%、按季度复利投资 3 年。求终值。
1 i=0.08, N=4×3=12i=0.08，N=4×3=12
2 A=1500(1.08)12≈3777.26

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

4. Working out simple interest instead算成了单利
$5000 is invested at 3% compounded annually for 4 years. Find the amount.$5000 以 3%、按年复利投资 4 年。求终值。
1 Interest =5000(0.03)(4)=600利息 =5000(0.03)(4)=600
2 A=5000+600=5600

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

5. Rounding the rate per period too early每期利率舍入太早
$6000 is invested at 5% compounded monthly for 10 years. Find the amount.$6000 以 5%、按月复利投资 10 年。求终值。
1 i=0.05÷12≈0.004
2 A=6000(1.004)120≈9687.17

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 1 — 4% means 4 out of 100, so r=0.04. With r=4 the money would grow 400% a year.4% 是百分之四，r=0.04。用 r=4 等于每年涨 400%。 A=1000(1.04)3≈$1124.86.A=1000(1.04)3≈$1124.86。
  2. line第 2 — The balance is multiplied by 1.005 once a MONTH. Four years is 12×4=48 months, so the exponent is 48.余额每「月」乘一次 1.005。4 年是 12×4=48 个月，指数应是 48。 A=2000(1.005)48≈$2540.98.A=2000(1.005)48≈$2540.98。
  3. line第 1 — 8% is the rate for a whole YEAR. Each quarter earns only a quarter of it: i=0.08÷4=0.02.8% 是「一整年」的利率。每个季度只拿四分之一：i=0.08÷4=0.02。 A=1500(1.02)12≈$1902.36. (The wrong answer, $3777.26, more than doubles the money in 3 years, which should look suspicious.)A=1500(1.02)12≈$1902.36。（错误答案 3777.26 加元，3 年就让钱翻了一倍多，这本身就该起疑。）
  4. line第 1 — That gives interest on the first $5000 only. With compounding, each year's interest also earns interest.这样只算了最初 $5000 的利息。复利时，每年的利息也会再生利息。 A=5000(1.03)4≈$5627.54, about $27.54 more than simple interest.A=5000(1.03)4≈$5627.54，比单利多约 $27.54。
  5. line第 1 — 0.05÷12=0.0041666… Cutting it to 0.004 looks tiny, but the error is raised to the power 120.0.05÷12=0.0041666…，舍成 0.004 看着差得不多，可这个误差被乘方了 120 次。 Type it in one line: 6000(1+0.05/12)120≈$9882.06, about $195 more.一行输入：6000(1+0.05/12)120≈$9882.06，多出约 $195。
