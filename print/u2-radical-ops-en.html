🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Which operation is it: add/subtract, multiply, or a radical in the denominator? Simplify every radical first; unlike-looking ones often turn out alike.是哪种运算：加减、相乘，还是分母里有根号？先把每个根式化简；看起来不同类的常常会变成同类。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 An exact answer in simplest form: like radicals collected, no square factor inside a root, and no root left in the denominator.最简精确值：同类根式已合并，根号里没有平方因数，分母里不留根号。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 For two brackets, a 2×2 box (rows = first bracket, columns = second). For a binomial denominator, write its conjugate right under it with the middle sign flipped.两个括号相乘时，画一个 2×2 方格（行是第一个括号，列是第二个）。分母是二项式时，在它正下方写出共轭式，中间的符号反过来。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Add only like radicals. Multiply outside × outside, inside × inside. One-term denominator: multiply by b​b​​. Two-term denominator: multiply top AND bottom by the conjugate.只合并同类根式。相乘：外乘外、里乘里。分母是单项：乘 b​b​​。分母是两项：分子分母「同时」乘共轭式。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Type the original and your answer into a calculator; they must agree to at least 3 decimal places.把原式和你的答案都输进计算器，至少小数点后 3 位要一致。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Add/subtract LIKE radicals only — simplify each first: 12​+27​=53​.只有同类根式才能加减——先逐个化简：12​+27​=53​。
  • Multiply outside × outside, inside × inside; expand brackets like FOIL.相乘：系数乘系数，根号里乘根号里；括号像多项式一样展开。
  • Rationalize: ×b​b​​; binomial bottom → × conjugate, (a+b​)(a−b​)=a2−b.分母有理化：单项乘 b​b​​；二项分母乘共轭式，(a+b​)(a−b​)=a2−b。

  3. Multiplying only the bottom by the conjugate只给分母乘共轭式

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 1 — Adding the insides feels like the same move as multiplying them. Only like radicals add, and only their outside numbers: like 2a+3a=5a.感觉相加和相乘一样，把里面的数直接合起来就行。其实只有同类根式能加，而且加的是外面的系数：就像 2a+3a=5a。 Simplify first: 22​+32​=52​. Decimal check: 2.83+4.24=7.07=52​, not 26​≈5.10.先化简：22​+32​=52​。小数检验：2.83+4.24=7.07=52​，而 26​≈5.10。
  2. line第 1 — Squaring each piece feels like the power rule (ab)2=a2b2, but that works for products, not sums. (A−B)2 means (A−B)(A−B): four products.把每一部分分别平方，像是用了 (ab)2=a2b2；可那只对乘积成立，对和差不成立。(A−B)2 就是 (A−B)(A−B)，要乘出四项。 5−2⋅25​+4=9−45​.5−2⋅25​+4=9−45​。
  3. line第 1 — The focus is on getting rid of the root below, so the top is forgotten. Multiplying only the bottom changes the value; top and bottom together is multiplying by 1.一心想消掉分母的根号，就忘了分子。只乘分母会改变值；分子分母一起乘才等于乘 1。 22(6​−2)​=6​−2≈0.449. Check: 2÷4.449≈0.449, not 1.22(6​−2)​=6​−2≈0.449。检验：2÷4.449≈0.449，不是 1。
