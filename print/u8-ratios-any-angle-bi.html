🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 What do you have: a point, or one ratio plus a clue (a quadrant, or the sign of another ratio)? Write the two of x, y, r you know, with signs.已知什么：一个点，还是一个比值加一条线索（象限，或另一个比值的符号）？写出已知的 x、y、r 中的两个，带上符号。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Which ratios, exact or decimal? And θ in 0∘≤θ<360∘, rounded as asked.要哪些比值，精确值还是小数？以及 0∘≤θ<360∘ 内的 θ，按要求取近似。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Sketch the axes, put the point in its quadrant, drop a line to the x-axis to make the reference triangle, and label x, y, r.画坐标轴，把点放进它的象限，向 x 轴作垂线得到参考三角形，标出 x、y、r。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Missing piece from x2+y2=r2 (sign from the quadrant, r always positive). Then sin=ry​, cos=rx​, tan=xy​; reference angle from the positive ratio; place it: 180∘−, 180∘+ or 360∘−.用 x2+y2=r2 求缺的那个（符号看象限，r 永远为正）。再写 sin=ry​、cos=rx​、tan=xy​；用正的比值求参考角，再放到象限里：180∘−、180∘+ 或 360∘−。
  5. ✅ Check检验 CAST: do the signs of your three ratios match the quadrant? Is θ really in that quadrant?CAST：三个比值的符号和象限对得上吗？θ 真的落在那个象限吗？

  • One ratio + quadrant: draw the triangle, find the third side, give x,y their quadrant signs.已知一个比值和象限：画三角形，求第三边，按象限给 x,y 定符号。
  • Angle: reference angle from the positive ratio, then place it in the quadrant.求角：用正的比值求参考角，再放进对应象限。

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 2 — A square root from a calculator is always positive, so the quadrant never got a say. In Q II the point is left of the y-axis.计算器开方总给正数，象限根本没被考虑进去。第二象限的点在 y 轴左边。 x=±12; Q II needs x<0, so x=−12 and cosθ=−1312​.x=±12；第二象限要 x<0，所以 x=−12，cosθ=−1312​。
  2. line第 3 — The calculator answers in its own range, not the range the question set. Its output is a clue, not the answer.计算器按自己的范围给答案，不按题目要求的范围。它给的只是线索，不是答案。 Q IV, reference angle tan−1815​≈61.9∘: θ=360∘−61.9∘=298.1∘.第四象限，参考角 tan−1815​≈61.9∘：θ=360∘−61.9∘=298.1∘。
  3. line第 2 — SOH CAH TOA was learned with "opposite" and "adjacent", and the switch to x and y did not stick. Sine goes with the height y.SOH CAH TOA 是用「对边」「邻边」学的，换成 x、y 后没记牢。正弦对应的是高度 y。 sinθ=ry​=4140​ (and cosθ=rx​=−419​).sinθ=ry​=4140​（而 cosθ=rx​=−419​）。
  4. line第 1 — Only one clue was used. sin negative allows Q III or Q IV; the second clue, tan positive, is what picks between them.只用了一条线索。sin 为负可能是第三或第四象限；第二条线索 tan 为正才能二选一。 sin <0 and tan >0: Q III. θ=180∘+36.9∘=216.9∘.sin <0 且 tan >0：第三象限。θ=180∘+36.9∘=216.9∘。
