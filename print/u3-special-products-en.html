🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 Count the terms. Is there a common factor? Write each square term as (something)2, and note the sign between them.数一数有几项。有没有公因式？把每个平方项写成 (某个式子)2，再看它们之间是什么符号。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 A product of brackets that cannot be split any further over the integers ("factor fully").写成乘积，而且每个括号在整数范围内都不能再分（「完全分解」）。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 A big square of side A with a small square of side B cut from the corner: the leftover L-shape rearranges into an (A−B) by (A+B) rectangle. A trinomial (A+B)2 is the big square split into A2, B2 and two AB strips.边长 A 的大正方形，从角上剪掉边长 B 的小正方形：剩下的 L 形能拼成 (A−B)×(A+B) 的长方形。三项式 (A+B)2 就是大正方形分成 A2、B2 和两条 AB。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Common factor first. Two squares with a minus: (A−B)(A+B). Three terms with square ends: test whether the middle is exactly 2AB; if yes (A±B)2, if not factor it as an ordinary trinomial.先提公因式。两个平方相减：(A−B)(A+B)。三项且首末是平方：检验中间项是不是正好 2AB；是就写 (A±B)2，不是就按普通三项式分解。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Expand your answer and get the original back. Then look at every bracket once more: is any of them still a difference of squares?把答案展开，要能还原成原式。再把每个括号看一遍：还有没有平方差可以分？

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • A2−B2=(A−B)(A+B): two squares with a MINUS.A2−B2=(A−B)(A+B)：两个平方数，中间是减号。
  • A2±2AB+B2=(A±B)2: check the middle is exactly 2AB.A2±2AB+B2=(A±B)2：一定要验证中间项正好是 2AB。
  • A2+B2 does not factor over the reals; keep going on x4−16.A2+B2 在实数范围内不能分解；x4−16 要继续分解。
A2−B2=(A−B)(A+B)
A2±2AB+B2=(A±B)2

🔍 Watch out for当心

  1. Factoring a sum of squares把平方和也分解了
  2. Stopping before it is fully factored没分解完就停了
  3. Writing a difference of squares as a perfect square把平方差写成了完全平方
  4. Calling it a perfect square without checking the middle没验中间项就当成完全平方
  5. Not looking for a common factor first没先找公因式

🔍 Common-errors sheet常见错误单

1. Factoring a sum of squares把平方和也分解了
Factor fully: 16x2+25.完全分解：16x2+25。
1 Both terms are squares: (4x)2 and 52.两项都是平方：(4x)2 和 52。
2 So 16x2+25=(4x−5)(4x+5).所以 16x2+25=(4x−5)(4x+5)。

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

2. Stopping before it is fully factored没分解完就停了
Factor fully: 16x4−1.完全分解：16x4−1。
1 16x4=(4x2)2 and 1=12.16x4=(4x2)2，1=12。
2 16x4−1=(4x2−1)(4x2+1). Done.16x4−1=(4x2−1)(4x2+1)。分解完了。

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

3. Writing a difference of squares as a perfect square把平方差写成了完全平方
Factor 9x2−49.分解因式 9x2−49。
1 9x2=(3x)2 and 49=72, with a minus between.9x2=(3x)2，49=72，中间是减号。
2 So 9x2−49=(3x−7)2.所以 9x2−49=(3x−7)2。

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

4. Calling it a perfect square without checking the middle没验中间项就当成完全平方
Factor x2+13x+36.分解因式 x2+13x+36。
1 x2=(x)2 and 36=62, so this is a perfect square.x2=(x)2，36=62，所以是完全平方。
2 x2+13x+36=(x+6)2.x2+13x+36=(x+6)2。

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

5. Not looking for a common factor first没先找公因式
Factor fully: 12x2−75.完全分解：12x2−75。
1 12 is not a perfect square, and neither is 75.12 不是完全平方数，75 也不是。
2 So 12x2−75 cannot be factored.所以 12x2−75 不能分解。

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 2 — The pattern (A−B)(A+B) needs a MINUS between the squares. Expanding (4x−5)(4x+5) gives 16x2−25, not +25.公式 (A−B)(A+B) 要求两个平方之间是减号。展开 (4x−5)(4x+5) 得 16x2−25，不是 +25。 A sum of two squares has no real factors, and 16 and 25 share no common factor, so it stays as it is.两个平方的和没有实数因式，16 和 25 也没有公因数，所以保持原样。
  2. line第 2 — The first round is right, but "factor fully" means checking each bracket again. 4x2−1=(2x)2−12 is another difference of squares.第一轮是对的，但「完全分解」要把每个括号再查一遍。4x2−1=(2x)2−12 还是平方差。 4x2−1=(2x−1)(2x+1); 4x2+1 is a sum and stays. So (2x−1)(2x+1)(4x2+1).4x2−1=(2x−1)(2x+1)；4x2+1 是平方和，保留。所以是 (2x−1)(2x+1)(4x2+1)。
  3. line第 2 — The minus sign got copied into both brackets. (3x−7)2=9x2−42x+49 has a middle term and ends in +49; a difference of squares needs one minus bracket and one plus bracket.把减号抄进了两个括号。(3x−7)2=9x2−42x+49 有中间项，末项是 +49；平方差要一个减号括号、一个加号括号。 9x2−49=(3x−7)(3x+7). Expand to check: 9x2+21x−21x−49 ✓.9x2−49=(3x−7)(3x+7)。展开检验：9x2+21x−21x−49 ✓。
  4. line第 1 — Square ends are not enough. For (x+6)2 the middle would be 2⋅x⋅6=12x, but here it is 13x.首末是平方还不够。(x+6)2 的中间项应是 2⋅x⋅6=12x，这里却是 13x。 Treat it as an ordinary trinomial: product 36, sum 13 gives 4 and 9, so (x+4)(x+9).按普通三项式分解：积 36、和 13，得 4 和 9，所以是 (x+4)(x+9)。
  5. line第 2 — The squares are hiding behind a common factor. Both 12 and 75 are multiples of 3, and after taking it out you get 4x2−25.平方藏在公因式后面。12 和 75 都是 3 的倍数，提出 3 后得到 4x2−25。 12x2−75=3(4x2−25)=3(2x−5)(2x+5).12x2−75=3(4x2−25)=3(2x−5)(2x+5)。
