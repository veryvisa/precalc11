🧭 Five questions before you write动笔前的五个问题

  1. 🔎 Given已知 What changes, and by how much each step (price step, sales lost, metres of fence)? Name the variable and say what it counts, e.g. "n = number of 1-dollar increases".什么在变？每一步变多少（涨价幅度、少卖多少、多少米篱笆）？设好变量并说清它数的是什么，比如「n = 涨 1 加元的次数」。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么 Is it asking WHEN or WHICH value (the x-coordinate of the vertex), HOW MUCH (the y-coordinate), or both? In what units?问的是「何时／取何值」（顶点横坐标）、「是多少」（顶点纵坐标），还是两样都要？单位是什么？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来 Sketch the shape (a pen against a wall, three widths) or make a small table of n, price and number sold. The graph is a downward parabola; the answer is at the top.画出形状（靠墙的围栏、三条宽），或者列一个 n、单价、销量的小表。图象是开口向下的抛物线，答案在最高点。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具 Write the quantity as a product of two linear factors, e.g. (price)(number sold) or (width)(length). Find where each factor is zero; the vertex is halfway between. If it is already expanded, use −2ab​.把要求的量写成两个一次因式的乘积，比如（单价）×（销量）或（宽）×（长）。求出每个因式为零的点，顶点就在两点正中间。如果已经展开了，就用 −2ab​。
  5. ✅ Check检验 Try one step either side of your answer: both must give less. Then answer the question asked in a sentence with units, and make sure it fits the story (price and sides positive).在答案两侧各试一步：两边都必须更小。然后用带单位的一句话回答题目真正问的，并看它是否符合情境（单价、边长为正）。

⭐ Must know必须记住

  • Optimize = find the vertex. "When / which value" → x-coordinate; "how much" → y-coordinate.求最值 = 求顶点。「何时／取何值」→ 横坐标；「最多／最少是多少」→ 纵坐标。
  • Build the function first: area =lw, revenue = price × number sold.先建函数：面积 = 长 × 宽，收入 = 单价 × 销量。
  • Product of two linear factors: best x = midpoint of their zeros.两个一次因式相乘：最优 x = 两个零点的中点。

5. Losing 1 sale per step instead of the given drop每涨一次只减 1 个，忘了题给的减少量
A museum has 400 visitors a day at 14 dollars each. Each 1-dollar increase loses 20 visitors. What price maximizes revenue?博物馆每张门票 14 加元时每天有 400 名游客。每涨 1 加元，少 20 名游客。票价定多少收入最高？
1 Let n = number of 1-dollar increases. Price 14+n, visitors 400−n.设 n = 涨 1 加元的次数。票价 14+n，游客 400−n。
2 R=(14+n)(400−n); zeros −14 and 400, so n=193.R=(14+n)(400−n)；零点 −14 和 400，所以 n=193。
3 Price 14+193=207 dollars.票价 14+193=207 加元。

✍️ Which line goes wrong first, and why?哪一行最先出错？为什么？

Key (fold this part back)答案（可以折起来）
  1. line第 2 — t=2 tells you WHEN the top happens. The question asks HOW HIGH, which is the h-value at that time.t=2 说的是「什么时候」到最高点。题目问的是「多高」，要的是那一刻的 h 值。 h(2)=−20+40+1=21. The maximum height is 21 m, reached after 2 s.h(2)=−20+40+1=21。最大高度 21 m，在第 2 秒到达。
  2. line第 3 — n counts the 1-dollar INCREASES, not the price. A price of 9 dollars would be a price cut, which the model never described.n 数的是涨价的「次数」，不是价格。9 加元等于降价了，模型里根本没有这回事。 Price =12+9=21 dollars, sales 150−45=105, revenue 21×105=2205 dollars.价格 =12+9=21 加元，销量 150−45=105 件，收入 21×105=2205 加元。
  3. line第 1 — The wall is free; only two widths and ONE length use fence. Using the full perimeter spends fence on a side that needs none.墙是现成的，只有两条宽和「一条」长要用篱笆。用整个周长等于在不需要的边上也花了篱笆。 2w+l=100, l=100−2w, A=w(100−2w); zeros 0 and 50, w=25, l=50, A=1250 m².2w+l=100，l=100−2w，A=w(100−2w)；零点 0 和 50，w=25，l=50，A=1250 m²。
  4. line第 1 — The formula is −2ab​. Without the 2 you land at the time the ball comes back to its starting height, which is the clue: the "maximum" equals the starting height 5.公式是 −2ab​。漏了 2，就落在小球回到起始高度的时刻——破绽就在这里：「最大高度」竟然等于起始高度 5。 t=−2(−4)24​=3; h(3)=−36+72+5=41 m.t=−2(−4)24​=3；h(3)=−36+72+5=41 m。
  5. line第 1 — Each increase loses 20 visitors, so after n increases 20n are gone. Writing 400−n means only one visitor leaves per step, and the answer (207 dollars for a museum) fails the sense check.每涨一次少 20 人，涨 n 次就少 20n 人。写成 400−n 等于每次只走一个人，结果（博物馆门票 207 加元）通不过合理性检查。 Visitors 400−20n.
