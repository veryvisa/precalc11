The real number system实数系统

  • The families nest inside each other: natural N={1,2,3,…} ⊂ whole W={0,1,2,…} ⊂ integers I ⊂ rational Q ⊂ real R. A number belongs to every family that contains its smallest family.数的家族一层套一层：自然数 N={1,2,3,…} ⊂ 整数（非负）W={0,1,2,…} ⊂ 整数 I ⊂ 有理数 Q ⊂ 实数 R。一个数属于包含它最小家族的所有家族。
  • Rational = can be written as ba​ with integers a,b, b=0. Its decimal either terminates (0.75) or repeats (0.36).有理数＝能写成 ba​（a,b 为整数，b=0）。它的小数要么有限（0.75），要么循环（0.36）。
  • Irrational Q​ = real but NOT rational: decimals never end and never repeat, e.g. π, 2​, 35​. Rational and irrational together make up the reals; no number is both.无理数 Q​＝是实数但不是有理数：小数无限且不循环，如 π、2​、35​。有理数和无理数合起来就是实数，没有数同时属于两者。
  • Simplify before you classify: 49​=7 and 3−8​=−2 are integers. An even root of a negative, like −4​, is not a real number at all.先化简再分类：49​=7、3−8​=−2 都是整数。负数的偶次方根（如 −4​）根本不是实数。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Calling 16​ or 3−8​ irrational because of the root sign. Always evaluate first: they are 4 and −2.看到根号就说 16​、3−8​ 是无理数。一定先求值：它们分别是 4 和 −2。
  • Thinking 2π​ or 32​​ is rational because it "looks like a fraction". Rational means top AND bottom are integers.以为 2π​、32​​ 长得像分数就是有理数。有理数要求分子分母都是整数。
  • Calling a long calculator decimal like 3.14159265 irrational. The screen just runs out of digits — decide from the exact form, not the display.看到计算器显示一长串小数就判定为无理数。屏幕只是位数不够——要按精确形式判断，不按显示。
  • Forgetting 0 is a whole number but not a natural number.忘了 0 是非负整数（W），但不是自然数（N）。

🎯 Classification questions are quick marks: simplify every root first, then list ALL sets (e.g. 4∈N,W,I,Q,R). Missing the bigger sets is the most common lost mark.分类题是送分题：先把每个根式化简，再写出它所属的全部数集（如 4∈N,W,I,Q,R）。漏写更大的数集是最常见的失分点。

Exponent laws (zero & negative)指数律（零指数与负指数）

  • Same base: multiply → ADD exponents; divide → SUBTRACT exponents. Power of a power → MULTIPLY exponents.同底数：相乘 → 指数相加；相除 → 指数相减。幂的乘方 → 指数相乘。
  • A power outside brackets hits EVERY factor inside, numbers included: (3x2)2=9x4, not 3x4.括号外的指数要作用到括号里的每一个因数，包括数字：(3x2)2=9x4，不是 3x4。
  • Zero exponent: a0=1 (a=0). Negative exponent means reciprocal: a−n=an1​. A factor with a negative exponent moves across the fraction bar and becomes positive.零指数：a0=1（a=0）。负指数表示倒数：a−n=an1​。带负指数的因数跨过分数线就变成正指数。
  • Final answers use positive exponents only. Order of work: brackets/powers first, then combine same bases.最终答案只写正指数。顺序：先处理括号和乘方，再合并同底数。
am⋅an=am+n,anam​=am−n,(am)n=amn

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Not raising the coefficient: (3x2)3=27x6, not 3x6.系数没乘方：(3x2)3=27x6，不是 3x6。
  • Treating a negative exponent as a negative number: 2−3=81​, not −8 or −6.把负指数当成负数：2−3=81​，不是 −8 或 −6。
  • Mixing up −32 and (−3)2: the first is −9 (only 3 is squared), the second is 9.混淆 −32 与 (−3)2：前者是 −9（只有 3 平方），后者是 9。
  • Moving a whole term instead of one factor: in x−2+y1​ you cannot just move x−2 up — the rule works for factors, not for terms joined by +.把整项而不是因数挪过分数线：x−2+y1​ 里的 x−2 不能直接挪到分子——法则只对因数成立，对用 + 连接的项不成立。

🎯 Tests love one big fraction with brackets and negative exponents. Write one line per step (power → numbers → each base) and finish by converting every negative exponent to positive — answers with negative exponents usually lose a mark.考试最爱出一个带括号和负指数的大分式。每步一行（乘方 → 数字 → 每个底数），最后把所有负指数变成正指数——答案里留负指数通常要扣分。

Rational exponents分数指数

  • The denominator of the exponent is the root; the numerator is the power: am/n=(na​)m=nam​.指数的分母是开几次方，分子是几次幂：am/n=(na​)m=nam​。
  • Take the root FIRST when evaluating by hand — the numbers stay small: 272/3=32=9 (not 3729​).手算时先开方——数字保持很小：272/3=32=9（不用算 3729​）。
  • A negative rational exponent still means reciprocal: 16−3/4=163/41​=81​. The sign of the exponent never makes the answer negative.负的分数指数仍然表示取倒数：16−3/4=163/41​=81​。指数是负的，结果并不会变成负数。
  • All the exponent laws still work with fractions — you just add, subtract and multiply fractions.所有指数律对分数指数照样成立——只是变成分数的加、减、乘。
an1​=na​,anm​=(na​)m=nam​

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Flipping the fraction: a2/3 is the CUBE root squared, not the square root cubed.把分数指数读反：a2/3 是「开立方再平方」，不是「开平方再立方」。
  • Making the answer negative: 16−1/2=41​, not −4.把结果写成负数：16−1/2=41​，不是 −4。
  • Calculator brackets: 272/3 must be typed as 27^(2/3). Without brackets the calculator does 272÷3=243.计算器漏括号：272/3 要输入 27^(2/3)。不加括号会算成 272÷3=243。
  • Even root of a negative base: (−16)1/2 is not real, but (−8)1/3=−2 is fine (odd root).负底数开偶次方：(−16)1/2 不是实数；但 (−8)1/3=−2 没问题（奇次方根）。

🎯 Non-calculator sections test am/n with nice bases (8, 16, 27, 32, 64, 81, 125). Memorize small powers of 2, 3 and 5 — then every question is "root, power, flip if negative".不许用计算器的部分常考底数是 8、16、27、32、64、81、125 的 am/n。记住 2、3、5 的小次幂，每道题就是「开方、乘方、负指数就倒过来」。
