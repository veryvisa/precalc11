📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • c2=a2+b2−2abcosC (c opposite C); C=90∘ gives Pythagoras.c2=a2+b2−2abcosC（c 对角 C）；C=90∘ 时就是勾股定理。
  • SAS → third side; SSS → angle from cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​.SAS → 求第三边；SSS → 由 cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​ 求角。
  • cosC<0 → C is obtuse; cos−1 handles it, no ambiguous case.cosC<0 → C 为钝角；cos−1 直接给出，没有两解问题。

💡 A boat sails 30 km, turns, and sails 45 km more. How far is it from port? You know two sides and the angle between them — the sine law cannot start, but the cosine law finishes it in one line.一艘船航行 30 km 后转向，又航行 45 km。它离港口多远？你知道两条边和它们的夹角——正弦定理开不了头，余弦定理一行就解决。

  1. c2=a2+b2−2abcosC. It is Pythagoras with a correction term; when C=90∘, cosC=0 and it becomes c2=a2+b2.c2=a2+b2−2abcosC。它是带修正项的勾股定理；当 C=90∘ 时 cosC=0，就变回 c2=a2+b2。
  2. Use it for SAS (two sides and the angle BETWEEN them → find the third side) and SSS (three sides → find an angle).用于 SAS（两边及其「夹角」→ 求第三边）和 SSS（三边 → 求角）。
  3. For an angle, rearrange first: cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​. A negative value means C is obtuse — the inverse cosine handles that correctly, no ambiguity.求角先变形：cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​。值为负说明 C 是钝角——反余弦能正确给出，没有歧义。

🎯 Test tip考试提示
Decide the law in 5 seconds: complete side–angle pair → sine law; SAS or SSS → cosine law. When solving a whole triangle from SSS, find the LARGEST angle first with the cosine law — then any sine-law step afterwards cannot hit the ambiguous case.5 秒内选定理：有完整的「边—对角」→ 正弦定理；SAS 或 SSS → 余弦定理。由 SSS 解整个三角形时，先用余弦定理求「最大」的角，之后再用正弦定理就不会碰到两解情形。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Mark what you know on a sketch and write each side next to the angle across from it (a faces A). Two sides and the angle BETWEEN them is SAS; three sides is SSS.在草图上标出已知量，把每条边写在它对面的角旁边（a 对着 A）。两边及其「夹角」是 SAS；三条边是 SSS。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    A side (SAS: the side across from the known angle) or an angle (SSS)? Give sides to the nearest tenth and angles in degrees.求边（SAS：已知角对面的那条边）还是求角（SSS）？边精确到 0.1，角用度。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    A rough triangle drawn roughly to scale. The biggest angle faces the longest side, and an obtuse angle makes its opposite side longer than Pythagoras would give.大致按比例画个三角形。最大的角对最长的边；钝角对面的边比用勾股定理算出的还长。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    SAS: c2=a2+b2−2abcosC; work out 2abcosC first, subtract, then square-root. SSS: cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​, then cos−1.SAS：c2=a2+b2−2abcosC；先算 2abcosC，再相减，最后开方。SSS：cosC=2aba2+b2−c2​，再用 cos−1。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Calculator in DEGREE mode. Does the biggest angle face the longest side? Is the answer a length, not c2?计算器是「角度制」吗？最大的角对着最长的边吗？答案是边长，而不是 c2 吗？

2Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。

A ship leaves port P and sails 30 km on a bearing of 040∘ to point T, then turns and sails 45 km on a bearing of 110∘ to point S. (a) How far is the ship from port? (b) What is the bearing of the ship from port? Nearest tenth / nearest degree.船从港口 P 出发，沿方位角 040∘ 航行 30 km 到 T，再转向沿方位角 110∘ 航行 45 km 到 S。(a) 船离港口多远？(b) 船相对港口的方位角是多少？分别精确到 0.1 km 和 1°。
  1. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Bearings are measured clockwise from north. Sketch P→T→S with a north line at T.方位角从正北顺时针量。画出 P→T→S，并在 T 处画一条指北线。

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

1Subtracting before multiplying先减后乘
In △ABC, a=5, b=9, ∠C=40∘. Find c.在 △ABC 中，a=5，b=9，∠C=40∘。求 c。

🧠 90cos40∘ is one product. Multiplication comes before subtraction, so it must be worked out before it is taken away from 106.90cos40∘ 是一个整体的乘积。先乘后减，要先把它算出来，再从 106 里减掉。

🔧 90cos40∘≈68.94, so c2≈106−68.94=37.06 and c≈6.1.90cos40∘≈68.94，所以 c2≈106−68.94=37.06，c≈6.1。

✔ c≈6.1

2Stopping at c squared停在了 c 的平方
In △ABC, a=6, b=8, ∠C=70∘. Find c.在 △ABC 中，a=6，b=8，∠C=70∘。求 c。

🧠 The formula gives c2, not c. A side of 67 cannot sit across a 70∘ angle when the other two sides are only 6 and 8.公式算出的是 c2，不是 c。另外两边只有 6 和 8，70∘ 角对面不可能是 67。

🔧 Take the square root: c=67.17​≈8.2.开平方：c=67.17​≈8.2。

✔ c≈8.2

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 C

3Dropping the minus sign of an obtuse cosine钝角余弦丢了负号
In △ABC, a=6, b=9, ∠C=120∘. Find c.在 △ABC 中，a=6，b=9，∠C=120∘。求 c。

🧠 cos120∘=−0.5, not 0.5. Then −108(−0.5)=+54: for an obtuse angle the last term ADDS.cos120∘=−0.5，不是 0.5。于是 −108(−0.5)=+54：钝角时最后一项是「加」。

🔧 c2=117+54=171, so c≈13.1. Check: the side across from the obtuse angle must be the longest, and 7.9 is shorter than 9.c2=117+54=171，c≈13.1。检验：钝角对面的边必须最长，而 7.9 比 9 还短。

✔ c≈13.1

4Subtracting the wrong side when finding an angle求角时减错了边
A triangle has sides a=5, b=7, c=9. Find ∠A.三角形三边 a=5，b=7，c=9。求 ∠A。

🧠 That form finds the angle across from c, which is C. The side that is subtracted on top must be the side across from the angle you want.这个式子求的是 c 对面的角，也就是 C。分子里被减掉的那条边，必须是所求角对面的边。

🔧 cosA=2bcb2+c2−a2​=12649+81−25​=126105​, so A≈33.6∘. Check: a=5 is the shortest side, so A must be the smallest angle.cosA=2bcb2+c2−a2​=12649+81−25​=126105​，所以 A≈33.6∘。检验：a=5 是最短边，A 应是最小角。

✔ ∠A≈33.6∘

5Using the bearing as the angle in the triangle把方位角当成三角形内角
A hiker walks 5 km due north, then turns onto a bearing of 060∘ and walks 4 km. How far is she from where she started?一名徒步者先向正北走 5 km，再转向方位角 060∘ 走 4 km。她离出发点多远？

🧠 60∘ is how far she TURNED. The angle inside the triangle is between the path back to the start (due south) and the new path: 180∘−60∘=120∘.60∘ 是她「转过」的角度。三角形内角夹在回到起点的方向（正南）和新路线之间：180∘−60∘=120∘。

🔧 d2=25+16−40cos120∘=41+20=61, so d≈7.8 km.d2=25+16−40cos120∘=41+20=61，所以 d≈7.8 km。

✔ d≈7.8 km

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 A, Check-up测评 Q2 D

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Doing the order of operations wrong: 64+121−176cos37∘ is NOT (64+121−176)cos37∘. Multiply 2abcosC first, then subtract.运算顺序错：64+121−176cos37∘ 不是 (64+121−176)cos37∘。先算 2abcosC，再相减。
  • Matching the wrong side with the angle: in c2=a2+b2−2abcosC, c must be the side OPPOSITE C.边与角配错：c2=a2+b2−2abcosC 中，c 必须是 C 的对边。
  • Forgetting the square root at the end and reporting c2 as c.最后忘了开平方，把 c2 当成 c。
  • In bearing problems, using the bearing itself as the triangle angle. Draw north lines at every point and find the interior angle.方位角题直接把方位角当作三角形内角。要在每个点画指北线，再求内角。

The largest angle faces 90 m. Area =21​absinC.最大角对着 90 m 的边。面积 =21​absinC。
