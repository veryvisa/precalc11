📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Domain in context: time from 0 to landing, lengths >0, counts are whole numbers.情境中的定义域：时间从 0 到落地，长度 >0，个数取整数。
  • Range in context: the vertex value (if inside the domain) and the endpoint values.情境中的值域：顶点值（若在定义域内）和端点值。
  • "At least / more than" → solve the inequality, then cut it to the domain.「至少／超过」→ 解不等式，再截到定义域内。

💡 The formula h(t)=−5t2+20t happily gives you a height at t=−2 or t=10 — but the ball did not exist before it was thrown, and it stops at the ground. Real situations cut the graph down to the part that makes sense.公式 h(t)=−5t2+20t 在 t=−2 或 t=10 时也能算出高度——可球在抛出之前并不存在，落地后也不会钻进地里。实际问题只保留图象中有意义的那一段。

  1. Domain in context: which input values are physically possible? Time ≥0 and stops when the object lands; lengths >0; counts of people or items are whole numbers.实际问题的定义域：哪些输入真的可能？时间 ≥0，物体落地后停止；长度 >0；人数、件数只能是整数。
  2. Range in context: the outputs you get from that domain. For a parabola that opens down, the top of the range is the vertex value; the bottom comes from an endpoint of the domain.实际问题的值域：由这个定义域得到的输出。开口向下的抛物线，值域上端是顶点值，下端由定义域的端点决定。
  3. "When is it at least / more than…?" is a quadratic inequality. Solve it, then keep only the part inside the context domain.「什么时候至少/超过……？」就是二次不等式。解出来以后，只保留落在实际定义域里的部分。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    What does the input stand for (time, a width, a number of price increases)? Write its limits first: time starts at 0 and stops when the object lands, every length is more than 0, counts are whole numbers.输入量代表什么（时间、宽度、涨价次数）？先写出它的限制：时间从 0 开始、落地就停；每条长度都大于 0；个数只能是整数。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    The domain, the range, or a window ("at least", "higher than")? Answer as an inequality with units.求定义域、值域，还是一个时间段（「至少」「高于」）？用带单位的不等式作答。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Sketch only the part of the arch that is real: the start point, the vertex and the end point. For a window, draw the horizontal line y= target.只画拱形中真实存在的那一段：起点、顶点、终点。求时间段时，再画一条水平线 y= 目标值。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Domain: solve "h=0" or "each length >0". Range: the vertex value and the values at the ends of the domain. Window: move everything to one side, factor, use a sign chart (flip the sign if you divide by a negative).定义域：解「h=0」或「每条长度 >0」。值域：顶点值和定义域两端的值。时间段：移到一边、分解、列符号表（除以负数要变号）。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Every answer must sit inside the domain. Test one value from your answer in the original model.每个答案都要落在定义域里。从答案里取一个值，代回原模型检验。

2Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和） (Grade年级 10)

4Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘
Evaluate −5t2+20t when t=−1.当 t=−1 时，求 −5t2+20t 的值。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

① Worked example — read it完整例题——看懂
A ball is kicked up from the ground: h(t)=−5t2+30t (h in metres, t in seconds). State the domain and the range.球从地面被踢起：h(t)=−5t2+30t（h 单位为米，t 单位为秒）。写出定义域和值域。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Time starts at t=0 and the model stops when the ball lands (h=0).时间从 t=0 开始，球落地（h=0）时模型结束。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    The domain (times) and the range (heights), with units.求定义域（时间）和值域（高度），带单位。
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    −5t(t−6)=0 gives t=0 or t=6. Domain: 0≤t≤6 s.−5t(t−6)=0，得 t=0 或 t=6。定义域：0≤t≤6 秒。
  4. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    The arch starts at (0,0), ends at (6,0), and its top is halfway, at t=3.拱形从 (0,0) 开始，到 (6,0) 结束，最高点在正中间 t=3。
  5. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    h(3)=−45+90=45. Lowest value is 0 at the ends. Range: 0≤h≤45 m.h(3)=−45+90=45。两端最低是 0。值域：0≤h≤45 米。
  6. ✅ Check检验
    Test t=1: h=25, which lies between 0 and 45 ✓.代 t=1：h=25，在 0 和 45 之间 ✓。
✔
Domain 0≤t≤6 s; range 0≤h≤45 m定义域 0≤t≤6 秒；值域 0≤h≤45 米
② Same idea — you fill the gaps同一思路——你来补空
A stone is thrown from a 50 m cliff: h(t)=−5t2+15t+50. State the domain and the range.从 50 m 高的悬崖抛出一块石头：h(t)=−5t2+15t+50。写出定义域和值域。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Time starts at 0 and stops when the stone reaches the ground (h=0).时间从 0 开始，石头落地（h=0）时结束。
  2. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    ✍️
    Your turn: solve h=0 and keep only the time that makes sense.轮到你：解 h=0，只保留有意义的时间。
    −5(t2−3t−10)=0, (t−5)(t+2)=0: t=5 (reject t=−2). Domain: 0≤t≤5 s.−5(t2−3t−10)=0，(t−5)(t+2)=0：t=5（舍去 t=−2）。定义域：0≤t≤5 秒。
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Vertex at t=−2(−5)15​=1.5: h(1.5)=−11.25+22.5+50=61.25 m.顶点在 t=−2(−5)15​=1.5：h(1.5)=−11.25+22.5+50=61.25 米。
  4. ✅ Check检验
    ✍️
    Your turn: compare the heights at t=0, the vertex and t=5 to write the range.轮到你：比较 t=0、顶点和 t=5 处的高度，写出值域。
    h(0)=50, h(1.5)=61.25, h(5)=0. Range: 0≤h≤61.25 m (not 50≤h: the stone falls past its start).h(0)=50，h(1.5)=61.25，h(5)=0。值域：0≤h≤61.25 米（不是从 50 起：石头会落到起点以下）。

A flare is fired from a 45 m tower: h(t)=−5t2+40t+45. State the domain and the range.从 45 m 高的塔上发射一枚信号弹：h(t)=−5t2+40t+45。写出定义域和值域。
  1. 🔎 Given已知

    h(4)=−80+160+45=125; h(0)=45; h(9)=0.h(4)=−80+160+45=125；h(0)=45；h(9)=0。

A garden beside a wall is fenced on three sides with 40 m of fence. Its area is A(x)=x(40−2x), where x is the width (m). State the domain and range in context.靠墙的菜地三面用 40 m 篱笆围起来，面积 A(x)=x(40−2x)，x 为宽（米）。写出实际意义下的定义域和值域。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Width x>0 and length 40−2x>0⇒x<20. Domain: 0<x<20.宽 x>0，长 40−2x>0⇒x<20。定义域：0<x<20。
  2. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Zeros are 0 and 20, so the vertex is at x=10: A=10(20)=200.零点为 0 和 20，顶点在 x=10：A=10(20)=200。

    Range: 0<A≤200 (m²). The endpoints give area 0, which is excluded.值域：0<A≤200（m²）。端点处面积为 0，不包含。

A ball is thrown up from the ground: h(t)=−5t2+20t. (a) State the domain and range. (b) For how long is the ball higher than 15 m?球从地面竖直上抛：h(t)=−5t2+20t。(a) 写出定义域和值域；(b) 球高于 15 m 的时间有多长？
  1. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    (a) h=0 when −5t(t−4)=0: t=0 or 4. Domain 0≤t≤4. Vertex t=2, h=20. Range 0≤h≤20.(a) −5t(t−4)=0 时 h=0：t=0 或 4。定义域 0≤t≤4。顶点 t=2，h=20。值域 0≤h≤20。
  2. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    (b) −5t2+20t>15⇒−5t2+20t−15>0⇒t2−4t+3<0 (divided by −5, flipped).(b) −5t2+20t>15⇒−5t2+20t−15>0⇒t2−4t+3<0（除以 −5，变号）。
  3. ✅ Check检验
    (t−1)(t−3)<0: between the zeros, 1<t<3. That is inside the domain, so the ball is above 15 m for 2 s.(t−1)(t−3)<0：取两零点之间，1<t<3，在定义域内。球高于 15 m 的时间为 2 秒。

  2. ✅ Check检验

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

Someone made each of these. Tap the line where it first goes wrong.每道题都有人这样做过。点出第一处出错的那一行。

1Writing all real numbers for a word problem应用题定义域写成全体实数
A rectangle has perimeter 20 m and width x m, so A=x(10−x). State the domain and range.长方形周长 20 m，宽 x m，所以 A=x(10−x)。写出定义域和值域。

🧠 The formula works for any x, but a rectangle does not: a width of −3 or 12 m makes a side negative.公式对任何 x 都能算，可长方形不行：宽为 −3 或 12 米时，总有一条边是负的。

🔧 Width x>0 and length 10−x>0: domain 0<x<10. Vertex x=5 gives A=25, so the range is 0<A≤25 m².宽 x>0，长 10−x>0：定义域 0<x<10。顶点 x=5 时 A=25，值域 0<A≤25 m²。

✔ 0<x<10; 0<A≤250<x<10；0<A≤25

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 A

2Keeping the negative time留下了负的时间
A ball's height is h(t)=−5t2+15t+20. State the domain.球的高度 h(t)=−5t2+15t+20。写出定义域。

🧠 The algebra is right, but the ball does not exist before it is thrown. t=−1 is a zero of the formula, not a moment in the story.代数没错，但球在抛出之前并不存在。t=−1 只是公式的零点，不是故事里的时刻。

🔧 Reject t=−1. Domain: 0≤t≤4 s.舍去 t=−1。定义域：0≤t≤4 秒。

✔ 0≤t≤4

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Practice练习 1

3Taking the range from the endpoints only只用端点求值域
h(t)=−5t2+20t+25 for 0≤t≤5. Find the range.h(t)=−5t2+20t+25，0≤t≤5。求值域。

🧠 An arch is highest in the middle, not at an end. The vertex was never checked.拱形最高点在中间，不在两端。顶点根本没算。

🔧 Vertex t=2: h(2)=−20+40+25=45. Range: 0≤h≤45.顶点 t=2：h(2)=−20+40+25=45。值域：0≤h≤45。

✔ 0≤h≤45

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 B

4Using the starting height as the lowest value把起始高度当最小值
A ball is thrown from a 40 m roof: h(t)=−5t2+10t+40. Find the range.从 40 m 高的屋顶抛出一个球：h(t)=−5t2+10t+40。求值域。

🧠 The ball does not stop at roof height. It falls past the roof all the way to the ground.球不会停在屋顶高度，它会越过屋顶一直落到地面。

🔧 h=0 when t2−2t−8=0, so t=4. The lowest height is 0, so the range is 0≤h≤45 m.t2−2t−8=0 时 h=0，即 t=4。最低高度是 0，所以值域是 0≤h≤45 米。

✔ 0≤h≤45

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 D

5Not flipping the sign when dividing by a negative除以负数时没变号
h(t)=−5t2+30t. When is the ball higher than 25 m?h(t)=−5t2+30t。球什么时候高于 25 m？

🧠 Dividing both sides by a negative number reverses the inequality. The answer "t<1 or t>5" also says the ball is highest at the start and the end, which is backwards.两边同除以负数，不等号要反向。「t<1 或 t>5」等于说球在开头和结尾最高，正好反了。

🔧 t2−6t+5<0, so 1<t<5. This is inside the domain 0≤t≤6: above 25 m for 4 s.t2−6t+5<0，所以 1<t<5。它在定义域 0≤t≤6 内：高于 25 m 的时间共 4 秒。

✔ 1<t<5 (4 s)1<t<5（4 秒）

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Giving "all real numbers" as the domain of a word problem. Ask what values are physically possible.应用题的定义域写成「全体实数」。要问哪些值实际上可能出现。
  • Stopping at the inequality answer without checking it against the domain — a negative time in your interval must be cut off.解出不等式就停了，没和定义域对照——区间里出现负的时间要截掉。
  • Taking the range from the endpoints only. For an arch, the highest value is the vertex, which is usually in the middle.只用端点求值域。拱形的最大值在顶点，通常在中间。
