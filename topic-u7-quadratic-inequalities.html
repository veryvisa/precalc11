📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Everything to one side, find the zeros, then decide the sign between and outside them.移到一边，求零点，再判断零点之间和两侧的符号。
  • Opens up: negative BETWEEN the zeros, positive OUTSIDE.开口向上：零点之间为负，两侧为正。
  • ≤,≥ include the zeros; outside answers are joined by "or" (∪).≤,≥ 包括零点；两侧的答案用「或」(∪) 连接。

💡 For which times is the ball higher than 15 m? For which prices does the business make a profit? Both are "where is the parabola above (or below) a line" — a quadratic inequality.球在哪段时间高于 15 m？票价在什么范围内能盈利？两者都是在问「抛物线什么时候在某条线的上方（或下方）」——这就是二次不等式。

  1. Step 1: move everything to one side so you compare with 0. Step 2: find the zeros (factor or formula). They split the number line into intervals.第一步：全部移到一边，和 0 比较。第二步：求零点（分解或公式），零点把数轴分成几段。
  2. Step 3: find the sign on each interval — test one number in each (sign chart), or picture the parabola: opening up, it is negative BETWEEN the zeros and positive OUTSIDE them.第三步：判断每一段的符号——每段代一个数（符号表），或者想象抛物线：开口向上时，两零点之间为负，两侧为正。
  3. Step 4: pick the intervals that match the sign asked for. Include the zeros for ≤ or ≥, exclude them for < or >.第四步：选出符号符合要求的区间。≤、≥ 包含零点，<、> 不包含。
  4. No real zeros (D<0)? Then the expression has one sign everywhere: the answer is all real numbers or no solution.没有实数零点（D<0）？那么式子在整条数轴上同号，答案要么是全体实数，要么无解。

🧮 Key formulas关键公式

a(x−r)(x−s)<0, a>0, r<s ⇒ r<x<s
Opens up: negative between the zeros开口向上：两零点之间为负
a(x−r)(x−s)>0, a>0 ⇒ x<r or x>s
Opens up: positive outside the zeros开口向上：两零点外侧为正
🎯 Test tip考试提示
A 5-second sketch of the parabola (which way it opens + where it crosses) answers almost every quadratic inequality. Use the sign chart when the question says "show your work" or the expression has more than two factors.花 5 秒画个抛物线草图（开口方向 + 与 x 轴交点），几乎所有二次不等式都能解决。题目要求「写出过程」或因式不止两个时，再用符号表。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Is one side 0? If not, move everything over first. Note the sign (<,≤,>,≥) and whether the x2 coefficient is positive or negative.有一边是 0 吗？不是就先全部移过去。记下不等号（<,≤,>,≥），以及 x2 的系数是正还是负。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Every x that makes it true. Usually one interval or two pieces joined by "or"; sometimes all real numbers, one point, or nothing.使它成立的所有 x。通常是一个区间，或用「或」连接的两段；有时是全体实数、一个点，或无解。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    A tiny parabola through the zeros (up if a>0, down if a<0), or a number line with the zeros and +/− in each region.过零点画个小抛物线（a>0 开口向上，a<0 向下），或者画一条数轴，标出零点和每一段的 +/−。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Factor (or use the formula) to get the zeros. Opens up: <0 BETWEEN the zeros, >0 OUTSIDE. Opens down: the other way round.分解（或用公式）求零点。开口向上：<0 在零点「之间」，>0 在零点「两侧」。开口向下：反过来。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Test one number inside your answer and one outside in the ORIGINAL inequality. Square brackets only for ≤ or ≥.在「原」不等式里代一个答案内的数和一个答案外的数。只有 ≤、≥ 才用方括号。

3Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘
Is (x−3)(x+1) positive or negative when x=0?当 x=0 时，(x−3)(x+1) 是正还是负？

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

Someone made each of these. Tap the line where it first goes wrong.每道题都有人这样做过。点出第一处出错的那一行。

1Picking the wrong region区间选反了
Solve x2−4x−21>0.解 x2−4x−21>0。

🧠 The answer "between the zeros" feels natural because the zeros are what you just found. But an upward parabola is ABOVE the axis outside its zeros.刚求出零点，「取中间」感觉最自然。但开口向上的抛物线在零点两侧才位于 x 轴上方。

🔧 Sketch or test x=0: −21>0 ✗, so the middle is wrong. Answer: x<−3 or x>7.画草图或代 x=0：−21>0 ✗，所以中间不对。答案：x<−3 或 x>7。

✔ (−∞,−3)∪(7,∞)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 B

2Square-rooting an inequality对不等式直接开方
Solve x2≥25.解 x2≥25。

🧠 It copies the equation habit x2=25⇒x=±5. With ≥, the −5 side runs the other way: x=−10 works but is not ≥−5.照搬了方程的习惯 x2=25⇒x=±5。换成 ≥ 时，−5 这一边方向相反：x=−10 满足，却不 ≥−5。

🔧 x2−25≥0, (x−5)(x+5)≥0, opens up, outside: x≤−5 or x≥5.x2−25≥0，(x−5)(x+5)≥0，开口向上，取两侧：x≤−5 或 x≥5。

✔ (−∞,−5]∪[5,∞)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Practice练习 1

3Factoring before comparing with zero没和 0 比较就分解
Solve x2+2x<24.解 x2+2x<24。

🧠 The sign rules for a product only work against 0. Knowing x(x+2) is below 24 says nothing about each factor.乘积的符号规律只在和 0 比较时才成立。知道 x(x+2)<24，并不能推出每个因式的情况。

🔧 x2+2x−24<0, (x+6)(x−4)<0, opens up, between: −6<x<4.x2+2x−24<0，(x+6)(x−4)<0，开口向上，取中间：−6<x<4。

✔ (−6,4)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Practice练习 3

4Multiplying by negative one without flipping乘以负一却没变号
Solve −x2+x+12>0.解 −x2+x+12>0。

🧠 Making a positive is a good move, but it changes the direction of every inequality, the same rule as −2x>6⇒x<−3.把 a 变正是好办法，但乘负数会让不等号反向，和 −2x>6⇒x<−3 是同一条规则。

🔧 x2−x−12<0, so between the zeros: −3<x<4. (Or keep the original: it opens down and is positive between its zeros.)x2−x−12<0，取零点之间：−3<x<4。（或者不乘：原式开口向下，在零点之间为正。）

✔ (−3,4)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Practice练习 4

5Wrong brackets at the zeros零点处括号用错
Solve x2−2x−8≥0.解 x2−2x−8≥0。

🧠 The zeros are the points where the expression equals 0, and ≥ asks for "0 or more", so they belong in the answer.零点处式子正好等于 0，而 ≥ 要的是「大于或等于 0」，所以零点要包含。

🔧 Square brackets at both zeros: (−∞,−2]∪[4,∞). Check x=4: 16−8−8=0≥0 ✓.两个零点都用方括号：(−∞,−2]∪[4,∞)。验算 x=4：16−8−8=0≥0 ✓。

✔ (−∞,−2]∪[4,∞)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 D, Check-up测评 Q2 A

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Solving x2<9 as x<±3. Square roots do not work on inequalities; find zeros ±3, then −3<x<3.把 x2<9 解成 x<±3。不等式不能直接开方；先求零点 ±3，得 −3<x<3。
  • Forgetting to flip after multiplying by −1 to make a positive. Or skip that step and use the graph of the original.为了让 a 为正乘了 −1，却忘了变号。也可以不乘，直接看原式的图象。
  • Not moving everything to one side first: x2+2x≥15 must become x2+2x−15≥0 before factoring.没先移项：x2+2x≥15 要先变成 x2+2x−15≥0 才能分解。
  • Writing "x≤−3 and x≥21​". No number does both; the word is "or" (union ∪).写成「x≤−3 且 x≥21​」。没有数能同时满足；应该是「或」（并集 ∪）。

A minor-hockey club runs a tournament. At a ticket price of x dollars it expects 400−20x spectators (for 0≤x≤20). The hall costs 600 dollars plus 2 dollars per spectator. (a) Show that the profit is P(x)=−20x2+440x−1400. (b) For which ticket prices is the profit at least 1000 dollars? (c) The club hopes for 1100 dollars. Is that possible? Justify.一家少年冰球俱乐部办赛事。票价为 x 加元时预计有 400−20x 名观众（0≤x≤20）。场地费 600 加元，另外每位观众 2 加元。(a) 证明利润 P(x)=−20x2+440x−1400；(b) 票价在什么范围时利润至少 1000 加元？(c) 俱乐部希望赚 1100 加元，可能吗？说明理由。
Profit = revenue - cost, and cost depends on the number of spectators.利润 = 收入 - 成本，成本与观众人数有关。
(b) 10≤x≤12 dollars. (c) No: the maximum profit is 1020 dollars (at x=11), which is less than 1100.(b) 票价 10≤x≤12 加元。(c) 不可能：最大利润是 1020 加元（x=11 时），低于 1100。
  1. Revenue x(400−20x); cost 600+2(400−20x)=1400−40x. So P=400x−20x2−1400+40x=−20x2+440x−1400.收入 x(400−20x)；成本 600+2(400−20x)=1400−40x。所以 P=400x−20x2−1400+40x=−20x2+440x−1400。
  2. P≥1000⇒−20x2+440x−2400≥0⇒x2−22x+120≤0⇒(x−10)(x−12)≤0.P≥1000⇒−20x2+440x−2400≥0⇒x2−22x+120≤0⇒(x−10)(x−12)≤0。
  3. Upward parabola, between the zeros: 10≤x≤12, inside the domain 0≤x≤20.开口向上，取两零点之间：10≤x≤12，在定义域 0≤x≤20 内。
  4. Vertex at x=11: P(11)=−2420+4840−1400=1020. Since 1020<1100, 1100 dollars is impossible.顶点在 x=11：P(11)=−2420+4840−1400=1020。因为 1020<1100，赚 1100 加元不可能。
