📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Restrictions → ISOLATE the radical → raise to the index → solve → CHECK in the original.写限制条件 → 孤立根式 → 两边乘方 → 解方程 → 代回原方程检验。
  • Squaring can create extraneous roots: reject any root that fails the original.平方可能产生增根：不满足原方程的根要舍去。
  • …​≥0: so x​=−4 has no solution, and …​=x−3 needs x≥3.…​≥0：所以 x​=−4 无解，…​=x−3 要求 x≥3。

💡 Solve x+3​=x−3 by squaring and you get x=1 and x=6 — but plug in x=1 and you get 2=−2. Squaring can invent answers; this topic shows you how to catch them.两边平方解 x+3​=x−3，得到 x=1 和 x=6——可把 x=1 代回去却得 2=−2。平方会「造出」假答案，这一节教你怎样揪出来。

  1. Four steps: (1) state restrictions (radicand ≥0 for even index); (2) ISOLATE the radical; (3) raise both sides to the index (square, cube…); (4) solve and CHECK every answer in the ORIGINAL equation.四步：(1) 写限制条件（偶次根号内 ≥0）；(2) 把根式单独移到一边；(3) 两边同时乘方（平方、立方……）；(4) 解出后把每个答案代回原方程检验。
  2. Squaring loses the sign: 2=−2 is false, but 22=(−2)2 is true. A root that solves the squared equation but not the original is an extraneous root — reject it.平方会丢掉符号：2=−2 不成立，但 22=(−2)2 成立。满足平方后方程、却不满足原方程的根叫增根，要舍去。
  3. A principal square root is never negative. So …​=x−3 also needs x−3≥0, and x​=−4 has no solution at all.算术平方根永远非负。所以 …​=x−3 还要求 x−3≥0，而 x​=−4 根本无解。
  4. Graphically, the solutions are the x-coordinates where y= left side and y= right side intersect. Extraneous roots simply do not show up as intersections.从图象看，解就是 y=左边 与 y=右边 两个图象交点的横坐标。增根在图上不会出现交点。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    What is under the root, and what is on the other side? Write the restriction: inside ≥0, and if the other side has x, it must be ≥0 too.根号里是什么？另一边是什么？写出限制条件：根号内 ≥0；另一边含 x 时它也必须 ≥0。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Every x that makes the ORIGINAL equation true. There may be two, one or none.让原方程成立的所有 x——可能有两个、一个，或者没有。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Two graphs, y= left side and y= right side; answers are where they meet. A root curve can never meet a negative number.两个图象：y=左边、y=右边，答案就是交点。根式曲线永远碰不到负数。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Isolate the radical, square BOTH WHOLE sides, then solve the linear or quadratic equation you get.先孤立根式，再把两边「整体」平方，然后解得到的一次或二次方程。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Put every answer back into the ORIGINAL equation. Mark ✓ or ✗; reject the ✗ ones (extraneous).把每个答案代回原方程，标 ✓ 或 ✗；✗ 的是增根，舍去。

1Multiplying binomials, (a+b)2二项式相乘与 (a+b)2
Expand (x−3)2.展开 (x−3)2。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Multiplying binomials, (a+b)2二项式相乘与 (a+b)2 (Grade年级 9–10)
Every term times every term (FOIL): (x+3)(x−5)=x2−5x+3x−15=x2−2x−15. A square has a middle term: (x+3)2=x2+6x+9, never x2+9.每一项乘每一项：(x+3)(x−5)=x2−5x+3x−15=x2−2x−15。平方会有中间项：(x+3)2=x2+6x+9，绝不是 x2+9。
  1. (x−4)2=(x−4)(x−4)=x2−4x−4x+16=x2−8x+16.(x−4)2=(x−4)(x−4)=x2−4x−4x+16=x2−8x+16。
  2. Shortcut: (a±b)2=a2±2ab+b2 — middle term =2× first × last.捷径：(a±b)2=a2±2ab+b2——中间项＝2×首项×末项。

2Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）
Solve x2−7x+10=0.解 x2−7x+10=0。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和） (Grade年级 10)
Take out a common factor first. Then find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b: x2−x−12 → −4 and 3 → (x−4)(x+3). Check by expanding.先提公因式。再找两个数，相乘等于 c、相加等于 b：x2−x−12 → −4 和 3 → (x−4)(x+3)。展开检验。
  1. x2+7x+10: 2×5=10, 2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5).x2+7x+10：2×5=10，2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5)。
  2. If c<0 the two numbers have different signs; if c>0 they share the sign of b.若 c<0，两个数异号；若 c>0，两个数与 b 同号。
Try one:试一道：
Factor x2−2x−15.分解 x2−2x−15。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Perfect squares and square roots完全平方数与平方根 (Grade年级 8–10)

  1. 72​: biggest square factor is 36 → 62​.72​：最大的平方因数是 36 → 62​。

4Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘
Evaluate x+5​−x when x=−1.当 x=−1 时，求 x+5​−x 的值。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。

🪜 Worked → fill in → your turn例题 → 填空 → 自己做

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

① Worked example — read it完整例题——看懂
Solve x+2​=x−4.解 x+2​=x−4。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Under the root: x+2≥0. Other side: x−4≥0, so any answer must have x≥4.根号内：x+2≥0。另一边：x−4≥0，所以答案必须满足 x≥4。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Find every x that makes x+2​=x−4 true.求所有使 x+2​=x−4 成立的 x。
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    The radical is already alone. Square both sides: x+2=(x−4)2=x2−8x+16.根式已经单独在一边。两边平方：x+2=(x−4)2=x2−8x+16。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Make one side 0: x2−9x+14=0, so (x−2)(x−7)=0: x=2 or x=7.移成一边为 0：x2−9x+14=0，即 (x−2)(x−7)=0：x=2 或 x=7。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    x=7: 9​=3 and 7−4=3 ✓. x=2: 4​=2 but 2−4=−2 ✗ (it also breaks x≥4).x=7：9​=3，7−4=3 ✓。x=2：4​=2，而 2−4=−2 ✗（也不满足 x≥4）。

Example 3例 3L3 Connect会用
Solve 2x+4​−x=1. State the restriction and verify your answer.解 2x+4​−x=1，写出限制条件并检验。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Restriction: x+4≥0, so x≥−4.限制条件：x+4≥0，即 x≥−4。
  2. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Isolate: 2x+4​=x+1. (Do NOT square while −x is still on the left — you would get a messier equation that still has a root in it.)移项：2x+4​=x+1。（−x 还在左边时不要平方——那样平方后根号还在，更乱。）
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Square both sides, squaring the 2 as well: 4(x+4)=(x+1)2, so 4x+16=x2+2x+1.两边平方，系数 2 也要平方：4(x+4)=(x+1)2，即 4x+16=x2+2x+1。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

1Squaring before isolating没孤立就平方
Solve x+5​+1=x.解 x+5​+1=x。

🧠 You cannot square term by term. (x+5​+1)2 has a middle term 2x+5​.不能逐项平方。(x+5​+1)2 有中间项 2x+5​。

🔧 Isolate first: x+5​=x−1, then x+5=x2−2x+1, x2−3x−4=0, x=4 or −1; only x=4 checks.先孤立：x+5​=x−1，再 x+5=x2−2x+1，x2−3x−4=0，x=4 或 −1；只有 x=4 通过检验。

✔ x=4

2Keeping the extraneous root留下了增根
Solve 2x+6​=x+1.解 2x+6​=x+1。

🧠 Squaring can create a fake root. At x=−5​ the right side x+1 is negative, but a square root never is.平方会造出假根。x=−5​ 时右边 x+1 是负数，而平方根不会是负数。

🔧 Check both in the original: only x=5​ works.把两个都代回原方程：只有 x=5​ 成立。

✔ x=5​

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 A, Check-up测评 Q2 A

3Squaring a binomial without the middle term二项式平方漏了中间项
Solve x+4​=x−2.解 x+4​=x−2。

🧠 (x−2)2 is x2−4x+4, not x2+4.(x−2)2=x2−4x+4，不是 x2+4。

🔧 x+4=x2−4x+4, x2−5x=0, x=0 or 5; only x=5 checks.x+4=x2−4x+4，x2−5x=0，x=0 或 5；只有 x=5 通过检验。

✔ x=5

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 B

4Forgetting to square the coefficient忘了把系数平方
Solve 3x−1​=x+1.解 3x−1​=x+1。

🧠 (3x−1​)2=9(x−1): the 3 is squared too.(3x−1​)2=9(x−1)：系数 3 也要平方。

🔧 9x−9=x2+2x+1, x2−7x+10=0, x=2 or 5; both check (3=3, 6=6).9x−9=x2+2x+1，x2−7x+10=0，x=2 或 5；两个都通过检验（3=3，6=6）。

✔ x=2 or x=5x=2 或 x=5

5Solving "root = negative number"把「根式＝负数」硬解
Solve x−3​+5=2.解 x−3​+5=2。

🧠 A square root is never negative, so x−3​=−3 is impossible. Squaring hides that.平方根不会是负数，所以 x−3​=−3 不可能成立。平方把这一点掩盖了。

🔧 Stop at line 1: no solution. (Check: 9​+5=8=2.)在第 1 行就停：无解。（检验：9​+5=8=2。）

✔ No solution无解

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Squaring term by term: (x​+1)2 is x+2x​+1, not x+1. Isolate the radical BEFORE squaring so this never happens.逐项平方：(x​+1)2=x+2x​+1，不是 x+1。先把根式单独移到一边再平方，就不会出这种错。
  • Skipping the check. Every root from a squared equation must be substituted into the ORIGINAL equation; an extraneous root left in the answer usually costs the mark.不检验。平方后得到的每个根都必须代回原方程；答案里留着增根通常会扣分。
  • Forgetting to square the coefficient: 2x+4​ squared is 4(x+4), not 2(x+4).忘了把系数平方：2x+4​ 平方是 4(x+4)，不是 2(x+4)。
  • Solving x​=−4 to get x=16. A square root cannot equal a negative number: no solution.把 x​=−4 解成 x=16。算术平方根不可能等于负数：无解。

h=16 m; the tower sees 14.4 km and the higher platform (25 m) sees 18 km.h=16 m；瞭望塔看到 14.4 km，较高平台（25 m）看到 18 km。
  1. Model: 3.6h+9​=3.6h​+3.6, so h+9​=h​+1.建模：3.6h+9​=3.6h​+3.6，即 h+9​=h​+1。
  2. Square: h+9=h+2h​+1, so 2h​=8 and h​=4. Square again: h=16.平方：h+9=h+2h​+1，得 2h​=8，h​=4。再平方：h=16。
  3. Check: 25​=5=16​+1. Distances: 3.6×4=14.4 km and 3.6×5=18 km, a difference of 3.6 km.检验：25​=5=16​+1。距离：3.6×4=14.4 km，3.6×5=18 km，相差 3.6 km。

💡 Key idea解题思路
Isolate one radical at a time; every squaring is a chance to introduce a fake root, so check at the end.每次只孤立一个根式；每次平方都可能引入假根，所以最后必须检验。
