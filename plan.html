🧭 Seven habits for using time well用好时间的七个习惯

Plan by the clock, not by the to-do list按时间计划，不按清单计划

Decide how long you will work, then fit tasks inside. A list without times always looks too short.先定做多久，再往里装任务。没写时间的清单看起来永远很短。

Estimate, then measure先估计，再计时

Write your guess before each block. The planner compares it with the real time, and learns your personal speed.每段开始前写下估计，计划器会和实际用时对比，学会你自己的速度。

Do the one that unlocks others first先做能解锁别的事的那件

Factoring unlocks rational expressions and quadratic equations. The map shows these links.因式分解解锁有理式和二次方程。图谱上的连线就是这种关系。

One clean solution beats three rewrites一份干净的解答胜过重写三遍

When a block's time is up, stop at a good-enough point and write one line: where to restart.时间到了，就在「够好」的地方停下，写一行：下次从哪里接着做。

Stuck for 10 minutes? Switch, don't stew卡住 10 分钟？换一题，别硬磨

Open the hint, read one example, or mark it and move on. Bring marked questions to a teacher or friend.看提示、看一道例题，或者做个记号先跳过。把记号题带去问老师或同学。

Short and often wins少量多次最有效

25 focused minutes a day plus 5 minutes of cards beats a 3-hour weekend cram — and is easier to start.每天专注 25 分钟加 5 分钟卡片，胜过周末突击 3 小时，而且更容易开始。

Rest is part of the plan休息也是计划的一部分

A 5-minute break after each block — water, stretch, look outside. Sleep is when memory is saved.每段之后休息 5 分钟：喝水、伸展、看看窗外。睡觉的时候，记忆才被存下来。
