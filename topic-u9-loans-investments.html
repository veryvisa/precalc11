📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Saving with equal deposits → FV. Loan → amount borrowed is PV; solve for PMT.定期等额存款 → 求终值 FV。贷款 → 借款额是现值 PV，求每期还款 PMT。
  • i = rate per PAYMENT period, n = number of payments (monthly, 5 years → n=60).i＝每个还款期的利率，n＝还款次数（按月 5 年 → n=60）。
  • Interest = total paid − amount borrowed (or − total deposited).利息＝总付款 − 借款额（储蓄则 − 总存入）。

💡 Put $200 a month into a TFSA from age 18 and you have over $31000 at 28 — about $7000 of it is interest you never deposited. The same math tells you what a car loan or mortgage really costs.从 18 岁起每月往 TFSA（免税储蓄账户）存 $200，到 28 岁就超过 $31000——其中约 $7000 是你没存过的利息。同一套数学也告诉你车贷、房贷真正要花多少。

  1. An annuity is a series of equal, regular payments. Saving → you want the future value FV. Borrowing → the loan is the present value PV and you want the payment PMT.年金就是一串金额相等、定期支付的款项。存钱 → 求终值 FV；借钱 → 贷款额就是现值 PV，要求每期还款 PMT。
  2. In the formulas i is the interest rate per PAYMENT period and n is the number of payments. When payments and compounding match (e.g. both monthly), i=12r​.公式中 i 是每个「付款期」的利率，n 是付款次数。付款与计息频率相同时（如都按月），i=12r​。
  3. With technology (TVM Solver or an online TVM calculator), enter N, I%, PV, PMT, FV, P/Y, C/Y and solve for the blank. Money you pay out is negative, money you receive is positive.用技术工具（TVM Solver 或网上的 TVM 计算器）：输入 N、I%、PV、PMT、FV、P/Y、C/Y，求空着的那一项。付出的钱为负，收到的钱为正。
  4. Total interest = (payment × number of payments) − amount borrowed (or − total deposited, for savings).总利息＝每期金额×期数－借款额（储蓄则减去存入总额）。

🧮 Key formulas关键公式

FV=PMT⋅i(1+i)n−1​
Future value of regular deposits定期存款的终值
PV=PMT⋅i1−(1+i)−n​
Present value (loan amount) of regular payments定期付款的现值（贷款额）
PMT=1−(1+i)−nPV⋅i​
Loan payment贷款每期还款额
i=(1+2r​)2/12−1
Canadian mortgage: compounded semi-annually, paid monthly加拿大房贷：半年复利、按月还款时的月利率

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    List the numbers in the words: the loan or savings amount, the payment, the yearly rate, how often interest is compounded (C/Y), how often you pay (P/Y), and how many years.把题目里的数列出来：贷款额或储蓄额、每期付款、年利率、每年计息几次（C/Y）、每年付款几次（P/Y）、多少年。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Which one is blank: the payment PMT, the loan size PV, or the savings total FV? Does the question also want the total paid or the interest?空着的是哪一个：每期付款 PMT、贷款额 PV，还是储蓄终值 FV？题目是否还要还款总额或利息？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Draw a timeline: one arrow for each payment at the end of each period. Money today sits at the left (PV); money at the end sits at the right (FV).画一条时间线：每期末画一个付款箭头。今天的钱在左端（PV），最后的钱在右端（FV）。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    i = rate per PAYMENT period, n = number of payments. Saving: FV formula. Loan size: PV formula. Loan payment: PMT formula. Or fill the TVM list, money out negative.i＝每个「付款期」的利率，n＝付款次数。储蓄用 FV 公式；求能借多少用 PV 公式；求月供用 PMT 公式。也可以填 TVM 清单，付出的钱为负。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Payments times n must be MORE than the loan (you pay interest) and LESS than the savings total (interest is added). The difference is the interest.每期付款乘以 n：贷款题要「大于」贷款额（你付了利息），储蓄题要「小于」终值（利息加进来了）。两者的差就是利息。

1Using the yearly rate as the monthly rate把年利率当成月利率

2Counting years instead of payments数了年数而不是付款次数
You save $50 at the end of every month for 6 years at 3% compounded monthly. Find the future value.你每月月底存 $50，存 6 年，年利率 3% 按月复利。求终值。

🧠 "6 years" is the number in the question, so it becomes n. But n counts deposits, and there is one every month.题目里的数是「6 年」，于是就成了 n。可是 n 数的是存款次数，每月存一次。

3Calling the total paid the interest把还款总额当成利息

4Using r over 12 for a Canadian mortgage加拿大房贷直接用 r 除以 12

5Using the savings formula to find a loan求贷款额却用了储蓄公式
You can afford $400 a month for 5 years. At 6% compounded monthly, what is the largest loan you can take?你每月能还 $400，还 5 年。年利率 6% 按月复利，最多能借多少？

🧠 Both formulas look alike, and FV is the one used most. But a loan is money you get TODAY, at the left end of the timeline, so it is a present value.两个公式长得很像，FV 又最常用。可是贷款是你「今天」拿到的钱，在时间线左端，所以是现值。

🔧 PV=400⋅0.0051−1.005−60​≈$20690.22. (Sense check: you pay 400×60=$24000 in total, so the loan must be LESS than that.)PV=400⋅0.0051−1.005−60​≈$20690.22。（合理性：你一共还 400×60=$24000，所以贷款额一定「小于」它。）

✔ $20690.22

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Using the annual rate as i. For monthly payments at 6.9%, i=0.069÷12, and n counts months.把年利率直接当 i。按月还款、年利率 6.9% 时，i=0.069÷12，n 按月数计。
  • Sign errors on the TVM Solver: if PV and PMT are both positive (or both negative) for a loan, the solver fails or gives nonsense.TVM 符号错误：贷款题里 PV 和 PMT 同正或同负，计算器会报错或给出离谱结果。
  • Leaving C/Y =12 for a Canadian mortgage, which is compounded semi-annually (C/Y =2).加拿大房贷是半年复利（C/Y =2），却把 C/Y 留成 12。
  • Calling the total of payments "the interest". Interest is total paid MINUS the amount borrowed.把还款总额当成利息。利息是还款总额减去借款额。
