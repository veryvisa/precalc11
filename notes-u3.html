Greatest common factor & grouping提公因式与分组分解

  • Always look for a greatest common factor FIRST: the largest number that divides every coefficient, times each variable to its LOWEST power.永远先找最大公因式：能整除所有系数的最大数，乘以每个字母的最低次幂。
  • A common factor can be a whole bracket: 2x(x−3)+5(x−3)=(x−3)(2x+5). Treat (x−3) like a single letter.公因式也可以是整个括号：2x(x−3)+5(x−3)=(x−3)(2x+5)。把 (x−3) 当成一个字母看待。
  • Four terms → try grouping: pair them, take a GCF from each pair, and the brackets should match. If they are opposites like (x−2) and (2−x), factor out −1.四项式 → 试分组：两两分组，各提公因式，括号应该相同。如果是 (x−2) 与 (2−x) 这种相反式，提出 −1。
  • Check by expanding — it takes ten seconds and catches every missing term.展开验算——十秒钟就能抓出漏掉的项。
ab+ac=a(b+c)
Common factor (distributive law backwards)提公因式（分配律倒过来用）
ax+ay+bx+by=a(x+y)+b(x+y)=(x+y)(a+b)
Factoring by grouping分组分解
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Taking out a common factor that is not the GREATEST: 4x2+8x=2x(2x+4) is not finished — 4x(x+2) is.提出的不是最大公因式：4x2+8x=2x(2x+4) 还没分解完，应为 4x(x+2)。
  • Losing a term that equals the GCF: 3x2y divided by itself is 1, which must stay in the bracket.某项正好等于公因式时把它丢了：3x2y 除以自己得 1，括号里必须保留。
  • Sign slip when grouping: −9x−18=−9(x+2), not −9(x−2).分组时符号出错：−9x−18=−9(x+2)，不是 −9(x−2)。

🎯 Marks are lost most often for not factoring FULLY. After every step ask: is there still a GCF? Is any bracket a difference of squares or a factorable trinomial?最常见的失分是没有「完全」分解。每一步之后问自己：还有公因式吗？某个括号是不是平方差或还能分解的三项式？

Factoring trinomials ax²+bx+c三项式 ax²+bx+c 的分解

  • When a=1: find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b. x2−2x−15: −5⋅3=−15, −5+3=−2, so (x−5)(x+3).a=1 时：找两个数，积为 c、和为 b。x2−2x−15：−5⋅3=−15，−5+3=−2，所以 (x−5)(x+3)。
  • When a=1 (decomposition): find two numbers that multiply to a⋅c and add to b. Split the middle term with them, then factor by grouping.a=1 时（拆项法）：找两个数，积为 a⋅c、和为 b。用它们把中间项拆成两项，再分组分解。
  • Signs guide the search: c>0 → both numbers have the sign of b; c<0 → opposite signs, the bigger one has the sign of b.符号帮你缩小范围：c>0 → 两数同号，且与 b 同号；c<0 → 两数异号，绝对值大的与 b 同号。
  • Take out a GCF (and a negative leading coefficient) first. If no pair of integers works, the trinomial does not factor over the integers.先提公因式（首项为负也先提出负号）。如果找不到这样一对整数，这个三项式在整数范围内不能分解。
x2+(m+n)x+mn=(x+m)(x+n)
Trinomial with a=1首项系数为 1 的三项式
ax2+bx+c:  mn=ac,  m+n=b ⇒ ax2+mx+nx+c
Decomposition: split the middle term拆项法：把中间项拆开
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Using c instead of ac when a=1. For 6x2+7x−3 you need product −18, not −3.a=1 时用 c 而不是 ac。6x2+7x−3 要找积为 −18 的数，不是 −3。
  • Forgetting the GCF: 2x2+10x+12=(2x+4)(x+3) is not fully factored; it is 2(x+2)(x+3).忘了提公因式：2x2+10x+12=(2x+4)(x+3) 没分解完，应为 2(x+2)(x+3)。
  • Sign mix-up: x2−x−12=(x+3)(x−4), not (x−3)(x+4) — the middle term is −x. Expand the middle term to check.符号弄反：x2−x−12=(x+3)(x−4)，不是 (x−3)(x+4)——中间项是 −x。展开中间项检验。

🎯 Write the product–sum pair in the margin before you write any brackets, then expand the middle term to check. Most lost marks are sign errors that a 5-second check would catch.写括号之前先在旁边写出「积—和」那对数，写完再展开中间项检查。大多数失分都是符号错误，5 秒钟的检查就能发现。

Difference of squares & perfect squares平方差与完全平方

  • Difference of squares: two perfect squares with a MINUS between them. a2x2−b2y2=(ax−by)(ax+by).平方差：两个完全平方之间是减号。a2x2−b2y2=(ax−by)(ax+by)。
  • Perfect square trinomial: first and last terms are squares, and the middle is twice their product. 9x2−30x+25=(3x−5)2 because 2(3x)(5)=30x.完全平方式：首末两项是平方，中间项是它们乘积的两倍。9x2−30x+25=(3x−5)2，因为 2(3x)(5)=30x。
  • A SUM of squares like x2+25 does not factor over the real numbers.平方和（如 x2+25）在实数范围内不能分解。
  • GCF first, then keep going: x2−16 hiding inside x4−16 factors again.先提公因式，再继续分：x4−16 里藏着的 x2−4 还能再分解。
A2−B2=(A−B)(A+B)
Difference of squares平方差公式
A2±2AB+B2=(A±B)2
Perfect square trinomials完全平方公式
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Factoring a sum: x2+9=(x+3)(x+3) (that gives x2+6x+9). A sum of squares does not factor.把平方和也分解：x2+9=(x+3)(x+3)（那等于 x2+6x+9）。平方和不能分解。
  • Calling x2+10x+16 a perfect square because 16 is a square. The middle must be 2⋅x⋅4=8x; here it is 10x, so it is (x+2)(x+8).因为 16 是平方数就把 x2+10x+16 当完全平方。中间项须为 2⋅x⋅4=8x，这里是 10x，所以应为 (x+2)(x+8)。
  • Stopping too early: x4−81=(x2−9)(x2+9) still has x2−9=(x−3)(x+3) inside.分解得太早停：x4−81=(x2−9)(x2+9) 里的 x2−9=(x−3)(x+3) 还能分。

🎯 When a question says "factor fully", check every bracket once more for a difference of squares — x4 and 16 questions are designed to need two rounds.题目说「完全分解」时，把每个括号再检查一遍是否还有平方差——含 x4、16 的题就是故意设计要分两轮的。

Factoring by substitution换元法分解

  • An expression is in quadratic form if it looks like a(stuff)2+b(stuff)+c. Replace the repeated stuff with one letter, e.g. m.形如 a(某式)2+b(某式)+c 的式子叫二次型。把重复出现的那部分换成一个字母，比如 m。
  • Factor the simple trinomial in m, then substitute back and simplify each bracket.把关于 m 的三项式分解，再代回去并化简每个括号。
  • After substituting back, check again: brackets like x2−4 are often differences of squares.代回之后再检查：x2−4 这类括号常常还是平方差。
  • Differences of squares can have binomials as A and B: (2x−1)2−(x+3)2=[(2x−1)−(x+3)][(2x−1)+(x+3)].平方差里的 A、B 也可以是二项式：(2x−1)2−(x+3)2=[(2x−1)−(x+3)][(2x−1)+(x+3)]。
a[f(x)]2+bf(x)+c  m=f(x) ​ am2+bm+c
Substitution for quadratic form二次型的换元
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Leaving the answer in m. Always substitute back — the question is about x.答案停留在 m。一定要代回去——题目问的是 x。
  • Dropping brackets when subtracting: (2x−1)−(x+3) is x−4, not x+2.相减时丢了括号：(2x−1)−(x+3) 等于 x−4，不是 x+2。
  • Not simplifying after substituting back: ((x+2)−2) should be written as x.代回后不化简：((x+2)−2) 应写成 x。

🎯 Write "Let m=…" as your first line — it earns method marks even if you slip later, and makes the substitution back hard to forget.第一行写「设 m=…」——即使后面算错也能拿到方法分，而且不容易忘记代回。
