📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Spot a(stuff)2+b(stuff)+c; let m= the stuff.认出 a(整体)2+b(整体)+c；令 m= 这个整体。
  • Factor in m, substitute back, simplify each bracket.按 m 分解，代回，再化简每个括号。
  • Re-check every bracket: x2−4 and friends factor again.每个括号再查一遍：x2−4 这类还能继续分解。

💡 x4−5x2+4 looks like a degree-4 monster, but it is really m2−5m+4 wearing a disguise (m=x2). Swap, factor, swap back.x4−5x2+4 看起来是四次「怪物」，其实是换了装的 m2−5m+4（m=x2）。换元、分解、再换回来。

  1. An expression is in quadratic form if it looks like a(stuff)2+b(stuff)+c. Replace the repeated stuff with one letter, e.g. m.形如 a(某式)2+b(某式)+c 的式子叫二次型。把重复出现的那部分换成一个字母，比如 m。
  2. Factor the simple trinomial in m, then substitute back and simplify each bracket.把关于 m 的三项式分解，再代回去并化简每个括号。
  3. After substituting back, check again: brackets like x2−4 are often differences of squares.代回之后再检查：x2−4 这类括号常常还是平方差。
  4. Differences of squares can have binomials as A and B: (2x−1)2−(x+3)2=[(2x−1)−(x+3)][(2x−1)+(x+3)].平方差里的 A、B 也可以是二项式：(2x−1)2−(x+3)2=[(2x−1)−(x+3)][(2x−1)+(x+3)]。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Look for ONE chunk that shows up twice: once squared, once to the first power (x4 and x2, or (x+2)2 and (x+2)). Write "Let m=" that chunk.找出出现了两次的「同一块」：一次是平方，一次是一次方（x4 和 x2，或 (x+2)2 和 (x+2)）。写下「设 m=」这一块。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    A full factorization in x, not in m, where no bracket can be factored again.用 x 写的完全分解（不是用 m），每个括号都不能再分解。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Rewrite the whole expression with m, e.g. m2−5m+4. It should now look like an ordinary trinomial you already know how to factor.用 m 把整个式子重写一遍，例如 m2−5m+4。它应该变成一个你已经会分解的普通三项式。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Factor in m (product and sum, or decomposition when a=1). Then put the chunk back in place of m and simplify each bracket.按 m 分解（积与和；a=1 时用拆项法）。再把这一块代回 m 的位置，化简每个括号。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Look at each bracket again: is it a difference of squares or does it have a common factor? Then put x=1 (or 2) into the original and into your answer.再看一遍每个括号：是不是平方差？有没有公因数？然后把 x=1（或 2）分别代入原式和答案。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号） (Grade年级 8–9)

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

Someone made each of these. Tap the line where it first goes wrong.每道题都有人这样做过。点出第一处出错的那一行。

1Leaving the answer in m答案停在 m 上
Factor fully: x4−6x2+5.完全分解：x4−6x2+5。

🧠 The factoring felt like the finish line. But m was only a helper letter; the question is about x.分解完就觉得到终点了。可 m 只是临时的帮手，题目问的是 x。

🔧 Put x2 back: (x2−1)(x2−5)=(x−1)(x+1)(x2−5). (x2−5 does not factor over the integers.)把 x2 代回：(x2−1)(x2−5)=(x−1)(x+1)(x2−5)。（x2−5 在整数范围内不能再分。）

✔ (x−1)(x+1)(x2−5)

2Stopping before the difference of squares没分解平方差就停了
Factor fully: x4−20x2+64.完全分解：x4−20x2+64。

🧠 After the substitution the work looks finished. But "fully" means checking every bracket again, and x2−4, x2−16 are both differences of squares.换元做完，看起来就结束了。可是「完全」分解要求每个括号再查一遍，x2−4 和 x2−16 都是平方差。

🔧 (x−2)(x+2)(x−4)(x+4).(x−2)(x+2)(x−4)(x+4)。

✔ (x−2)(x+2)(x−4)(x+4)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 B

3Putting x back instead of the whole chunk代回时只写了 x，没代回整块
Factor (x−1)2+3(x−1)−10.分解因式 (x−1)2+3(x−1)−10。

🧠 In the quartic questions m was "x-something" and it is easy to just write x. Here m stands for the whole x−1.做四次式时习惯把 m 当成「和 x 差不多」，顺手就写成了 x。这里的 m 代表整个 x−1。

🔧 ((x−1)+5)((x−1)−2)=(x+4)(x−3).((x−1)+5)((x−1)−2)=(x+4)(x−3)。

✔ (x+4)(x−3)

4Dropping the bracket when working out A minus B算 A−B 时丢了括号

🧠 The minus sign was given to x only. B is the whole (x+5), so the minus hits the 5 too.减号只给了 x。B 是整个 (x+5)，所以减号也要作用到 5。

5Distributing only part of the way when substituting back代回时分配律只做了一半
Factor 3(x−2)2+7(x−2)+2.分解因式 3(x−2)2+7(x−2)+2。

🧠 3m became 3x−2 because the bracket was not written. 3m=3(x−2)=3x−6.没写括号，3m 就成了 3x−2。其实 3m=3(x−2)=3x−6。

🔧 (3(x−2)+1)((x−2)+2)=(3x−5)x, so x(3x−5).(3(x−2)+1)((x−2)+2)=(3x−5)x，即 x(3x−5)。

✔ x(3x−5)

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Leaving the answer in m. Always substitute back — the question is about x.答案停留在 m。一定要代回去——题目问的是 x。
  • Dropping brackets when subtracting: (2x−1)−(x+3) is x−4, not x+2.相减时丢了括号：(2x−1)−(x+3) 等于 x−4，不是 x+2。
  • Not simplifying after substituting back: ((x+2)−2) should be written as x.代回后不化简：((x+2)−2) 应写成 x。

💡 Key idea解题思路
Let m=x2 to reduce to an ordinary trinomial; both resulting factors are differences of squares.令 m=x2 化成普通三项式；得到的两个因式都是平方差。
🔀 Other ways to think不同的思考方式
  • Substitute m=x2, factor, substitute back.换元 m=x2，分解，再代回。
  • Spot the product-sum pair 4 and 9 directly on x2 without writing m.不写 m，直接在 x2 上找积和对 4 和 9。
  • Test values: A(2)=A(3)=0 shows (x−2) and (x−3) are factors.代入试值：A(2)=A(3)=0，说明 (x−2)、(x−3) 是因式。
