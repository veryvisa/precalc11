🧭 Four ideas about time关于时间的四个想法

Planning fallacy计划谬误

Almost everyone guesses too short, even for things they have done before. It is not a flaw in you — it is how brains plan. The fix is not trying harder: write a guess, time it, compare. After a few rounds your guesses get closer by themselves.几乎每个人都会把时间估短，连做过的事也一样。这不是你的问题，是大脑做计划的方式。办法不是更用力，而是：写下估计、计时、对比。几轮之后，估计会自己变准。

Good enough for this course对这门课来说「够了」

Each topic page has an "Enough when you can…" bar. That is the course level. Going further is fine — later, on purpose — not because you feel you must know everything before moving on.每个知识点页都有一条「能做到这些就够了」。那就是课程要求。想学得更深可以——以后、有意识地去学——而不是因为觉得非得什么都懂才能往前走。

Park it, don't chase it先存起来，别追下去

A curious question in the middle of a topic is a good sign. Write it on the Park-it list in one line; it will still be there after the test.学到一半冒出好奇的问题，是好事。在「先存起来」清单上写一行，考完试它还在那儿。

Check, then calibrate先检查，再校准

When the timer reaches your estimate, stop for ten seconds and look at the bar. Reached it? Move on. Not yet? Choose: five more minutes, or a mark and ask the teacher.计时到了估计的时间，停下来十秒，看看那条「够了」。做到了？往下走。还没有？选一个：再给五分钟，或者做个记号去问老师。
