📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • A=P(1+nr​)nt: r as a decimal, n = periods per year, exponent nt.A=P(1+nr​)nt：r 用小数，n 是每年计息次数，指数是 nt。
  • Per period i=nr​; periods =nt (monthly for 6 years = 72).每期利率 i=nr​；期数 =nt（按月 6 年＝72 期）。
  • Rule of 72: doubling time ≈rate in %72​ years — an estimate only.72 法则：翻倍时间 ≈年利率（%）72​ 年——只是估算。

💡 One bank offers 4.5% compounded annually, another 4.4% compounded monthly. Which pays more on $8000 over 5 years? The answer is not obvious — and the formula settles it in two lines.一家银行年利率 4.5%、按年复利，另一家 4.4%、按月复利。8000 加元存 5 年，哪家给得多？答案并不显然——公式两行就能算清。

  1. Compound interest earns interest on interest. Each period the balance is multiplied by (1+i), where i=nr​ is the rate per compounding period.复利就是「利滚利」。每个计息期余额乘以 (1+i)，其中 i=nr​ 是每期利率。
  2. In A=P(1+nr​)nt: P = principal, r = annual rate as a decimal, n = compounding periods per year (annually 1, semi-annually 2, quarterly 4, monthly 12, daily 365), t = years.在 A=P(1+nr​)nt 中：P＝本金，r＝年利率（小数），n＝每年计息次数（按年 1、半年 2、季度 4、按月 12、按日 365），t＝年数。
  3. Same annual rate, more frequent compounding → slightly more money. But a higher rate usually beats more frequent compounding — always compute both.年利率相同时，计息越频繁，钱越多一点。但更高的利率通常胜过更频繁的计息——两个都要算。
  4. Rule of 72: money doubles in about rate in %72​ years. It is a mental estimate, not an exact answer.72 法则：资金大约 利率（百分数）72​ 年翻一番。这是心算估计，不是精确答案。

A=P(1+nr​)nt

🎯 Test tip考试提示
Write P, r, n, t (and i, nt) as a list before substituting — most lost marks are a wrong n or nt. Round only the final dollar answer, to the cent.代入前先列出 P、r、n、t（以及 i、nt）——大部分失分是 n 或 nt 用错。只在最后把金额四舍五入到分。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    List the numbers: principal P, yearly rate r as a DECIMAL, times per year it compounds n, and years t. Read the words for n: annually 1, semi-annually 2, quarterly 4, monthly 12.列出数字：本金 P、年利率 r（写成「小数」）、每年计息次数 n、年数 t。从文字里读出 n：按年 1，半年 2，按季 4，按月 12。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Is the unknown the final amount A, the interest A−P, or the amount to invest now, P? Money is rounded to the cent, at the very end.要求的是终值 A、利息 A−P，还是现在要投入的 P？金额四舍五入到分，而且只在最后一步。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    A growth table: each period the balance is multiplied by (1+i). After N periods it has been multiplied N times.一张增长表：每过一期，余额乘一次 (1+i)。过了 N 期就乘了 N 次。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    i=nr​ and N=nt, then A=P(1+i)N typed in ONE line with brackets. To find P, divide A by (1+i)N. ("Compounded semi-annually", the usual way a Canadian fixed-rate mortgage is quoted, means n=2.)i=nr​，N=nt，再把 A=P(1+i)N 带括号「一行」输入计算器。求 P 就用 A 除以 (1+i)N。（「半年复利」——加拿大固定利率房贷通常这样标——就是 n=2。）
  5. ✅ Check检验
    A must be bigger than P, and a little bigger than simple interest P(1+rt). The Rule of 72 gives a rough size.A 一定比 P 大，而且比单利 P(1+rt) 稍大一点。72 法则可以估个大概。

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

Someone made each of these. Tap the line where it first goes wrong.每道题都有人这样做过。点出第一处出错的那一行。

1Using the percent as a whole number把百分数当整数用
$1000 is invested at 4% compounded annually for 3 years. Find the amount.$1000 以 4%、按年复利投资 3 年。求终值。

🧠 4% means 4 out of 100, so r=0.04. With r=4 the money would grow 400% a year.4% 是百分之四，r=0.04。用 r=4 等于每年涨 400%。

🔧 A=1000(1.04)3≈$1124.86.A=1000(1.04)3≈$1124.86。

✔ $1124.86

2Using years instead of periods as the exponent指数用了年数而不是期数
$2000 is invested at 6% compounded monthly for 4 years. Find the amount.$2000 以 6%、按月复利投资 4 年。求终值。

🧠 The balance is multiplied by 1.005 once a MONTH. Four years is 12×4=48 months, so the exponent is 48.余额每「月」乘一次 1.005。4 年是 12×4=48 个月，指数应是 48。

🔧 A=2000(1.005)48≈$2540.98.A=2000(1.005)48≈$2540.98。

✔ $2540.98

3Forgetting to divide the rate by n忘了把利率除以 n
$1500 is invested at 8% compounded quarterly for 3 years. Find the amount.$1500 以 8%、按季度复利投资 3 年。求终值。

🧠 8% is the rate for a whole YEAR. Each quarter earns only a quarter of it: i=0.08÷4=0.02.8% 是「一整年」的利率。每个季度只拿四分之一：i=0.08÷4=0.02。

🔧 A=1500(1.02)12≈$1902.36. (The wrong answer, $3777.26, more than doubles the money in 3 years, which should look suspicious.)A=1500(1.02)12≈$1902.36。（错误答案 3777.26 加元，3 年就让钱翻了一倍多，这本身就该起疑。）

✔ $1902.36

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 B, Check-up测评 Q2 D

4Working out simple interest instead算成了单利
$5000 is invested at 3% compounded annually for 4 years. Find the amount.$5000 以 3%、按年复利投资 4 年。求终值。

🧠 That gives interest on the first $5000 only. With compounding, each year's interest also earns interest.这样只算了最初 $5000 的利息。复利时，每年的利息也会再生利息。

🔧 A=5000(1.03)4≈$5627.54, about $27.54 more than simple interest.A=5000(1.03)4≈$5627.54，比单利多约 $27.54。

✔ $5627.54

5Rounding the rate per period too early每期利率舍入太早
$6000 is invested at 5% compounded monthly for 10 years. Find the amount.$6000 以 5%、按月复利投资 10 年。求终值。

🧠 0.05÷12=0.0041666… Cutting it to 0.004 looks tiny, but the error is raised to the power 120.0.05÷12=0.0041666…，舍成 0.004 看着差得不多，可这个误差被乘方了 120 次。

🔧 Type it in one line: 6000(1+0.05/12)120≈$9882.06, about $195 more.一行输入：6000(1+0.05/12)120≈$9882.06，多出约 $195。

✔ $9882.06

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Using the percent as a whole number: r=0.04, not 4.把百分数直接当整数用：r=0.04，不是 4。
  • Forgetting to multiply years by n: monthly for 6 years is 72 periods, not 6.忘了年数要乘 n：按月复利 6 年是 72 期，不是 6 期。
  • Rounding i=120.04​ to 0.0033 before raising it to a power — the answer can be off by dollars. Keep full precision until the last step.在乘方之前把 i=120.04​ 舍成 0.0033——结果可能差好几加元。最后一步才四舍五入。
  • Treating the Rule of 72 as exact. Say "about" and use the formula when the question asks for an exact or rounded value.把 72 法则当成精确值。要说「大约」；题目要精确或指定精度时用公式。
