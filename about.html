ℹ️ About this course关于课程

This site follows the BC Ministry of Education Pre-calculus 11 curriculum: real numbers and powers, radicals, polynomial factoring, rational expressions and equations, quadratic functions and equations, linear and quadratic inequalities, trigonometry (non-right triangles and angles in standard position), and financial literacy. Big ideas: algebra lets us generalize relationships; operations extend to powers, radicals and polynomials; quadratic relationships are everywhere; trigonometry uses proportional reasoning for indirect measurement.本站按照 BC 省教育部 Pre-calculus 11 课程大纲编写：实数与幂、根式、多项式因式分解、有理式与分式方程、二次函数与方程、一次与二次不等式、三角（非直角三角形与标准位置角）、金融素养。核心观念：代数让我们概括关系；运算延伸到幂、根式和多项式；二次关系无处不在；三角用比例推理做间接测量。
