📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • a>0 opens up (minimum), a<0 opens down (maximum); the vertex sits on the axis x=−2ab​.a>0 开口向上（有最小值），a<0 开口向下（有最大值）；顶点在对称轴 x=−2ab​ 上。
  • Range uses the vertex y-value: y≥q (up) or y≤q (down). Domain is always all real numbers.值域看顶点的纵坐标：开口向上 y≥q，向下 y≤q。定义域永远是全体实数。
  • Axis = midpoint of the zeros, or of any two points at the same height.对称轴 = 两个零点的中点，或任意两个等高点的中点。

💡 A basketball shot, a jet of water, a satellite dish — all parabolas. Six facts (vertex, axis, max/min, domain, range, intercepts) tell you everything about the curve.投篮的轨迹、喷泉的水柱、卫星锅的截面都是抛物线。顶点、对称轴、最值、定义域、值域、截距——掌握这六样，就看懂了整条曲线。

  1. A quadratic function is f(x)=ax2+bx+c with a=0. Its graph is a parabola: opens up if a>0 (has a minimum), opens down if a<0 (has a maximum).二次函数是 f(x)=ax2+bx+c（a=0），图象是抛物线：a>0 开口向上，有最小值；a<0 开口向下，有最大值。
  2. The vertex sits on the axis of symmetry x=−2ab​, exactly halfway between the x-intercepts. Plug that x back in to get the max or min value.顶点在对称轴 x=−2ab​ 上，正好在两个 x 截距的正中间。把这个 x 代回去，就得到最大值或最小值。
  3. Domain is always all real numbers. Range stops at the vertex: y≥q if it opens up, y≤q if it opens down (where q is the y-value of the vertex).定义域永远是全体实数。值域到顶点为止：开口向上是 y≥q，开口向下是 y≤q（q 是顶点的纵坐标）。
  4. y-intercept: set x=0, so it is just c. x-intercepts (zeros, roots): set f(x)=0 and solve. f(3) means "the y-value when x=3".y 截距：令 x=0，就是 c。x 截距（零点、根）：令 f(x)=0 解方程。f(3) 的意思是「x=3 时的 y 值」。

🎯 Test tip考试提示
Find the axis first — it gives the vertex, the max/min and the range in two lines. When the quadratic factors, the midpoint of the zeros is the fastest axis.先求对称轴，两行就能得到顶点、最值和值域。能分解时，用两个零点的中点求对称轴最快。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Write down a, b and c with their signs. The sign of a already tells you: up (minimum) or down (maximum).先写下带符号的 a、b、c。a 的符号已经告诉你：开口向上（有最小值）还是向下（有最大值）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Which of the six facts are wanted? Remember that a max or min VALUE is a y-value, and the range is about y.题目要六项特征中的哪几项？记住：最大值或最小值是一个 y 值，值域讲的也是 y。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    A quick U or ∩ with a dashed vertical line for the axis. The vertex sits on that line, and the two x-intercepts are mirror images across it.随手画个 U 或 ∩，再画一条竖直虚线当对称轴。顶点在这条线上，两个 x 截距关于它对称。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Axis first: x=−2ab​ (or the midpoint of the zeros). Put that x back into f to get the vertex height.先求对称轴：x=−2ab​（或两个零点的中点）。把这个 x 代回 f，得到顶点的高度。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    The zeros must be equally far from the axis. The range must use the vertex y-value, with ≥ for up and ≤ for down.两个零点到对称轴的距离必须相等。值域要用顶点的纵坐标：开口向上用 ≥，向下用 ≤。

1Losing the minus sign in the axis对称轴丢了负号
Find the vertex of y=x2+8x+7.求 y=x2+8x+7 的顶点。

🧠 The formula is x=−2ab​. With b positive the axis is on the negative side.公式是 x=−2ab​。b 为正时，对称轴在负的一侧。

🔧 x=−28​=−4, then y=16−32+7=−9.x=−28​=−4，再算 y=16−32+7=−9。

✔ (−4,−9)

2Writing the range with the x-value of the vertex用顶点横坐标写值域
Find the range of y=x2−10x+21.求 y=x2−10x+21 的值域。

🧠 The range lists y-values, so it must use the height of the vertex, not where it sits left or right.值域列的是 y 值，要用顶点的高度，而不是它在左右哪个位置。

🔧 The lowest y is −4, so the range is y≥−4.最小的 y 是 −4，所以值域是 y≥−4。

✔ y≥−4

3Range in the wrong direction值域方向写反
Find the range of f(x)=−3x2+6x+1.求 f(x)=−3x2+6x+1 的值域。

🧠 Habit: most parabolas you have drawn open up. Here a=−3<0, so the vertex is the TOP and every other y is below it.习惯作怪：画过的抛物线大多开口向上。这里 a=−3<0，顶点是「最高点」，其他 y 都比它低。

🔧 Opens down, so the range is y≤4.开口向下，值域是 y≤4。

✔ y≤4

4Squaring a negative input without brackets代入负数平方时没加括号
For f(x)=−x2+4x+3, find f(−2).对于 f(x)=−x2+4x+3，求 f(−2)。

🧠 The two minus signs looked like they cancel. But the square acts first: (−2)2=4, and then the minus in front makes it −4.两个负号看起来像能抵消。但平方先算：(−2)2=4，前面的负号再把它变成 −4。

🔧 −4−8+3=−9.−4−8+3=−9。

✔ −9

5Taking a given point as the top把题目给的点当成最高点
A parabola opens down, has x-intercepts 0 and 8, and passes through (2,6). Find its maximum value.一条开口向下的抛物线，x 截距为 0 和 8，且过点 (2,6)。求它的最大值。

🧠 The top is always on the axis. The point (2,6) only helps find a; it is not on the axis x=4.最高点一定在对称轴上。点 (2,6) 只用来求 a，它不在对称轴 x=4 上。

🔧 Axis x=20+8​=4; y=−21​(4)(−4)=8.对称轴 x=20+8​=4；y=−21​(4)(−4)=8。

✔ 8

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Getting the sign of the axis wrong: for y=x2+6x, x=−26​=−3, not 3. Write the minus sign in front before you plug in.对称轴符号算反：y=x2+6x 的对称轴是 x=−26​=−3，不是 3。代数之前先把前面的负号写上。
  • Writing the range with x or using the x-value of the vertex. Range is about y and uses the vertex y-value: vertex (2,−2) opening up gives y≥−2.值域写成关于 x 的，或者用了顶点的横坐标。值域讲的是 y，用顶点纵坐标：顶点 (2,−2) 开口向上，值域 y≥−2。
  • Evaluating f(−3) for f(x)=−x2 as 9. Brackets first: −(−3)2=−9.把 f(x)=−x2 的 f(−3) 算成 9。要先算括号：−(−3)2=−9。
