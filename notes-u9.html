Compound interest复利

  • Compound interest earns interest on interest. Each period the balance is multiplied by (1+i), where i=nr​ is the rate per compounding period.复利就是「利滚利」。每个计息期余额乘以 (1+i)，其中 i=nr​ 是每期利率。
  • In A=P(1+nr​)nt: P = principal, r = annual rate as a decimal, n = compounding periods per year (annually 1, semi-annually 2, quarterly 4, monthly 12, daily 365), t = years.在 A=P(1+nr​)nt 中：P＝本金，r＝年利率（小数），n＝每年计息次数（按年 1、半年 2、季度 4、按月 12、按日 365），t＝年数。
  • Same annual rate, more frequent compounding → slightly more money. But a higher rate usually beats more frequent compounding — always compute both.年利率相同时，计息越频繁，钱越多一点。但更高的利率通常胜过更频繁的计息——两个都要算。
  • Rule of 72: money doubles in about rate in %72​ years. It is a mental estimate, not an exact answer.72 法则：资金大约 利率（百分数）72​ 年翻一番。这是心算估计，不是精确答案。
A=P(1+nr​)nt
Compound interest (future value of a lump sum)复利公式（一次性存入的终值）
P=(1+nr​)ntA​
Present value: how much to invest now现值：现在需要投入多少
I=A−P

doubling time≈r(%)72​
Rule of 72 (estimate)72 法则（估算）
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Using the percent as a whole number: r=0.04, not 4.把百分数直接当整数用：r=0.04，不是 4。
  • Forgetting to multiply years by n: monthly for 6 years is 72 periods, not 6.忘了年数要乘 n：按月复利 6 年是 72 期，不是 6 期。
  • Rounding i=120.04​ to 0.0033 before raising it to a power — the answer can be off by dollars. Keep full precision until the last step.在乘方之前把 i=120.04​ 舍成 0.0033——结果可能差好几加元。最后一步才四舍五入。
  • Treating the Rule of 72 as exact. Say "about" and use the formula when the question asks for an exact or rounded value.把 72 法则当成精确值。要说「大约」；题目要精确或指定精度时用公式。

🎯 Write P, r, n, t (and i, nt) as a list before substituting — most lost marks are a wrong n or nt. Round only the final dollar answer, to the cent.代入前先列出 P、r、n、t（以及 i、nt）——大部分失分是 n 或 nt 用错。只在最后把金额四舍五入到分。

Loans & investments with regular payments定期付款的贷款与投资

  • An annuity is a series of equal, regular payments. Saving → you want the future value FV. Borrowing → the loan is the present value PV and you want the payment PMT.年金就是一串金额相等、定期支付的款项。存钱 → 求终值 FV；借钱 → 贷款额就是现值 PV，要求每期还款 PMT。
  • In the formulas i is the interest rate per PAYMENT period and n is the number of payments. When payments and compounding match (e.g. both monthly), i=12r​.公式中 i 是每个「付款期」的利率，n 是付款次数。付款与计息频率相同时（如都按月），i=12r​。
  • With technology (TVM Solver or an online TVM calculator), enter N, I%, PV, PMT, FV, P/Y, C/Y and solve for the blank. Money you pay out is negative, money you receive is positive.用技术工具（TVM Solver 或网上的 TVM 计算器）：输入 N、I%、PV、PMT、FV、P/Y、C/Y，求空着的那一项。付出的钱为负，收到的钱为正。
  • Total interest = (payment × number of payments) − amount borrowed (or − total deposited, for savings).总利息＝每期金额×期数－借款额（储蓄则减去存入总额）。

Canadian mortgage: compounded semi-annually, paid monthly加拿大房贷：半年复利、按月还款时的月利率
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Using the annual rate as i. For monthly payments at 6.9%, i=0.069÷12, and n counts months.把年利率直接当 i。按月还款、年利率 6.9% 时，i=0.069÷12，n 按月数计。
  • Sign errors on the TVM Solver: if PV and PMT are both positive (or both negative) for a loan, the solver fails or gives nonsense.TVM 符号错误：贷款题里 PV 和 PMT 同正或同负，计算器会报错或给出离谱结果。
  • Leaving C/Y =12 for a Canadian mortgage, which is compounded semi-annually (C/Y =2).加拿大房贷是半年复利（C/Y =2），却把 C/Y 留成 12。
  • Calling the total of payments "the interest". Interest is total paid MINUS the amount borrowed.把还款总额当成利息。利息是还款总额减去借款额。

🎯 Show the TVM entries (N, I%, PV, PMT, FV, P/Y, C/Y) as a list — on tests that list IS the work. Then state total paid and interest with one subtraction each.把 TVM 的输入（N、I%、PV、PMT、FV、P/Y、C/Y）列成清单——考试中这份清单就是解题过程。再各用一次减法写出还款总额和利息。

Buy, lease or rent?买、租赁还是租用？

  • Buy: you pay the price (cash or loan with interest) and you OWN an asset at the end. Net cost = everything you paid − what the asset is worth at the end.买：付车价（现金或贷款加利息），最后车归你。净成本＝付出的全部－到时资产的价值。
  • Lease: lower monthly payments, but at the end you own nothing. Total cost = down payment + all monthly payments + end fees (extra kilometres, wear).租赁（lease）：月付更低，但到期什么都不归你。总成本＝首付＋全部月付＋到期费用（超里程、磨损）。
  • Cars depreciate (lose value) and homes usually appreciate (gain value). Both follow V=P(1±r)t — the compound-interest pattern with a minus or plus.车会贬值，房子通常升值。两者都符合 V=P(1±r)t——就是复利公式，减号或加号。
  • Rent vs buy a home: compare the costs you never get back. Rent is all unrecoverable; for owners it is mortgage interest, property tax, strata fees, maintenance and lost investment return on the down payment — mortgage principal is NOT a cost, it becomes equity.租房还是买房：比较「拿不回来的钱」。房租全部拿不回；业主的是房贷利息、地税、物业费（strata）、维修费，以及首付本可以投资赚到的回报——还掉的本金不是成本，它变成了你的房产净值。
V=P(1−r)t
Depreciation (value after t years)贬值（t 年后的价值）
V=P(1+r)t
Appreciation升值
net cost of buying=down+all payments−resale value
Net cost of buying买的净成本
lease cost=down+monthly×months+end fees
Total cost of leasing租赁总成本
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Comparing only monthly payments. A lease always looks cheaper per month; compare TOTAL cost minus what you own at the end.只比较月供。租赁按月看总是更便宜；要比较总成本减去最后拥有的资产价值。
  • Using V=P(1−rt) or subtracting 15% of the ORIGINAL price every year. Depreciation compounds: each year is 15% of that year’s value.用 V=P(1−rt) 或每年都减原价的 15%。贬值是复合的：每年减的是当年价值的 15%。
  • Counting the whole mortgage payment as a cost of owning. Only the interest part is lost; the principal part is savings in your home.把整笔房贷月供都算作买房成本。只有利息部分是损失；本金部分是存在房子里的钱。

🎯 Buy-vs-lease questions are graded on organisation: make two columns (buy, lease), list every cost with its calculation, subtract the resale value on the buy side, then give a one-sentence recommendation with a reason.买与租的题目主要看条理：分两栏（买、租），逐项列出成本和算式，在「买」一栏减去转卖价值，最后用一句话给出建议并说明理由。
