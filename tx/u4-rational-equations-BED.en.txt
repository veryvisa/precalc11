You know, forgetting to multiply the whole numbers or losing a minus sign in front of a fraction are classic mistakes, so watch out. Second, let's talk about work problems. A job done in T hours means a rate of 1 over T of the job per hour.

Remember, rates working together add up. Definitely not the times. Adding a 4-hour job and a 12-hour job to get 16 hours is exactly what not to do. Your go-to equation is 1 over T1 plus 1 over T2 equals 1 over big T. Finally, for travel problems, time equals distance divided by speed, so build your equations directly from the times.
