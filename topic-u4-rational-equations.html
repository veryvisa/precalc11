📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • NPVs first; then multiply EVERY term (whole numbers too) by the LCD.先写不允许值；再把每一项（包括整数项）都乘以公分母。
  • An answer equal to an NPV is extraneous — reject it; all rejected → no solution.等于不允许值的解是增根，舍去；全部舍去 → 无解。
  • Work: rates add, t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​. Travel: time =speeddistance​.工作问题：效率相加，t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​。行程问题：时间 =速度路程​。

💡 Two hoses together fill a hot tub in 4 hours, and the big one alone is 6 hours faster than the small one. How long does each take alone? Rates add as fractions — and one of the two answers you get is impossible.两根水管一起 4 小时灌满一个热水池，大水管单独灌比小水管快 6 小时。各自单独要多久？效率是分数相加——而你算出的两个答案里有一个不可能。

  1. Write the NPVs first. Then multiply EVERY term on both sides by the LCD — the fractions disappear and you solve an ordinary linear or quadratic equation.先写不允许值。然后把两边的每一项都乘以最简公分母——分母消失，剩下普通的一次或二次方程。
  2. Any answer equal to an NPV is extraneous: reject it. If every answer is rejected, the equation has no solution.等于不允许值的答案就是增根，要舍去。如果所有答案都被舍去，方程无解。
  3. Work problems: a job done in t hours means a rate of t1​ job per hour. Rates working together add: t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​.工程问题：t 小时完成一件工作，效率就是每小时 t1​。合作时效率相加：t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​。
  4. Travel problems: time =speeddistance​. Build the equation from the times (e.g. "one trip took 1 h less").行程问题：时间 =速度路程​。用时间列方程（如「这一趟少用 1 小时」）。

Exam word problems are built so one root is negative or an NPV. Define your variable in words ("let t = hours for the small hose"), and end with a sentence answer — both are usually marked.考试应用题通常设计成有一个根是负数或不允许值。用文字定义变量（「设小水管需 t 小时」），最后用一句话作答——这两处一般都有分。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Factor every denominator and write the NPVs on line 1. In a word problem, define the variable in words and add the context limits (time and speed >0, people a whole number).把每个分母分解，第一行写出不允许值。应用题先用文字定义变量，再加上实际限制（时间、速度 >0，人数是整数）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Every value of x that makes the ORIGINAL equation true and is allowed. There may be one, two, or none.所有让「原」方程成立而且被允许的 x——可能一个、两个，也可能没有。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    For word problems, a small table: one row per person, pipe or trip; columns for rate (or speed) and time. The equation comes from one column.应用题画一个小表：每个人、每根管或每一段路一行；列是效率（或速度）和时间。方程来自其中一列。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Multiply EVERY term on both sides — whole numbers too — by the LCD. Solve the linear or quadratic equation left over.把两边「每一项」——包括整数项——都乘以最简公分母，再解剩下的一次或二次方程。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Cross out any answer that is an NPV or breaks the context. Put the survivors back into the original equation.划掉等于不允许值或不符合实际的答案，把剩下的代回原方程检验。

🧱 Prerequisite check先修检查

4 quick Grade 9–10 questions, about 3 minutes. Miss one and a 5-minute repair card opens right there; fix it, then carry on.4 道九、十年级小题，约 3 分钟。错了就在原地打开 5 分钟补救卡，补好再继续。

1Lowest common denominator最简公分母
What is the lowest common denominator of x−21​ and x2−43​?x−21​ 和 x2−43​ 的最简公分母是什么？

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Lowest common denominator最简公分母 (Grade年级 8–10)
Factor each denominator; the LCD uses every factor the HIGHEST number of times it appears. For 6 and 4: 2⋅3 and 22 → 22⋅3=12. For x and x2: x2. For (x+1) and (x−2): (x+1)(x−2).把每个分母分解；最简公分母取每个因式出现的最高次数。6 和 4：2⋅3 与 22 → 22⋅3=12。x 和 x2：x2。(x+1) 和 (x−2)：(x+1)(x−2)。
  1. 2x1​ and 3x21​: LCD =6x2.2x1​ 与 3x21​：最简公分母 =6x2。
  2. Multiply top AND bottom by what is missing: 2x1​=6x23x​.分子分母同乘缺的部分：2x1​=6x23x​。
Try one:试一道：
What is the LCD of x2−91​ and x+31​?x2−91​ 与 x+31​ 的最简公分母是？

2Solving a linear equation解一元一次方程
Solve 3(x−2)=x+4.解 3(x−2)=x+4。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Solving a linear equation解一元一次方程 (Grade年级 8–9)
Do the same thing to both sides. Clear brackets, collect x on one side and numbers on the other, then divide. With fractions, multiply every term by the common denominator first.两边做同样的事。先去括号，把含 x 的项移到一边、数字移到另一边，最后除以系数。有分数时先把每一项都乘以公分母。
  1. 3(x−2)=x+8 → 3x−6=x+8 → 2x=14 → x=7.3(x−2)=x+8 → 3x−6=x+8 → 2x=14 → x=7。
  2. 2x​+3x​=5: multiply every term by 6 → 3x+2x=30 → x=6.2x​+3x​=5：每项乘 6 → 3x+2x=30 → x=6。
  3. Check by substituting: 3(7−2)=15 and 7+8=15 ✓.代回检验：3(7−2)=15，7+8=15 ✓。
Try one:试一道：
Solve 4x−1​=6x​.解 4x−1​=6x​。

3Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）
Solve x2−x−12=0.解 x2−x−12=0。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和） (Grade年级 10)
Take out a common factor first. Then find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b: x2−x−12 → −4 and 3 → (x−4)(x+3). Check by expanding.先提公因式。再找两个数，相乘等于 c、相加等于 b：x2−x−12 → −4 和 3 → (x−4)(x+3)。展开检验。
  1. x2+7x+10: 2×5=10, 2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5).x2+7x+10：2×5=10，2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5)。
  2. If c<0 the two numbers have different signs; if c>0 they share the sign of b.若 c<0，两个数异号；若 c>0，两个数与 b 同号。
Try one:试一道：
Factor x2−2x−15.分解 x2−2x−15。

4Percent, decimals and rates百分数、小数与比率
A tap fills a tank in 5 hours. What fraction of the tank does it fill in 1 hour?一个水龙头 5 小时灌满一个水箱。它 1 小时灌满水箱的几分之几？

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Percent, decimals and rates百分数、小数与比率 (Grade年级 8–10)
Percent to decimal: divide by 100 (4.5%=0.045). A rate per year split into n periods is nr​ per period: 6% compounded monthly is 0.005 per month. Increase by r: multiply by (1+r).百分数化小数：除以 100（4.5%=0.045）。年利率分成 n 期，每期是 nr​：年利率 6% 按月复利，每月 0.005。增长 r：乘以 (1+r)。
  1. $200 increased by 3%: 200×1.03=206.$200 增长 3%：200×1.03=206。
  2. 3.6% compounded quarterly: 40.036​=0.009 per quarter.年利率 3.6% 按季复利：每季 40.036​=0.009。

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

Solve x−3x​+x2​=x−33​.解方程 x−3x​+x2​=x−33​。

    NPVs: x=3, 0. LCD =x(x−3).不允许值：x=3, 0。最简公分母 =x(x−3)。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么

2Not multiplying the whole-number term整数项没乘公分母
Solve x+x4​=5.解方程 x+x4​=5。

🧠 Only the terms that looked like fractions got multiplied. But the LCD must multiply EVERY term, and the 5 is a term too.只乘了看起来像分数的项。可是公分母要乘「每一项」，5 也是一项。

🔧 x2+4=5x, so x2−5x+4=0, (x−1)(x−4)=0: x=1 or x=4 (neither is the NPV 0).x2+4=5x，即 x2−5x+4=0，(x−1)(x−4)=0：x=1 或 x=4（都不是不允许值 0）。

3Losing the minus in front of a fraction分式前面的减号丢了
Solve x3​−xx−6​=2.解方程 x3​−xx−6​=2。

🧠 When the fraction disappeared, its numerator lost its brackets. The minus must hit the whole numerator: −(x−6)=−x+6.分母一消失，分子的括号也跟着丢了。减号要作用于整个分子：−(x−6)=−x+6。

🔧 3−(x−6)=2x, so 9−x=2x, x=3. Check: 1−3−3​=2 ✓.3−(x−6)=2x，即 9−x=2x，x=3。检验：1−3−3​=2 ✓。

✔ x=3

4Adding times instead of rates把时间相加，而不是把效率相加
One printer finishes a job in 4 h, another in 12 h. How long working together?一台打印机 4 小时完成一项任务，另一台要 12 小时。两台一起要多久？

🧠 Two machines together must be FASTER than either alone, so the answer has to be under 4 h. What adds is the part of the job done each hour.两台一起一定比任何一台单独快，所以答案必须少于 4 小时。能相加的是「每小时完成的部分」。

🔧 41​+121​=123​+121​=31​ job per hour, so together T=3 h.41​+121​=123​+121​=31​（每小时完成的部分），所以一起 T=3 小时。

5Subtracting the times the wrong way round时间相减的顺序弄反了

  • Multiplying only the fractions by the LCD and forgetting whole-number terms: in 1+x2−412​=…, the 1 becomes x2−4.只给分式乘最简公分母，忘了整数项：1+x2−412​=… 里的 1 要变成 x2−4。
