📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Buy: net cost = total paid − value at the end. Lease: down + payments + end fees; you own nothing.买：净成本＝总支出 − 期末残值。租：首付＋月供＋到期费用；到期什么都不归你。
  • Depreciation compounds: V=P(1−r)t; appreciation: V=P(1+r)t.贬值是复利式的：V=P(1−r)t；升值：V=P(1+r)t。
  • Rent vs buy: compare unrecoverable costs; mortgage principal is equity, not a cost.租房还是买房：比较收不回的成本；房贷本金是自己的资产，不算成本。

💡 A lease at $389 a month looks far cheaper than a $703 car-loan payment. Add up everything over four years — including what the car is worth at the end — and buying can come out ahead.每月 $389 的租赁看上去比 $703 的车贷便宜得多。把四年的所有花费加起来——包括车到时还值多少钱——买车反而可能更划算。

  1. Buy: you pay the price (cash or loan with interest) and you OWN an asset at the end. Net cost = everything you paid − what the asset is worth at the end.买：付车价（现金或贷款加利息），最后车归你。净成本＝付出的全部－到时资产的价值。
  2. Lease: lower monthly payments, but at the end you own nothing. Total cost = down payment + all monthly payments + end fees (extra kilometres, wear).租赁（lease）：月付更低，但到期什么都不归你。总成本＝首付＋全部月付＋到期费用（超里程、磨损）。
  3. Cars depreciate (lose value) and homes usually appreciate (gain value). Both follow V=P(1±r)t — the compound-interest pattern with a minus or plus.车会贬值，房子通常升值。两者都符合 V=P(1±r)t——就是复利公式，减号或加号。
  4. Rent vs buy a home: compare the costs you never get back. Rent is all unrecoverable; for owners it is mortgage interest, property tax, strata fees, maintenance and lost investment return on the down payment — mortgage principal is NOT a cost, it becomes equity.租房还是买房：比较「拿不回来的钱」。房租全部拿不回；业主的是房贷利息、地税、物业费（strata）、维修费，以及首付本可以投资赚到的回报——还掉的本金不是成本，它变成了你的房产净值。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    For each option, list every cash flow over the SAME number of years: down payment, each payment times the number of months, end fees, and what you own at the end (its resale value).对每种方案，列出「相同年数」内的每一笔钱：首付、每期付款乘以月数、到期费用，以及期末拥有的东西（转卖价值）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Which option has the lower NET cost, and by how much? Then give a one-sentence recommendation with a reason.哪种方案的「净成本」更低？低多少？最后用一句话给出建议并说明理由。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    A two-column table, Buy | Lease (or Rent | Own). One line per cost; the buy column ends with "− resale value".一张两栏表：买｜租（或租房｜买房）。每项成本一行；「买」那一栏最后一行是「− 转卖价值」。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Resale value V=P(1−r)t; appreciation V=P(1+r)t. A loan payment comes from the payment formula or TVM. For a home, count only interest, tax, strata, maintenance and lost return; a Canadian fixed-rate mortgage is quoted compounded semi-annually (C/Y =2, P/Y =12).转卖价值 V=P(1−r)t；升值 V=P(1+r)t。贷款月供用月供公式或 TVM。房子只算利息、地税、物业费、维修和机会成本；加拿大固定利率房贷按半年复利标示（C/Y =2，P/Y =12）。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Same time span on both sides? Resale less than the price? A lower monthly payment does NOT mean a lower total.两边年数一样吗？转卖价低于原价吗？月供低「不等于」总成本低。

A car loses 15% of its value each year. Each year its value is multiplied by…一辆车每年贬值 15%。它的价值每年乘以……

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Percent, decimals and rates百分数、小数与比率 (Grade年级 8–10)
Percent to decimal: divide by 100 (4.5%=0.045). A rate per year split into n periods is nr​ per period: 6% compounded monthly is 0.005 per month. Increase by r: multiply by (1+r).百分数化小数：除以 100（4.5%=0.045）。年利率分成 n 期，每期是 nr​：年利率 6% 按月复利，每月 0.005。增长 r：乘以 (1+r)。
  1. $200 increased by 3%: 200×1.03=206.$200 增长 3%：200×1.03=206。
  2. 3.6% compounded quarterly: 40.036​=0.009 per quarter.年利率 3.6% 按季复利：每季 40.036​=0.009。

2Exponent rules (product, quotient, power)指数运算法则（同底乘、除、乘方）
Evaluate 20000(0.9)2.计算 20000(0.9)2。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Exponent rules (product, quotient, power)指数运算法则（同底乘、除、乘方） (Grade年级 9)
Same base: multiply → ADD exponents (x3x4=x7); divide → SUBTRACT (x7÷x2=x5); power of a power → MULTIPLY ((x3)2=x6). A power of a product hits every factor: (2x)3=8x3.同底数：相乘→指数相加（x3x4=x7）；相除→指数相减（x7÷x2=x5）；幂的乘方→指数相乘（(x3)2=x6）。积的乘方每个因数都要乘方：(2x)3=8x3。
  1. (3x2)2⋅x=9x4⋅x=9x5.(3x2)2⋅x=9x4⋅x=9x5。
  2. 2x26x5​=3x3 (divide the numbers, subtract the exponents).2x26x5​=3x3（系数相除，指数相减）。

4Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘
You paid $21500 in total for a car and sold it for $9800. What was your net cost?你买车一共付了 $21500，后来卖了 $9800。净成本是多少？

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。

🪜 Worked → fill in → your turn例题 → 填空 → 自己做

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

① Worked example — read it完整例题——看懂
Buy a $26000 car for cash and sell it after 3 years (it loses 18% of its value each year), or lease it for 3 years: $1500 down, $369 a month and $400 in end-of-lease fees. Which costs less over the 3 years, and by how much?现金买一辆 $26000 的车，3 年后卖掉（每年贬值 18%）；或者租 3 年：首付 $1500、每月 $369、到期费用 $400。3 年里哪个花得少？少多少？
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Both options cover the same 3 years. Buy: pay $26000, get the resale value back. Lease: down payment, 36 payments and fees, nothing back.两种方案都是 3 年。买：付 $26000，最后拿回转卖价值。租：首付、36 期月付和费用，最后什么都拿不回。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    The net cost of each option, and the difference.求两种方案的净成本，以及差额。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Two columns, Buy | Lease. The buy column ends with "− resale value".两栏：买｜租。「买」一栏最后一行是「− 转卖价值」。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Resale: 26000(0.82)3≈$14335.57. Net cost of buying =26000−14335.57=$11664.43.转卖价值：26000(0.82)3≈$14335.57。买的净成本 =26000−14335.57=$11664.43。
  5. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Lease: 1500+369×36+400=1500+13284+400=$15184.租：1500+369×36+400=1500+13284+400=$15184。
  6. ✅ Check检验
    Buying is 15184−11664.43=$3519.57 cheaper. It needs $26000 up front, so the lease only wins if you cannot spare that cash.买便宜 15184−11664.43=$3519.57。不过买要先拿出 $26000，所以只有拿不出这笔现金时，租才占优。
✔

1Comparing only the monthly payments只比较月供
A $26000 car. Lease: $2000 down, $329 a month for 48 months, $500 in fees. Buy: no down payment and a loan of $580 a month for 48 months, then sell the car (it loses 15% a year). Which costs less over 4 years?一辆 $26000 的车。租：首付 $2000，每月 $329 共 48 个月，费用 $500。买：不付首付，贷款每月 $580 共 48 个月，之后卖掉（每年贬值 15%）。4 年里哪个花得少？

🧠 A lease payment is low because you never own the car. Buying hands you a car worth thousands at the end, and the monthly numbers ignore that.租的月付低，是因为车永远不归你。买车到最后手里有一辆值好几千元的车，只看月供就把这一点漏掉了。

2Forgetting to subtract the resale value忘了减去转卖价值
Buy a $28000 car for cash (it loses 15% a year and is sold after 3 years), or lease: $2500 down, $359 a month for 36 months, $400 in fees?现金买一辆 $28000 的车（每年贬值 15%，3 年后卖掉），还是租：首付 $2500，每月 $359 共 36 个月，费用 $400？

🧠 After 3 years the buyer still owns a car and sells it. That money comes back, so it must be taken off the buy side.3 年后买车的人手里还有车，可以卖掉。这笔钱会回来，要从「买」那一栏减掉。

🔧 Resale 28000(0.85)3≈$17195.50, so the net cost of buying is $10804.50. Buying is cheaper by $5019.50.转卖价值 28000(0.85)3≈$17195.50，所以买的净成本是 $10804.50。买便宜 $5019.50。

✔ Buying, by $5019.50买，便宜 $5019.50

3Taking the same amount off every year每年都减同样的钱
A $24000 car loses 20% of its value each year. What is it worth after 3 years?一辆 $24000 的车每年贬值 20%。3 年后值多少？

🧠 The 20% is taken from the value AT THAT TIME. After year 1 the car is worth less, so 20% of it is less than $4800.20% 是按「当时」的价值算的。第一年后车已经不值那么多了，它的 20% 就不到 $4800。

🔧 It keeps 80% each year: V=24000(0.8)3=$12288.每年保留 80%：V=24000(0.8)3=$12288。

✔ $12288

4Multiplying by the rate instead of what is kept乘的是贬值率而不是保留率
A $15000 car loses 10% of its value each year. What is it worth after 2 years?一辆 $15000 的车每年贬值 10%。2 年后值多少？

🧠 Losing 10% means keeping 90%. Multiplying by 0.1 keeps only 10% each year, which is why the answer is absurdly small.贬值 10% 就是保留 90%。乘 0.1 等于每年只留下 10%，所以答案小得离谱。

🔧 V=15000(0.9)2=$12150.V=15000(0.9)2=$12150。

✔ $12150

5Counting the whole mortgage payment as a cost把整笔房贷还款都算成本
Rent is $2000 a month. Owning a similar home this year: mortgage payments $24000 (of which $15000 is interest), property tax $2000, strata $3600, maintenance $1200, lost return on the down payment $2500. Which costs less in money you never get back?月租 $2000。买一套类似的房子，今年要付：房贷还款 $24000（其中利息 $15000）、地税 $2000、物业费 $3600、维修 $1200、首付的机会成本 $2500。按拿不回来的钱算，哪个更省？

🧠 Only the $15000 of interest is gone. The other $9000 pays down the loan, so it stays yours as equity in the home.真正花掉的只有 $15000 利息。另外 $9000 用来还本金，变成你在房子里的净值，仍然是你的。

🔧 Own: 15000+2000+3600+1200+2500=$24300. Renting is cheaper by only $300, so a small rise in the home's value would tip it the other way.买：15000+2000+3600+1200+2500=$24300。租房只便宜 $300，房价稍微涨一点就会反过来。

✔ Renting, by $300租房，便宜 $300

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Comparing only monthly payments. A lease always looks cheaper per month; compare TOTAL cost minus what you own at the end.只比较月供。租赁按月看总是更便宜；要比较总成本减去最后拥有的资产价值。
  • Using V=P(1−rt) or subtracting 15% of the ORIGINAL price every year. Depreciation compounds: each year is 15% of that year’s value.用 V=P(1−rt) 或每年都减原价的 15%。贬值是复合的：每年减的是当年价值的 15%。
  • Counting the whole mortgage payment as a cost of owning. Only the interest part is lost; the principal part is savings in your home.把整笔房贷月供都算作买房成本。只有利息部分是损失；本金部分是存在房子里的钱。

The car actually loses 17% of its value each year. (a) Find the cost of leasing and returning the car, and the monthly cost of that option. (b) Find the car's real market value after 3 years. (c) Should you exercise the buyout? Justify with numbers, and say what residual price would make the buyout fair.一辆 $36000 的车，租赁合同为首付 $2000，另每月 $419，共 36 个月。租期结束时可以还车，也可以按合同残值价 $21000 买下。这辆车实际每年贬值 17%。(a) 求租赁并还车的总成本和该方案的每月成本。(b) 求 3 年后这辆车的实际市场价值。(c) 你应该行使买断权吗？用数字说明，并说明残值定为多少才算公平。
Market value after 3 years is 36000(0.83)3. Compare it with the residual price.3 年后的市场价值是 36000(0.83)3。把它与残值价比较。
(a) $17084, about $474.56 a month; (b) $20584.33; (c) No: the buyout is $415.67 above market; a fair residual is about $20584.(a) $17084，每月约 $474.56；(b) $20584.33；(c) 不应买断：残值价比市场价高 $415.67；公平的残值约 $20584。

  3. The residual is 21000−20584.33=$415.67 more than the car is worth, so buying it out overpays; you would pay more than you could get for it. A fair residual is the expected market value, about $20584.残值价比车的实际价值高 21000−20584.33=$415.67，买断就是多付钱，买下后也卖不出这个价。公平的残值应等于预期市场价，约 $20584。
