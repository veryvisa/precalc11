Linear inequalities & interval notation一次不等式与区间记号

  • Solve like an equation: same thing to both sides. One extra rule: multiplying or dividing by a NEGATIVE number flips the sign (< becomes >).像解方程一样，两边做同样的运算。只多一条规则：两边乘或除以一个负数，不等号要反向（< 变 >）。
  • Three ways to write the answer x≤4: interval (−∞,4]; set {x∣x≤4, x∈R}; number line with a closed dot at 4 and shading to the left.x≤4 有三种写法：区间 (−∞,4]；集合 {x∣x≤4, x∈R}；数轴上 4 处画实心点，向左涂。
  • Square bracket / closed dot = included (≤, ≥). Round bracket / open dot = not included (<, >). Infinity always gets a round bracket.方括号/实心点 = 包含（≤、≥）；圆括号/空心点 = 不包含（<、>）。无穷大永远用圆括号。
a<b and k<0 ⇒ ka>kb
Multiplying by a negative flips the sign乘以负数，不等号反向
x≤4 ⇔ (−∞,4]
Inequality ↔ interval notation不等式 ↔ 区间记号
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Forgetting to flip when dividing by a negative: −3x<12 gives x>−4, not x<−4. Test a number to be sure.除以负数忘了变号：−3x<12 得 x>−4，不是 x<−4。代一个数检验一下。
  • Writing [−∞,4] or (−∞,4) for x≤4. Infinity is never included; 4 is included, so (−∞,4].把 x≤4 写成 [−∞,4] 或 (−∞,4)。无穷大永远不包含，4 要包含，所以是 (−∞,4]。
  • Flipping the sign when you only SUBTRACT a number. Only multiplying or dividing by a negative flips it.只是减去一个数也变号。只有乘或除以负数才变号。

🎯 After solving, test one value from your answer in the ORIGINAL inequality — it takes 15 seconds and catches every forgotten flip. Match brackets to dots: [ ↔ closed dot, ( ↔ open dot.解完后，在答案范围里取一个数代回「原」不等式，15 秒就能抓住所有忘记变号的错误。括号和点要对应：[ ↔ 实心点，( ↔ 空心点。

Quadratic inequalities (sign analysis)二次不等式（符号分析）

  • Step 1: move everything to one side so you compare with 0. Step 2: find the zeros (factor or formula). They split the number line into intervals.第一步：全部移到一边，和 0 比较。第二步：求零点（分解或公式），零点把数轴分成几段。
  • Step 3: find the sign on each interval — test one number in each (sign chart), or picture the parabola: opening up, it is negative BETWEEN the zeros and positive OUTSIDE them.第三步：判断每一段的符号——每段代一个数（符号表），或者想象抛物线：开口向上时，两零点之间为负，两侧为正。
  • Step 4: pick the intervals that match the sign asked for. Include the zeros for ≤ or ≥, exclude them for < or >.第四步：选出符号符合要求的区间。≤、≥ 包含零点，<、> 不包含。
  • No real zeros (D<0)? Then the expression has one sign everywhere: the answer is all real numbers or no solution.没有实数零点（D<0）？那么式子在整条数轴上同号，答案要么是全体实数，要么无解。
a(x−r)(x−s)<0, a>0, r<s ⇒ r<x<s
Opens up: negative between the zeros开口向上：两零点之间为负
a(x−r)(x−s)>0, a>0 ⇒ x<r or x>s
Opens up: positive outside the zeros开口向上：两零点外侧为正
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Solving x2<9 as x<±3. Square roots do not work on inequalities; find zeros ±3, then −3<x<3.把 x2<9 解成 x<±3。不等式不能直接开方；先求零点 ±3，得 −3<x<3。
  • Forgetting to flip after multiplying by −1 to make a positive. Or skip that step and use the graph of the original.为了让 a 为正乘了 −1，却忘了变号。也可以不乘，直接看原式的图象。
  • Not moving everything to one side first: x2+2x≥15 must become x2+2x−15≥0 before factoring.没先移项：x2+2x≥15 要先变成 x2+2x−15≥0 才能分解。
  • Writing "x≤−3 and x≥21​". No number does both; the word is "or" (union ∪).写成「x≤−3 且 x≥21​」。没有数能同时满足；应该是「或」（并集 ∪）。

🎯 A 5-second sketch of the parabola (which way it opens + where it crosses) answers almost every quadratic inequality. Use the sign chart when the question says "show your work" or the expression has more than two factors.花 5 秒画个抛物线草图（开口方向 + 与 x 轴交点），几乎所有二次不等式都能解决。题目要求「写出过程」或因式不止两个时，再用符号表。

Domain & range limits in context实际问题中的定义域与值域

  • Domain in context: which input values are physically possible? Time ≥0 and stops when the object lands; lengths >0; counts of people or items are whole numbers.实际问题的定义域：哪些输入真的可能？时间 ≥0，物体落地后停止；长度 >0；人数、件数只能是整数。
  • Range in context: the outputs you get from that domain. For a parabola that opens down, the top of the range is the vertex value; the bottom comes from an endpoint of the domain.实际问题的值域：由这个定义域得到的输出。开口向下的抛物线，值域上端是顶点值，下端由定义域的端点决定。
  • "When is it at least / more than…?" is a quadratic inequality. Solve it, then keep only the part inside the context domain.「什么时候至少/超过……？」就是二次不等式。解出来以后，只保留落在实际定义域里的部分。
h(t)≥0  and  t≥0
Typical projectile restrictions抛体问题的常见限制
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Giving "all real numbers" as the domain of a word problem. Ask what values are physically possible.应用题的定义域写成「全体实数」。要问哪些值实际上可能出现。
  • Stopping at the inequality answer without checking it against the domain — a negative time in your interval must be cut off.解出不等式就停了，没和定义域对照——区间里出现负的时间要截掉。
  • Taking the range from the endpoints only. For an arch, the highest value is the vertex, which is usually in the middle.只用端点求值域。拱形的最大值在顶点，通常在中间。

🎯 Context questions usually have a part (a) "state the domain and range" worth easy marks — write them with units and the reason (e.g. "time cannot be negative"). For "at least/more than" parts, solve, then intersect with the domain.实际问题常有一问「写出定义域和值域」，是送分题——带上单位和理由（如「时间不能为负」）。「至少/超过」那一问，解完不等式再和定义域取交集。
