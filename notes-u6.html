Solving by factoring & square roots因式分解法与直接开平方法

  • Factoring method: get 0 on one side, factor, then set each factor equal to 0 (zero product property).因式分解法：先让一边为 0，再分解，然后令每个因式等于 0（零积性质）。
  • Square root method: when the equation is "something squared = number", isolate the square and take ± ​. Forgetting ± loses half the answers.直接开平方法：方程是「某式的平方 = 数」时，先把平方项单独放一边，再开方取 ±。忘了 ± 就丢掉一半答案。
  • If the squared thing equals a negative number, there is no real solution. Never divide both sides by x — you lose the root x=0.若平方等于负数，则无实数解。不要两边同除以 x，否则会丢掉 x=0 这个根。
AB=0 ⇒ A=0 or B=0
Zero product property零积性质
(x−p)2=k ⇒ x=p±k​(k≥0)
Square root method直接开平方法
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Setting factors equal to the right side when it is not 0: (x−2)(x+1)=4 does NOT mean x−2=4. Expand and move the 4 over first.右边不是 0 就让因式等于它：(x−2)(x+1)=4 不能推出 x−2=4。先展开，把 4 移过来。
  • Dividing 3x2=12x by x and getting only x=4. Factor instead: 3x(x−4)=0, so x=0 or x=4.把 3x2=12x 两边除以 x，只得到 x=4。应该分解：3x(x−4)=0，得 x=0 或 x=4。
  • Writing x2=25⇒x=5. It is x=±5.写成 x2=25⇒x=5。应为 x=±5。

🎯 Before anything else, look for a ready-made square — (x−3)2=18 takes one step with square roots but four if you expand. If b2−4ac is a perfect square, it factors.先看有没有现成的平方——(x−3)2=18 开平方一步就完，展开要四步。若 b2−4ac 是完全平方数，就一定能分解。

Completing the square配方法

  • Recipe: (1) make a=1 by dividing; (2) move the constant to the right; (3) add (2b​)2 to BOTH sides; (4) write the left side as (x+2b​)2; (5) take ± ​.步骤：(1) 两边同除，使 a=1；(2) 常数项移到右边；(3) 两边同时加 (2b​)2；(4) 左边写成 (x+2b​)2；(5) 开方取 ±。
  • Solving an equation, you add to both sides. Converting a function to vertex form, you add and subtract on the same side. Same idea, different bookkeeping.解方程时是两边同时加；化函数为顶点式时是在同一边加了再减。思路一样，记账方式不同。
  • If the right side ends up negative, there is no real solution — you can stop.若右边最后是负数，就没有实数解，可以停了。
x2+bx=k ⇒ (x+2b​)2=k+(2b​)2
Completing the square in an equation方程中的配方
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Adding (2b​)2 to the left side only. In an equation it must go on both sides.只在左边加 (2b​)2。解方程时两边都要加。
  • Completing the square before dividing by a. With 2x2−12x, the half-of-b trick only works after you make the x2 coefficient 1.还没除以 a 就配方。2x2−12x 要先让 x2 的系数变成 1，「取 b 的一半」才管用。
  • Squaring half of b wrong with fractions: half of 35​ is 65​, not 35​⋅2.分数取一半出错：35​ 的一半是 65​，不是乘以 2。

🎯 If a question says "by completing the square", the formula alone earns little credit — show the "add to both sides" line. Use the formula afterwards as a check.题目写明「用配方法」时，只用公式几乎拿不到分——要写出「两边同时加」那一行。之后可以用公式验算。

The quadratic formula求根公式

  • Put the equation in the form ax2+bx+c=0 first (everything on one side, zero on the other). Then read off a, b, c — signs included.先把方程整理成 ax2+bx+c=0（全部移到一边，另一边是 0），再读出 a、b、c，连同符号一起读。
  • The formula is just completing the square done once, in general. That is why it always works.这个公式就是把配方法一般化地做了一遍，所以它永远管用。
  • The ± gives two answers. Simplify the radical, then divide EVERY term on top by the bottom — or factor first and cancel.± 给出两个解。先化简根式，再把分子的每一项都除以分母——或者先提公因式再约分。
x=2a−b±b2−4ac​​
Quadratic formula (for ax2+bx+c=0, a=0)求根公式（适用于 ax2+bx+c=0，a=0）
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Dividing only part of the top by 2a: 2−6±25​​ is −3±5​, not −6±5​.只把分子的一部分除以 2a：2−6±25​​ 等于 −3±5​，不是 −6±5​。
  • Squaring a negative b wrong on the calculator: type (−3)2 with brackets, or just remember that b2 is never negative.计算器上平方负数出错：(−3)2 要加括号，或者直接记住 b2 永远非负。
  • Using the formula before moving everything to one side — in x2=3x+1, c is −1, not 1.还没把所有项移到一边就套公式——x2=3x+1 中 c 是 −1，不是 1。

🎯 If b2−4ac is a perfect square, the quadratic factors — factoring is faster and less error-prone. Use the formula when it is not, or when a question says "exact" or "to the nearest hundredth".如果 b2−4ac 是完全平方数，方程一定能分解，分解更快、更不容易错。不是完全平方，或题目要求「精确解」「保留两位小数」时再用公式。

The discriminant判别式

  • The discriminant is D=b2−4ac, the part under the root in the quadratic formula. It tells you how many real roots there are before you solve.判别式 D=b2−4ac，就是求根公式里根号下面的部分。不用解方程，就能知道有几个实数根。
  • D>0: two different real roots (graph crosses the x-axis twice). D=0: two equal real roots, i.e. one root (vertex touches the x-axis). D<0: no real roots (graph misses the x-axis).D>0：两个不相等的实数根（图象与 x 轴有两个交点）。D=0：两个相等的实数根，即一个根（顶点碰到 x 轴）。D<0：没有实数根（图象与 x 轴不相交）。
  • If D is a perfect square (0,1,4,9,…), the roots are rational and the quadratic factors.若 D 是完全平方数（0,1,4,9,…），根是有理数，方程可以因式分解。
  • To find k: write D in terms of k, then set D=0, D>0 or D<0 as the question asks, and solve for k.求 k：先用 k 表示 D，再按题意令 D=0、D>0 或 D<0，解出 k。
D=b2−4ac
Discriminant of ax2+bx+c=0ax2+bx+c=0 的判别式
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Using b=k instead of b=k−2 in x2+(k−2)x+4. The whole bracket is b, so square the whole bracket.在 x2+(k−2)x+4 中把 b 当成 k。整个括号才是 b，要把整个括号平方。
  • Saying "D=0 means no solution". D=0 means exactly one (repeated) root; only D<0 means no real roots.说「D=0 无解」。D=0 是恰好一个（重）根，只有 D<0 才没有实数根。
  • Forgetting a=0: in kx2+6x+1=0, k=0 makes it linear, so it cannot have two roots.忘了 a=0：kx2+6x+1=0 中若 k=0 就成了一次方程，不可能有两个根。

🎯 "Nature of the roots" questions want D calculated and a sentence. For "find k" questions, write the inequality first, then solve — and for k2<64 remember the answer is between −8 and 8, not just k<8.「判断根的情况」要写出 D 的计算和一句结论。「求 k」先写不等式再解——k2<64 的答案是 −8 与 8 之间，不只是 k<8。

Choosing a method & word problems选方法与应用题

  • Pick the fastest method: a ready-made square → square roots; D is a perfect square → factor; a=1 with even b → complete the square; anything else → quadratic formula; decimals or a picture wanted → graph and read the x-intercepts.选最快的方法：有现成平方 → 开平方；D 是完全平方数 → 分解；a=1 且 b 为偶数 → 配方；其他情况 → 求根公式；要小数或有图 → 画图读 x 截距。
  • Word problems: (1) let the variable be what you are asked for; (2) write the equation from the words (area, Pythagoras, height = value); (3) solve; (4) reject roots that make no sense (negative length or time).应用题：(1) 设所求量为未知数；(2) 根据题意列方程（面积、勾股定理、高度等于某值）；(3) 解方程；(4) 舍去不合实际的根（负的长度或时间）。
  • Both roots can be valid: a ball passes a height once going up and once coming down.两个根也可能都有效：球上升时经过某高度一次，下落时又经过一次。
x=2a−b±b2−4ac​​
Works for every quadratic对所有二次方程都适用
a2+b2=c2
Pythagorean theorem (right-triangle problems)勾股定理（直角三角形问题）
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Adding the frame width once: a frame of width x adds 2x to each dimension (one x on each side).相框宽只加了一次：宽为 x 的相框让每个方向增加 2x（两边各一个 x）。
  • Keeping a negative length or time in the final answer. Solve the equation fully, then reject with a reason.最后答案里留着负的长度或时间。先把方程完整解出来，再写理由舍去。
  • Throwing away a valid second root: if both times are positive and the question asks "when", give both.把有效的第二个根也扔了：两个时间都为正、题目问「什么时候」时，两个都要写。

🎯 Check D first — 10 seconds tells you whether to factor or reach for the formula. In word problems, always end with a sentence and units; marks are often lost for "x=4" with no "the frame is 4 cm wide".先看 D——10 秒钟就知道该分解还是用公式。应用题最后一定写一句带单位的话；只写「x=4」而不写「相框宽 4 cm」常常被扣分。
