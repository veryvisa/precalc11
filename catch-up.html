🧭 Catch-up path: if you missed about 4 weeks补课路线：落下约 4 周怎么办

A calm, time-boxed way back into class. You do not have to learn everything you missed — only what the class is using now, in the order it needs it. Each topic is one 25-minute block: a 5-question check first, study only what you missed, then check again.一条平静、有时间框的回班路线。你不用补完落下的所有内容，只补班里现在要用的，并按需要的先后顺序来。每个知识点是一个 25 分钟的小段：先做 5 道题的小测，只学错了的部分，再测一次。
