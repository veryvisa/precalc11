Non-permissible values & simplifying不允许值与化简

  • A rational expression is a fraction of polynomials. Its non-permissible values (NPVs) are the x-values that make the DENOMINATOR zero. Factor the denominator, set each factor =0.有理式是多项式之比。它的不允许值就是让分母为 0 的 x。把分母分解，令每个因式 =0。
  • Find NPVs from the ORIGINAL expression, before cancelling. A cancelled factor still counts: the simplified form must carry the restriction.不允许值要从原式找，在约分之前。约掉的因式照样算数：化简后的式子必须带着限制条件。
  • To simplify: factor top and bottom completely, then cancel common FACTORS (never terms).化简：分子分母都完全分解，再约去公因式（只能约因式，不能约项）。
  • Opposites cancel to −1: b−aa−b​=−1. Spot pairs like (4−x) and (x−4).互为相反数的因式约成 −1：b−aa−b​=−1。注意 (4−x) 与 (x−4) 这种配对。
BCAC​=BA​,B=0, C=0
Cancelling a common factor约去公因式
b−aa−b​=−1,a=b
Opposite factors相反因式
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Cancelling terms instead of factors: x+3x+6​=36​. Only something MULTIPLIED on top and bottom can cancel.约掉的是项不是因式：x+3x+6​=36​。只有分子分母中「相乘」的公因式才能约。
  • Finding NPVs after simplifying — the cancelled factor’s restriction gets lost. Always state them from the original.化简之后才找不允许值——约掉的因式的限制就丢了。一定从原式写出。
  • Setting the NUMERATOR to zero for NPVs. A zero numerator is fine (the expression is just 0).令分子为 0 求不允许值。分子为 0 没问题（式子的值就是 0）。

🎯 Write the NPVs on the first line, straight from the factored denominator, before you cancel anything. Answers without NPVs usually lose a mark even if the simplification is right.在第一行就从分解后的分母写出不允许值，然后再约分。即使化简正确，漏写不允许值通常也要扣分。

Multiplying & dividing有理式乘除

  • Multiply: factor every numerator and denominator, cancel common factors (top of one with bottom of any), then multiply what is left.乘法：把每个分子分母都分解，约去公因式（任一分子与任一分母之间都可以约），再把剩下的相乘。
  • Divide: multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction ("keep, change, flip"), then proceed as multiplication.除法：乘以第二个分式的倒数（「前不变、除变乘、后颠倒」），再按乘法做。
  • NPVs for division come from THREE places: both original denominators AND the numerator of the divisor (it ends up on the bottom after flipping).除法的不允许值来自三处：两个原分母，以及除式的分子（颠倒后它跑到了分母）。
BA​⋅DC​=BDAC​
Multiplying (B,D=0)分式相乘（B,D=0）
BA​÷DC​=BA​⋅CD​
Dividing (B,C,D=0)分式相除（B,C,D=0）
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Flipping the wrong fraction, or flipping before writing NPVs. Write NPVs first, then flip only the SECOND fraction.颠倒错了分式，或者颠倒后才写不允许值。先写不允许值，再只颠倒第二个分式。
  • Missing the NPVs from the divisor’s numerator — in ÷x+1x−5​, x=5 too.漏掉除式分子带来的不允许值——÷x+1x−5​ 中还要 x=5。
  • Cancelling across a plus sign: in xx+3​, nothing cancels.跨加号约分：xx+3​ 中什么都不能约。

🎯 Division questions are where NPV marks are lost: list the restrictions BEFORE you flip, from all three denominators-to-be.除法题最容易丢不允许值的分：在颠倒之前，从三个（将要成为的）分母中写全限制条件。

Adding & subtracting有理式加减

  • Factor every denominator. The lowest common denominator (LCD) uses each different factor the greatest number of times it appears in any one denominator.先把每个分母分解。最简公分母（LCD）由各个不同因式组成，每个因式取它在单个分母中出现的最高次数。
  • Rewrite each fraction over the LCD by multiplying its top and bottom by the missing factors. Then add or subtract the numerators only.把每个分式的分子分母同乘所缺的因式，化成以 LCD 为分母。然后只把分子相加减。
  • Subtracting a fraction subtracts its WHOLE numerator: put it in brackets. −(x−2)=−x+2.减去一个分式，就是减去它的整个分子：一定加括号。−(x−2)=−x+2。
  • Finish by factoring the numerator and cancelling if possible. State the NPVs from the original denominators.最后把分子分解，能约就约。不允许值由原来的分母给出。
BA​±DC​=BDAD±BC​
Adding/subtracting (use the LCD when the denominators share factors)分式加减（分母有公因式时用最简公分母）
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Adding denominators: x1​+y1​=x+y2​. Only numerators are added, over a common denominator.把分母也相加：x1​+y1​=x+y2​。只有通分后的分子相加。
  • Not distributing the minus: x+1x​−x+1x−2​=x+1x−x+2​=x+12​, not x+1−2​.减号没分配：x+1x​−x+1x−2​=x+1x−x+2​=x+12​，不是 x+1−2​。
  • Using the product of denominators when they share a factor — it works, but leaves a big expression you then have to simplify.分母有公因式时仍直接相乘作公分母——也能做，但会留下很大的式子，还得再化简。

🎯 Most errors happen in the numerator after the LCD is set up. Write the full numerator with brackets on one line, expand on the next, and test with x=0 (or any allowed value) at the end.大多数错误发生在通分之后的分子里。先在一行写出带括号的完整分子，下一行展开，最后用 x=0（或任何允许值）代入检验。

Rational equations & applications分式方程与应用题

  • Write the NPVs first. Then multiply EVERY term on both sides by the LCD — the fractions disappear and you solve an ordinary linear or quadratic equation.先写不允许值。然后把两边的每一项都乘以最简公分母——分母消失，剩下普通的一次或二次方程。
  • Any answer equal to an NPV is extraneous: reject it. If every answer is rejected, the equation has no solution.等于不允许值的答案就是增根，要舍去。如果所有答案都被舍去，方程无解。
  • Work problems: a job done in t hours means a rate of t1​ job per hour. Rates working together add: t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​.工程问题：t 小时完成一件工作，效率就是每小时 t1​。合作时效率相加：t1​1​+t2​1​=T1​。
  • Travel problems: time =speeddistance​. Build the equation from the times (e.g. "one trip took 1 h less").行程问题：时间 =速度路程​。用时间列方程（如「这一趟少用 1 小时」）。

Work together (time alone t1​,t2​; together T)合作工程（单独用时 t1​,t2​；合作用时 T）

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Multiplying only the fractions by the LCD and forgetting whole-number terms: in 1+x2−412​=…, the 1 becomes x2−4.只给分式乘最简公分母，忘了整数项：1+x2−412​=… 里的 1 要变成 x2−4。
  • Not comparing answers with the NPVs. Write the NPVs on line 1 so you remember to check against them.不拿答案对照不允许值。第一行就写不允许值，才记得核对。
  • Adding times instead of rates in work problems: together is NOT t1​+t2​; it is faster than either one alone.工程问题把时间相加而不是效率相加：合作用时不是 t1​+t2​，而是比任何一个单独都快。

🎯 Exam word problems are built so one root is negative or an NPV. Define your variable in words ("let t = hours for the small hose"), and end with a sentence answer — both are usually marked.考试应用题通常设计成有一个根是负数或不允许值。用文字定义变量（「设小水管需 t 小时」），最后用一句话作答——这两处一般都有分。
