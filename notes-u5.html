Graphs & characteristics图象与特征

  • A quadratic function is f(x)=ax2+bx+c with a=0. Its graph is a parabola: opens up if a>0 (has a minimum), opens down if a<0 (has a maximum).二次函数是 f(x)=ax2+bx+c（a=0），图象是抛物线：a>0 开口向上，有最小值；a<0 开口向下，有最大值。
  • The vertex sits on the axis of symmetry x=−2ab​, exactly halfway between the x-intercepts. Plug that x back in to get the max or min value.顶点在对称轴 x=−2ab​ 上，正好在两个 x 截距的正中间。把这个 x 代回去，就得到最大值或最小值。
  • Domain is always all real numbers. Range stops at the vertex: y≥q if it opens up, y≤q if it opens down (where q is the y-value of the vertex).定义域永远是全体实数。值域到顶点为止：开口向上是 y≥q，开口向下是 y≤q（q 是顶点的纵坐标）。
  • y-intercept: set x=0, so it is just c. x-intercepts (zeros, roots): set f(x)=0 and solve. f(3) means "the y-value when x=3".y 截距：令 x=0，就是 c。x 截距（零点、根）：令 f(x)=0 解方程。f(3) 的意思是「x=3 时的 y 值」。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Getting the sign of the axis wrong: for y=x2+6x, x=−26​=−3, not 3. Write the minus sign in front before you plug in.对称轴符号算反：y=x2+6x 的对称轴是 x=−26​=−3，不是 3。代数之前先把前面的负号写上。
  • Writing the range with x or using the x-value of the vertex. Range is about y and uses the vertex y-value: vertex (2,−2) opening up gives y≥−2.值域写成关于 x 的，或者用了顶点的横坐标。值域讲的是 y，用顶点纵坐标：顶点 (2,−2) 开口向上，值域 y≥−2。
  • Evaluating f(−3) for f(x)=−x2 as 9. Brackets first: −(−3)2=−9.把 f(x)=−x2 的 f(−3) 算成 9。要先算括号：−(−3)2=−9。

🎯 Find the axis first — it gives the vertex, the max/min and the range in two lines. When the quadratic factors, the midpoint of the zeros is the fastest axis.先求对称轴，两行就能得到顶点、最值和值域。能分解时，用两个零点的中点求对称轴最快。

Vertex form & transformations顶点式与图象变换

  • Vertex form: y=a(x−p)2+q. The vertex is (p,q), the axis is x=p, and the max/min value is q.顶点式：y=a(x−p)2+q。顶点是 (p,q)，对称轴是 x=p，最值是 q。
  • p slides the graph left/right — opposite to the sign you see: (x−3)2 moves right 3, (x+3)2 moves left 3. q slides up/down with its own sign.p 让图象左右平移，方向与看到的符号相反：(x−3)2 向右 3，(x+3)2 向左 3。q 上下平移，方向与符号一致。
  • a is the shape: ∣a∣>1 narrower (vertical stretch), 0<∣a∣<1 wider, a<0 reflected in the x-axis. Step pattern from the vertex: over 1 up a, over 2 up 4a, over 3 up 9a.a 决定形状：∣a∣>1 变窄（竖直拉伸），0<∣a∣<1 变宽，a<0 关于 x 轴翻折。从顶点出发的步长规律：横走 1 竖走 a，横走 2 竖走 4a，横走 3 竖走 9a。
  • Number of x-intercepts without solving: if a and q have opposite signs → 2; q=0 → 1; same signs → 0.不用解方程就知道 x 截距个数：a 与 q 异号 → 2 个；q=0 → 1 个；同号 → 0 个。

(x,y) → (x+p, ay+q)

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Reading the vertex of y=(x+4)2−1 as (4,−1). The bracket flips the sign: vertex (−4,−1).把 y=(x+4)2−1 的顶点读成 (4,−1)。括号里的符号要反过来：顶点是 (−4,−1)。
  • Calling a=21​ a "stretch by 2". It is a vertical stretch by a factor of 21​ — the graph gets wider, not narrower.把 a=21​ 说成「拉伸 2 倍」。正确说法是竖直方向伸缩为原来的 21​，图象变宽而不是变窄。
  • When finding a, substituting the vertex instead of the other point. The vertex is already built in; use the second point.求 a 时把顶点再代一遍。顶点已经写进式子里了，要代另一个点。

🎯 Multiple-choice questions love to test the sign of p. Say it out loud: "x minus p". To write an equation from a graph, read the vertex first, then use one clean grid point to get a.选择题最爱考 p 的符号，心里默念「x 减 p」。从图象写方程时，先读顶点，再找一个正好落在格点上的点求 a。

Standard form → vertex form一般式化顶点式

  • Completing the square uses x2+bx+(2b​)2=(x+2b​)2. Add (2b​)2 and subtract it right away, so the value does not change.配方用的是 x2+bx+(2b​)2=(x+2b​)2。加上 (2b​)2 后立刻减去它，式子的值不变。
  • If a=1, factor a out of the x2 and x terms only. The number you subtract must then be multiplied by a when it leaves the bracket.a=1 时，只把 x2 项和 x 项的 a 提出来。括号里减去的那个数，拿出括号时要乘以 a。
  • Check fast: the vertex x-value must equal −2ab​, and the vertex y-value must equal f(−2ab​).快速验算：顶点横坐标应等于 −2ab​，纵坐标应等于 f(−2ab​)。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Forgetting to multiply by a when the subtracted number leaves the bracket: 2(x2−6x+9−9) gives −18 outside, not −9.减去的数拿出括号时忘了乘 a：2(x2−6x+9−9) 拿出来是 −18，不是 −9。
  • Factoring a out of the constant too. Only the x2 and x terms go in the bracket; c stays outside unchanged.把常数项也提了 a。只有 x2 项和 x 项进括号，c 原样留在外面。
  • Losing the sign when a is negative: −2x2+6x=−2(x2−3x), not −2(x2+3x).a 为负时丢了符号：−2x2+6x=−2(x2−3x)，不是 −2(x2+3x)。

🎯 Tests often ask for vertex form "by completing the square" — show the add-and-subtract step even if you found the vertex with −2ab​. Use the shortcut afterwards as a 10-second check.考试常要求「用配方法」化顶点式，即使你用 −2ab​ 找到了顶点，也要写出加减配方的步骤。捷径留作 10 秒钟的验算。

Factored form & writing equations交点式与求解析式

  • Factored form: y=a(x−r)(x−s). The x-intercepts are r and s — again, opposite to the signs in the brackets.交点式：y=a(x−r)(x−s)。x 截距是 r 和 s——同样与括号里的符号相反。
  • The axis is halfway: x=2r+s​. Plug it in to get the vertex height. If the graph only touches the x-axis at r, use y=a(x−r)2.对称轴在正中间：x=2r+s​，代回去得到顶点高度。若图象只在 r 处碰到 x 轴，就用 y=a(x−r)2。
  • Writing an equation: put the intercepts in, substitute one more point (often the y-intercept or the vertex) to find a.求解析式：先把截距填进去，再代入另一个点（常用 y 截距或顶点）求 a。
  • Pick the form for the job: standard → y-intercept c; vertex → max/min and transformations; factored → zeros.按需选形式：一般式看 y 截距 c；顶点式看最值和变换；交点式看零点。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Reading the intercepts of y=(x+3)(x−2) as 3 and −2. Set each bracket to 0: x=−3 and x=2.把 y=(x+3)(x−2) 的截距读成 3 和 −2。令每个括号为 0：x=−3、x=2。
  • Forgetting a: two parabolas can share the same zeros. Always use a third point to find a.忘了 a：零点相同的抛物线有无数条，一定要用第三个点求 a。
  • Factoring 2x2−4x−16 as (x+2)(x−4) and dropping the 2. The common factor stays in front: 2(x+2)(x−4).把 2x2−4x−16 分解成 (x+2)(x−4)，丢了 2。公因数要留在前面：2(x+2)(x−4)。

🎯 When a graph question gives two clean x-intercepts, start with factored form — it saves a system of equations. Convert to the form the question asks for at the end.图象题给出两个整齐的 x 截距时，先用交点式，可以省掉解方程组。最后再换成题目要求的形式。

Max/min problems最值应用题

  • Any "largest/smallest/maximum/minimum/optimal" question with a squared quantity is a vertex question. The answer to "when/what value" is the x-coordinate; the answer to "how much" is the y-coordinate.凡是带平方关系、问「最大/最小/最多/最优」的题，都是求顶点。「何时/取什么值」答横坐标，「是多少」答纵坐标。
  • Build the function first: name the variable, write the quantity to optimize as a product (area = length × width, revenue = price × number sold).先建函数：设好变量，把要优化的量写成乘积（面积 = 长 × 宽，收入 = 单价 × 销量）。
  • Find the vertex the fastest way: x=−2ab​, completing the square, or the midpoint of the zeros of the factored product.用最快的方法找顶点：x=−2ab​、配方，或者乘积形式里两个零点的中点。
x=−2ab​
Where the max/min happens取到最值的位置
R=(price)×(quantity)
Revenue收入 = 单价 × 销量
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Answering with the wrong coordinate: "When is the height greatest?" wants t; "What is the greatest height?" wants h. Read the question twice.答错坐标：「什么时候最高」问的是 t，「最高是多少」问的是 h。题目读两遍。
  • In revenue problems, giving n=5 as the price. n is the number of increases; the price is 20+n.收入题里把 n=5 当成票价。n 是涨价次数，票价是 20+n。
  • Using perimeter 2l+2w when one side is a river or wall. Only count the sides that actually need fence.一边靠河或靠墙时还用周长 2l+2w。只算真正需要篱笆的边。

🎯 Leave the function in "product form" like (20+n)(300−10n) — the zeros are visible, the vertex is their midpoint, and you skip expanding. Always finish with a sentence that answers the question asked, with units.把函数留在 (20+n)(300−10n) 这样的乘积形式：零点一眼可见，顶点就是中点，还省去展开。最后一定写一句带单位、直接回答题问的话。
