📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • GCF first: largest number dividing every coefficient × each variable to its LOWEST power.先提公因式：能整除所有系数的最大数 × 每个字母的最低次幂。
  • A bracket can be the common factor: 2x(x−3)+5(x−3)=(x−3)(2x+5).整个括号也能当公因式：2x(x−3)+5(x−3)=(x−3)(2x+5)。
  • Four terms → group in pairs; opposite brackets: (2−x)=−(x−2).四项式 → 两两分组；括号互为相反数时用 (2−x)=−(x−2)。

💡 x3+2x2−9x−18 has four terms and no obvious pattern — split it into two pairs and it falls apart into (x+2)(x−3)(x+3).x3+2x2−9x−18 有四项、看不出规律——分成两组，它就拆成了 (x+2)(x−3)(x+3)。

  1. Always look for a greatest common factor FIRST: the largest number that divides every coefficient, times each variable to its LOWEST power.永远先找最大公因式：能整除所有系数的最大数，乘以每个字母的最低次幂。
  2. A common factor can be a whole bracket: 2x(x−3)+5(x−3)=(x−3)(2x+5). Treat (x−3) like a single letter.公因式也可以是整个括号：2x(x−3)+5(x−3)=(x−3)(2x+5)。把 (x−3) 当成一个字母看待。
  3. Four terms → try grouping: pair them, take a GCF from each pair, and the brackets should match. If they are opposites like (x−2) and (2−x), factor out −1.四项式 → 试分组：两两分组，各提公因式，括号应该相同。如果是 (x−2) 与 (2−x) 这种相反式，提出 −1。
  4. Check by expanding — it takes ten seconds and catches every missing term.展开验算——十秒钟就能抓出漏掉的项。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Count the terms. For each term, note the number and the power of each letter. Is the same number, letter or bracket in EVERY term?数一数有几项。每一项记下数字和每个字母的次数。有没有哪个数、字母或括号在「每一项」里都出现？
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    The expression written as a product, factored FULLY: nothing common is left and no bracket can be factored again.把式子写成乘积，而且要「完全」分解：没有公因式剩下，也没有哪个括号还能再分解。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    A box: the GCF goes outside, and each term divided by the GCF goes inside, one per cell. For four terms, draw a line between the first two and the last two.画一个框：公因式写在框外，每一项除以公因式的结果写在框里，一项一格。四项式就在前两项和后两项之间画一条竖线。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    GCF = the biggest number that divides every coefficient × each letter to its LOWEST power; divide every term by it. Four terms: factor each pair so that the two brackets match (take out a negative if the third term is negative), then take the bracket out.公因式＝能整除所有系数的最大数 × 每个字母的「最低」次幂；每一项都除以它。四项式：两组各提公因式，让两个括号相同（第三项是负的就提负号），再把括号提出来。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Expand your answer: it must give back every original term, signs included. Then look at each bracket: is it a difference of squares?把答案展开：必须得回原来的每一项，符号也要一样。再看每个括号：是不是平方差？

🧱 Prerequisite check先修检查

4 quick Grade 9–10 questions, about 3 minutes. Miss one and a 5-minute repair card opens right there; fix it, then carry on.4 道九、十年级小题，约 3 分钟。错了就在原地打开 5 分钟补救卡，补好再继续。

1Exponent rules (product, quotient, power)指数运算法则（同底乘、除、乘方）
Simplify x2x5​.化简 x2x5​。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Exponent rules (product, quotient, power)指数运算法则（同底乘、除、乘方） (Grade年级 9)
Same base: multiply → ADD exponents (x3x4=x7); divide → SUBTRACT (x7÷x2=x5); power of a power → MULTIPLY ((x3)2=x6). A power of a product hits every factor: (2x)3=8x3.同底数：相乘→指数相加（x3x4=x7）；相除→指数相减（x7÷x2=x5）；幂的乘方→指数相乘（(x3)2=x6）。积的乘方每个因数都要乘方：(2x)3=8x3。
  1. (3x2)2⋅x=9x4⋅x=9x5.(3x2)2⋅x=9x4⋅x=9x5。
  2. 2x26x5​=3x3 (divide the numbers, subtract the exponents).2x26x5​=3x3（系数相除，指数相减）。
Try one:试一道：
Simplify 4x(2x3)2​.化简 4x(2x3)2​。

2Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号）
Expand −3(x−4).展开 −3(x−4)。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号） (Grade年级 8–9)
Multiply EVERY term inside the bracket: 3(x−4)=3x−12. A minus in front changes every sign inside: −(x−5)=−x+5, and 2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1.括号里的每一项都要乘到：3(x−4)=3x−12。前面是负号时，括号里每一项都变号：−(x−5)=−x+5，2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1。
  1. −2(3x−1)=−6x+2 (the −1 becomes +2).−2(3x−1)=−6x+2（−1 变成 +2）。
  2. x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6.x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6。
Try one:试一道：
Expand and simplify 4x−(2x−7).去括号并化简 4x−(2x−7)。

3Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘
Evaluate (−12)÷(−4).计算 (−12)÷(−4)。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。
Try one:试一道：
Evaluate b2−4ac when a=2, b=−3, c=−1.当 a=2, b=−3, c=−1 时，求 b2−4ac。

4Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）
Factor x2−x−12.分解 x2−x−12。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和） (Grade年级 10)
Take out a common factor first. Then find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b: x2−x−12 → −4 and 3 → (x−4)(x+3). Check by expanding.先提公因式。再找两个数，相乘等于 c、相加等于 b：x2−x−12 → −4 和 3 → (x−4)(x+3)。展开检验。
  1. x2+7x+10: 2×5=10, 2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5).x2+7x+10：2×5=10，2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5)。
  2. If c<0 the two numbers have different signs; if c>0 they share the sign of b.若 c<0，两个数异号；若 c>0，两个数与 b 同号。
Try one:试一道：
Factor x2−2x−15.分解 x2−2x−15。

🪜 Worked → fill in → your turn例题 → 填空 → 自己做

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

① Worked example — read it完整例题——看懂
Factor fully: x3+3x2−4x−12.完全分解：x3+3x2−4x−12。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Four terms and no number or letter common to all four, so try grouping.四项，没有四项都有的数或字母，所以试分组。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    A product of factors that cannot be broken down any further.写成不能再分解的因式乘积。
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Pair them: (x3+3x2)+(−4x−12). First pair: x2(x+3).分组：(x3+3x2)+(−4x−12)。第一组：x2(x+3)。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Second pair: take out −4 (not 4) so the bracket matches: −4(x+3).第二组：提 −4（不是 4），括号才相同：−4(x+3)。
  5. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Take out the bracket: (x+3)(x2−4).提出括号：(x+3)(x2−4)。
  6. ✅ Check检验
    x2−4 is a difference of squares: (x−2)(x+2). Final: (x+3)(x−2)(x+2). Expand to check: it gives back all four terms.x2−4 是平方差：(x−2)(x+2)。最终：(x+3)(x−2)(x+2)。展开检验：四项全部得回。
✔
(x+3)(x−2)(x+2)

Factor fully: x3−5x2−9x+45.完全分解：x3−5x2−9x+45。
  1. 🔎 Given已知

  2. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    First pair: x3−5x2=x2(x−5).第一组：x3−5x2=x2(x−5)。
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    ✍️
    Your turn: factor −9x+45 so that its bracket is also (x−5).轮到你：分解 −9x+45，让它的括号也是 (x−5)。
    −9x+45=−9(x−5). So the whole thing is x2(x−5)−9(x−5)=(x−5)(x2−9).−9x+45=−9(x−5)。于是整个式子是 x2(x−5)−9(x−5)=(x−5)(x2−9)。
  4. ✅ Check检验
    ✍️
    Your turn: is (x−5)(x2−9) finished? Factor any bracket that still breaks down.轮到你：(x−5)(x2−9) 分解完了吗？还能分解的括号继续分解。

Factor 6x3y−9x2y2+3x2y.分解因式 6x3y−9x2y2+3x2y。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Numbers: GCF of 6, 9, 3 is 3. Variables: lowest powers are x2 and y. GCF =3x2y.数字：6、9、3 的最大公因数是 3。字母：最低次幂是 x2 和 y。公因式 =3x2y。
  2. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Divide each term: 3x2y6x3y​=2x, 3x2y−9x2y2​=−3y, 3x2y3x2y​=1.逐项相除：3x2y6x3y​=2x，3x2y−9x2y2​=−3y，3x2y3x2y​=1。

    3x2y(2x−3y+1). The last term leaves 1, not 0.3x2y(2x−3y+1)。最后一项剩下的是 1，不是 0。

🔍 Common mistakes常见错误

Someone made each of these. Tap the line where it first goes wrong.每道题都有人这样做过。点出第一处出错的那一行。

1Taking out a factor that is not the greatest提出的不是最大公因式
Factor 12x3−18x2.分解因式 12x3−18x2。

🧠 Both terms have at least x2, so x2 (the LOWEST power) belongs in the GCF. The bracket 2x2−3x still has a common x, which shows you stopped too early.两项都至少含 x2，所以公因式里要放 x2（取「最低」次幂）。括号 2x2−3x 里还有公因式 x，说明停早了。

🔧 GCF =6x2: 12x3−18x2=6x2(2x−3).公因式 =6x2：12x3−18x2=6x2(2x−3)。

✔ 6x2(2x−3)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 D

2Losing the 1 left by a term equal to the GCF和公因式相同的项丢了 1
Factor 8x2y+4xy.分解因式 8x2y+4xy。

🧠 4xy÷4xy=1, not 0. Dropping it leaves one term where there were two; expanding 4xy(2x) gives only 8x2y.4xy÷4xy=1，不是 0。丢掉它就从两项变成了一项；展开 4xy(2x) 只得到 8x2y。

🔧 8x2y+4xy=4xy(2x+1).8x2y+4xy=4xy(2x+1)。

✔ 4xy(2x+1)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q1 B

3A sign slip in the second pair第二组符号出错
Factor x3+4x2−5x−20.分解因式 x3+4x2−5x−20。

🧠 −5×(−4)=+20, not −20. Dividing −20 by −5 gives +4, so the bracket is (x+4).−5×(−4)=+20，不是 −20。−20÷(−5)=+4，括号应是 (x+4)。

🔧 x2(x+4)−5(x+4)=(x+4)(x2−5). (x2−5 does not factor with whole numbers.)x2(x+4)−5(x+4)=(x+4)(x2−5)。（x2−5 不能用整数再分解。）

✔ (x+4)(x2−5)

4Stopping before a difference of squares平方差没分解就停了
Factor fully: x3−2x2−16x+32.完全分解：x3−2x2−16x+32。

🧠 The grouping is right, but "fully" means checking every bracket again. x2−16 is a difference of squares.分组没错，但「完全分解」要把每个括号再检查一遍。x2−16 是平方差。

🔧 x2−16=(x−4)(x+4), so the answer is (x−2)(x−4)(x+4).x2−16=(x−4)(x+4)，所以答案是 (x−2)(x−4)(x+4)。

✔ (x−2)(x−4)(x+4)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 A

5Treating opposite brackets as the same把相反的括号当成相同
Factor 5x(x−3)+2(3−x).分解因式 5x(x−3)+2(3−x)。

🧠 (3−x) is not (x−3); it is −(x−3). So +2(3−x)=−2(x−3), and the sign of the 2 changes.(3−x) 不是 (x−3)，而是 −(x−3)。所以 +2(3−x)=−2(x−3)，2 前面的符号要变。

🔧 5x(x−3)−2(x−3)=(x−3)(5x−2). Check with x=0: original =6, answer =(−3)(−2)=6 ✓.5x(x−3)−2(x−3)=(x−3)(5x−2)。用 x=0 检验：原式 =6，答案 =(−3)(−2)=6 ✓。

✔ (x−3)(5x−2)

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Taking out a common factor that is not the GREATEST: 4x2+8x=2x(2x+4) is not finished — 4x(x+2) is.提出的不是最大公因式：4x2+8x=2x(2x+4) 还没分解完，应为 4x(x+2)。
  • Losing a term that equals the GCF: 3x2y divided by itself is 1, which must stay in the bracket.某项正好等于公因式时把它丢了：3x2y 除以自己得 1，括号里必须保留。
  • Sign slip when grouping: −9x−18=−9(x+2), not −9(x−2).分组时符号出错：−9x−18=−9(x+2)，不是 −9(x−2)。
