📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • y=a(x−r)(x−s): zeros r,s (opposite signs to the brackets); axis x=2r+s​.y=a(x−r)(x−s)：零点 r,s（与括号里符号相反）；对称轴 x=2r+s​。
  • Zeros alone do not fix the parabola: always use a third point to find a.只有零点定不出抛物线：一定要用第三个点求 a。
  • Touches the x-axis only at r: y=a(x−r)2.只在 r 处与 x 轴相切：y=a(x−r)2。

💡 You see where a parabola crosses the x-axis and one other point on it. That is enough to write its exact equation in one line.只要知道抛物线和 x 轴的两个交点，再加上它上面的任意一点，一行就能写出它的精确方程。

  1. Factored form: y=a(x−r)(x−s). The x-intercepts are r and s — again, opposite to the signs in the brackets.交点式：y=a(x−r)(x−s)。x 截距是 r 和 s——同样与括号里的符号相反。
  2. The axis is halfway: x=2r+s​. Plug it in to get the vertex height. If the graph only touches the x-axis at r, use y=a(x−r)2.对称轴在正中间：x=2r+s​，代回去得到顶点高度。若图象只在 r 处碰到 x 轴，就用 y=a(x−r)2。
  3. Writing an equation: put the intercepts in, substitute one more point (often the y-intercept or the vertex) to find a.求解析式：先把截距填进去，再代入另一个点（常用 y 截距或顶点）求 a。
  4. Pick the form for the job: standard → y-intercept c; vertex → max/min and transformations; factored → zeros.按需选形式：一般式看 y 截距 c；顶点式看最值和变换；交点式看零点。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Write the zeros (x-intercepts) and the one extra point. A graph that only touches the x-axis gives a double zero.写下零点（x 截距）和多给的那个点。图象只碰到 x 轴的，是一个重零点。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Which form is wanted: factored, vertex or standard? Or is it the vertex or the max/min value?题目要哪种形式：交点式、顶点式还是一般式？还是要顶点或最值？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Mark the zeros on the x-axis and the axis halfway between them. If the extra point is above the x-axis between the zeros, the parabola opens down, so expect a<0.在 x 轴上标出零点，再在中间画对称轴。如果多给的点在两个零点之间且在 x 轴上方，抛物线开口向下，a 应为负。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Write y=a(x−r)(x−s) with the bracket signs opposite to the zeros, put in the extra point and solve for a.写出 y=a(x−r)(x−s)，括号里的符号与零点相反；代入多给的点，解出 a。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    The extra point must fit, and the sign of a must match your sketch. Expand only at the end if standard form is asked.多给的点必须满足方程，a 的符号要和草图一致。题目要一般式时，最后再展开。

🧱 Prerequisite check先修检查

5 quick Grade 9–10 questions, about 3 minutes. Miss one and a 5-minute repair card opens right there; fix it, then carry on.5 道九、十年级小题，约 3 分钟。错了就在原地打开 5 分钟补救卡，补好再继续。

1Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）
Solve (x+4)(x−3)=0.解 (x+4)(x−3)=0。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和） (Grade年级 10)
Take out a common factor first. Then find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b: x2−x−12 → −4 and 3 → (x−4)(x+3). Check by expanding.先提公因式。再找两个数，相乘等于 c、相加等于 b：x2−x−12 → −4 和 3 → (x−4)(x+3)。展开检验。
  1. x2+7x+10: 2×5=10, 2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5).x2+7x+10：2×5=10，2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5)。
  2. If c<0 the two numbers have different signs; if c>0 they share the sign of b.若 c<0，两个数异号；若 c>0，两个数与 b 同号。
Try one:试一道：
Factor x2−2x−15.分解 x2−2x−15。

2Solving a linear equation解一元一次方程
Solve 12=−6a.解 12=−6a。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Solving a linear equation解一元一次方程 (Grade年级 8–9)
Do the same thing to both sides. Clear brackets, collect x on one side and numbers on the other, then divide. With fractions, multiply every term by the common denominator first.两边做同样的事。先去括号，把含 x 的项移到一边、数字移到另一边，最后除以系数。有分数时先把每一项都乘以公分母。
  1. 3(x−2)=x+8 → 3x−6=x+8 → 2x=14 → x=7.3(x−2)=x+8 → 3x−6=x+8 → 2x=14 → x=7。
  2. 2x​+3x​=5: multiply every term by 6 → 3x+2x=30 → x=6.2x​+3x​=5：每项乘 6 → 3x+2x=30 → x=6。
  3. Check by substituting: 3(7−2)=15 and 7+8=15 ✓.代回检验：3(7−2)=15，7+8=15 ✓。
Try one:试一道：
Solve 4x−1​=6x​.解 4x−1​=6x​。

3Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘
Evaluate (1+2)(1−4).求 (1+2)(1−4) 的值。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。
Try one:试一道：
Evaluate b2−4ac when a=2, b=−3, c=−1.当 a=2, b=−3, c=−1 时，求 b2−4ac。

4Fractions: add, subtract, multiply, divide分数的四则运算
What number is halfway between −3 and 7?正好在 −3 和 7 中间的数是多少？

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Fractions: add, subtract, multiply, divide分数的四则运算 (Grade年级 7–9)
Add or subtract only with a common denominator: 21​+31​=63​+62​=65​. Multiply straight across. Divide = multiply by the reciprocal (flip the second fraction).加减必须先通分：21​+31​=63​+62​=65​。乘法分子乘分子、分母乘分母。除以一个分数＝乘它的倒数（把第二个分数倒过来）。
  1. 32​−41​: common denominator 12 → 128​−123​=125​.32​−41​：公分母 12 → 128​−123​=125​。
  2. 43​÷89​=43​⋅98​=3624​=32​.43​÷89​=43​⋅98​=3624​=32​。
  3. Never add denominators: 21​+31​=52​.分母不能相加：21​+31​=52​。
Try one:试一道：
Simplify 65​−41​.化简 65​−41​。

5Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号）
Expand −(x−2)(x−6).展开 −(x−2)(x−6)。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号） (Grade年级 8–9)
Multiply EVERY term inside the bracket: 3(x−4)=3x−12. A minus in front changes every sign inside: −(x−5)=−x+5, and 2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1.括号里的每一项都要乘到：3(x−4)=3x−12。前面是负号时，括号里每一项都变号：−(x−5)=−x+5，2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1。
  1. −2(3x−1)=−6x+2 (the −1 becomes +2).−2(3x−1)=−6x+2（−1 变成 +2）。
  2. x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6.x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6。
Try one:试一道：
Expand and simplify 4x−(2x−7).去括号并化简 4x−(2x−7)。

🪜 Worked → fill in → your turn例题 → 填空 → 自己做

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

① Worked example — read it完整例题——看懂
A parabola has x-intercepts −3 and 1 and y-intercept 6. Find its equation and its vertex.一条抛物线的 x 截距为 −3 和 1，y 截距为 6。求它的方程和顶点。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Zeros −3 and 1, so the brackets are (x+3) and (x−1). Extra point (0,6).零点 −3 和 1，括号是 (x+3) 和 (x−1)。多给的点是 (0,6)。
  2. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    (0,6) is above the x-axis and between the zeros, so the parabola opens down: expect a<0.(0,6) 在 x 轴上方、两个零点之间，所以开口向下：a 应为负。
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    y=a(x+3)(x−1). Put in (0,6): 6=a(3)(−1)=−3a, so a=−2.y=a(x+3)(x−1)。代入 (0,6)：6=a(3)(−1)=−3a，得 a=−2。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Axis x=2−3+1​=−1; y=−2(2)(−2)=8, so the vertex is (−1,8).对称轴 x=2−3+1​=−1；y=−2(2)(−2)=8，顶点 (−1,8)。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    a=−2<0 matches the picture, and −2(0+3)(0−1)=6 ✓.a=−2<0 与草图一致，且 −2(0+3)(0−1)=6 ✓。
✔
y=−2(x+3)(x−1); vertex (−1,8)y=−2(x+3)(x−1)；顶点 (−1,8)

  1. 🔎 Given已知

  2. 🧰 Tool用什么工具

1Reading the zeros with the bracket signs照括号里的符号读零点
Find the x-intercepts and the axis of y=3(x+2)(x−8).求 y=3(x+2)(x−8) 的 x 截距和对称轴。

🧠 The numbers were copied straight out of the brackets. A zero is where a bracket equals 0: x+2=0 gives x=−2.直接把括号里的数抄了出来。零点是让括号等于 0 的值：x+2=0 得 x=−2。

🔧 Zeros −2 and 8; axis x=2−2+8​=3.零点 −2 和 8；对称轴 x=2−2+8​=3。

✔ x-intercepts −2 and 8; axis x=3x 截距 −2 和 8；对称轴 x=3

2Leaving out a漏掉了 a
A parabola has x-intercepts −1 and 3 and passes through (1,−8). Find its equation.一条抛物线的 x 截距为 −1 和 3，且过点 (1,−8)。求它的方程。

🧠 Many parabolas share the same two zeros; they differ only by a. The point (1,−8) was never used, and at x=1 this gives −4, not −8.有无数条抛物线有相同的两个零点，它们只差一个 a。点 (1,−8) 根本没用上，而且这个式子在 x=1 时是 −4，不是 −8。

🔧 y=a(x+1)(x−3); −8=a(2)(−2)=−4a, so a=2.y=a(x+1)(x−3)；−8=a(2)(−2)=−4a，得 a=2。

✔ y=2(x+1)(x−3)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 A

3Dropping a when finding the vertex height求顶点高度时丢了 a
Find the vertex of y=−3(x−1)(x−7).求 y=−3(x−1)(x−7) 的顶点。

🧠 The brackets were worked out, and the −3 in front was forgotten. It multiplies everything.只算了两个括号，忘了前面的 −3。它要乘整个式子。

🔧 y=−3(3)(−3)=27. Opens down, so (4,27) is a maximum.y=−3(3)(−3)=27。开口向下，(4,27) 是最高点。

✔ (4,27)

4Losing the sign when solving for a解 a 时丢了符号
A parabola has x-intercepts 1 and 7 and passes through (5,16). Find its equation.一条抛物线的 x 截距为 1 和 7，且过点 (5,16)。求它的方程。

🧠 (4)(−2) is −8, not 8. A quick picture warns you too: a point above the x-axis between the zeros means the parabola opens down.(4)(−2) 是 −8，不是 8。画个草图也能提醒你：两个零点之间、x 轴上方的点说明开口向下。

🔧 16=−8a, so a=−2.16=−8a，所以 a=−2。

✔ y=−2(x−1)(x−7)

🔗 The same mistake is a wrong option in:同一个错误也是这些题的错误选项： Check-up测评 Q2 D

5Dropping the common factor丢了公因数
Write y=3x2−6x−24 in factored form.把 y=3x2−6x−24 写成交点式。

🧠 The 3 was used to make the trinomial easier, then left behind. Without it the curve is only a third as steep, and every y-value is wrong.提出 3 是为了让三项式好分解，结果把它落下了。没有它，曲线只有原来三分之一陡，每个 y 值都不对。

🔧 Keep the 3 in front: y=3(x−4)(x+2).把 3 留在前面：y=3(x−4)(x+2)。

✔ y=3(x−4)(x+2)

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Reading the intercepts of y=(x+3)(x−2) as 3 and −2. Set each bracket to 0: x=−3 and x=2.把 y=(x+3)(x−2) 的截距读成 3 和 −2。令每个括号为 0：x=−3、x=2。
  • Forgetting a: two parabolas can share the same zeros. Always use a third point to find a.忘了 a：零点相同的抛物线有无数条，一定要用第三个点求 a。
  • Factoring 2x2−4x−16 as (x+2)(x−4) and dropping the 2. The common factor stays in front: 2(x+2)(x−4).把 2x2−4x−16 分解成 (x+2)(x−4)，丢了 2。公因数要留在前面：2(x+2)(x−4)。
