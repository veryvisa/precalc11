📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Optimize = find the vertex. "When / which value" → x-coordinate; "how much" → y-coordinate.求最值 = 求顶点。「何时／取何值」→ 横坐标；「最多／最少是多少」→ 纵坐标。
  • Build the function first: area =lw, revenue = price × number sold.先建函数：面积 = 长 × 宽，收入 = 单价 × 销量。
  • Product of two linear factors: best x = midpoint of their zeros.两个一次因式相乘：最优 x = 两个零点的中点。

💡 Raise the ticket price and fewer people come; lower it and you earn less per ticket. Somewhere in between is the price that earns the most — and it sits at the vertex of a parabola.票价提高，来的人就少；票价降低，每张又赚得少。中间总有一个收入最高的价格——它就在一条抛物线的顶点上。

  1. Any "largest/smallest/maximum/minimum/optimal" question with a squared quantity is a vertex question. The answer to "when/what value" is the x-coordinate; the answer to "how much" is the y-coordinate.凡是带平方关系、问「最大/最小/最多/最优」的题，都是求顶点。「何时/取什么值」答横坐标，「是多少」答纵坐标。
  2. Build the function first: name the variable, write the quantity to optimize as a product (area = length × width, revenue = price × number sold).先建函数：设好变量，把要优化的量写成乘积（面积 = 长 × 宽，收入 = 单价 × 销量）。
  3. Find the vertex the fastest way: x=−2ab​, completing the square, or the midpoint of the zeros of the factored product.用最快的方法找顶点：x=−2ab​、配方，或者乘积形式里两个零点的中点。

🎯 Test tip考试提示
Leave the function in "product form" like (20+n)(300−10n) — the zeros are visible, the vertex is their midpoint, and you skip expanding. Always finish with a sentence that answers the question asked, with units.把函数留在 (20+n)(300−10n) 这样的乘积形式：零点一眼可见，顶点就是中点，还省去展开。最后一定写一句带单位、直接回答题问的话。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    What changes, and by how much each step (price step, sales lost, metres of fence)? Name the variable and say what it counts, e.g. "n = number of 1-dollar increases".什么在变？每一步变多少（涨价幅度、少卖多少、多少米篱笆）？设好变量并说清它数的是什么，比如「n = 涨 1 加元的次数」。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Is it asking WHEN or WHICH value (the x-coordinate of the vertex), HOW MUCH (the y-coordinate), or both? In what units?问的是「何时／取何值」（顶点横坐标）、「是多少」（顶点纵坐标），还是两样都要？单位是什么？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Sketch the shape (a pen against a wall, three widths) or make a small table of n, price and number sold. The graph is a downward parabola; the answer is at the top.画出形状（靠墙的围栏、三条宽），或者列一个 n、单价、销量的小表。图象是开口向下的抛物线，答案在最高点。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Write the quantity as a product of two linear factors, e.g. (price)(number sold) or (width)(length). Find where each factor is zero; the vertex is halfway between. If it is already expanded, use −2ab​.把要求的量写成两个一次因式的乘积，比如（单价）×（销量）或（宽）×（长）。求出每个因式为零的点，顶点就在两点正中间。如果已经展开了，就用 −2ab​。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Try one step either side of your answer: both must give less. Then answer the question asked in a sentence with units, and make sure it fits the story (price and sides positive).在答案两侧各试一步：两边都必须更小。然后用带单位的一句话回答题目真正问的，并看它是否符合情境（单价、边长为正）。

1Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号）
Expand (5+n)(40−2n).展开 (5+n)(40−2n)。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号） (Grade年级 8–9)
Multiply EVERY term inside the bracket: 3(x−4)=3x−12. A minus in front changes every sign inside: −(x−5)=−x+5, and 2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1.括号里的每一项都要乘到：3(x−4)=3x−12。前面是负号时，括号里每一项都变号：−(x−5)=−x+5，2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1。
  1. −2(3x−1)=−6x+2 (the −1 becomes +2).−2(3x−1)=−6x+2（−1 变成 +2）。
  2. x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6.x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6。
Try one:试一道：
Expand and simplify 4x−(2x−7).去括号并化简 4x−(2x−7)。

2Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘
Find −2ab​ when a=−4 and b=24.当 a=−4、b=24 时，求 −2ab​。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。
Try one:试一道：
Evaluate b2−4ac when a=2, b=−3, c=−1.当 a=2, b=−3, c=−1 时，求 b2−4ac。

3Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）
For which values of x is (x+6)(18−3x)=0?x 取什么值时 (x+6)(18−3x)=0？

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和） (Grade年级 10)
Take out a common factor first. Then find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b: x2−x−12 → −4 and 3 → (x−4)(x+3). Check by expanding.先提公因式。再找两个数，相乘等于 c、相加等于 b：x2−x−12 → −4 和 3 → (x−4)(x+3)。展开检验。
  1. x2+7x+10: 2×5=10, 2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5).x2+7x+10：2×5=10，2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5)。
  2. If c<0 the two numbers have different signs; if c>0 they share the sign of b.若 c<0，两个数异号；若 c>0，两个数与 b 同号。
Try one:试一道：
Factor x2−2x−15.分解 x2−2x−15。

4Rearranging a formula公式变形（求某个字母）
A fence satisfies 2w+l=100. Write l in terms of w.篱笆满足 2w+l=100。用 w 表示 l。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Rearranging a formula公式变形（求某个字母） (Grade年级 9–10)
Treat the other letters as numbers and undo operations in reverse order. A=P(1+r) for r: divide by P, then subtract 1: r=PA​−1.把其他字母当成数字，按相反顺序撤销运算。由 A=P(1+r) 求 r：先除以 P，再减 1：r=PA​−1。
  1. y=a(x−p)2+q for a: subtract q, divide by (x−p)2: a=(x−p)2y−q​.由 y=a(x−p)2+q 求 a：减 q，再除以 (x−p)2：a=(x−p)2y−q​。
  2. Substitute numbers first if the question gives them — fewer letters, fewer slips.题目给了数字就先代入——字母越少，越不容易错。
Try one:试一道：
The point (1,5) is on y=a(x−3)2−3. Find a.点 (1,5) 在 y=a(x−3)2−3 上，求 a。

🪜 Worked → fill in → your turn例题 → 填空 → 自己做

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

① Worked example — read it完整例题——看懂
A gym has 400 members paying 50 dollars a month. Each 5-dollar increase in the fee loses 20 members. What fee gives the most monthly revenue, and how much is it?一家健身房有 400 名会员，每月会费 50 加元。会费每涨 5 加元，就流失 20 名会员。会费定多少时月收入最高？最高是多少？
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Let n = number of 5-dollar increases. Fee =50+5n, members =400−20n.设 n = 涨 5 加元的次数。会费 =50+5n，会员 =400−20n。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    We want the fee (from n) and the revenue (the top value).要求的是会费（由 n 得出）和最高收入（顶点的值）。
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    R=(50+5n)(400−20n). Zeros: 50+5n=0 gives n=−10; 400−20n=0 gives n=20.R=(50+5n)(400−20n)。零点：50+5n=0 得 n=−10；400−20n=0 得 n=20。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Halfway: n=2−10+20​=5. Fee 50+25=75 dollars, members 400−100=300, R=75×300=22500 dollars.中点：n=2−10+20​=5。会费 50+25=75 加元，会员 400−100=300，R=75×300=22500 加元。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    n=4: 70×320=22400; n=6: 80×280=22400. Both less ✓. Answer: charge 75 dollars a month for 22 500 dollars of revenue.n=4：70×320=22400；n=6：80×280=22400。两边都更小 ✓。答：会费定 75 加元，月收入 22 500 加元。

  1. ✏️ Picture it画出来

    Let n = number of 1-dollar increases. Price =20+n dollars, tickets =300−10n.设涨价 n 次（每次 1 加元）。单价 =20+n 加元，销量 =300−10n 张。
  2. 🧰 Tool用什么工具

  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具

  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Vertex: n=−2(−10)100​=5.顶点：n=−2(−10)100​=5。
  5. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Price 20+5=25 dollars; tickets 300−50=250; R=25×250=6250 dollars.单价 20+5=25 加元；销量 300−50=250 张；R=25×250=6250 加元。
  6. ✅ Check检验
    Sense check: 6250 is more than the original 20×300=6000 ✓.合理性检查：6250 比原来的 20×300=6000 多 ✓。
  1. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    R=(20+n)(300−10n)=0 when n=−20 (free tickets) or n=30 (nobody buys).R=(20+n)(300−10n)=0 时，n=−20（票免费）或 n=30（没人买）。

2Giving n as the price把 n 当成了价格
A club sells 150 shirts at 12 dollars each. Each 1-dollar increase loses 5 sales. What price maximizes revenue?社团以每件 12 加元卖出 150 件 T 恤。每涨 1 加元，少卖 5 件。定价多少收入最高？

5Losing 1 sale per step instead of the given drop每涨一次只减 1 个，忘了题给的减少量
A museum has 400 visitors a day at 14 dollars each. Each 1-dollar increase loses 20 visitors. What price maximizes revenue?博物馆每张门票 14 加元时每天有 400 名游客。每涨 1 加元，少 20 名游客。票价定多少收入最高？

🧠 Each increase loses 20 visitors, so after n increases 20n are gone. Writing 400−n means only one visitor leaves per step, and the answer (207 dollars for a museum) fails the sense check.每涨一次少 20 人，涨 n 次就少 20n 人。写成 400−n 等于每次只走一个人，结果（博物馆门票 207 加元）通不过合理性检查。
