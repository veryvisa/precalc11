📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • a=1: two numbers that multiply to c and add to b.a=1：找两个数，积为 c、和为 b。
  • a=1: two numbers that multiply to ac and add to b; split the middle, then group.a=1：找积为 ac、和为 b 的两个数，拆中间项再分组。
  • Signs: c>0 → both take the sign of b; c<0 → opposite signs, bigger one takes the sign of b.符号：c>0 → 两数同号，与 b 同号；c<0 → 两数异号，绝对值大的与 b 同号。

💡 Guessing the factors of 6x2+7x−3 can take ten tries. The decomposition method finds them on the first try, every time.猜 6x2+7x−3 的因式可能要试十次。拆项法（十字相乘的系统版）每次第一次就能找到。

  1. When a=1: find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b. x2−2x−15: −5⋅3=−15, −5+3=−2, so (x−5)(x+3).a=1 时：找两个数，积为 c、和为 b。x2−2x−15：−5⋅3=−15，−5+3=−2，所以 (x−5)(x+3)。
  2. When a=1 (decomposition): find two numbers that multiply to a⋅c and add to b. Split the middle term with them, then factor by grouping.a=1 时（拆项法）：找两个数，积为 a⋅c、和为 b。用它们把中间项拆成两项，再分组分解。
  3. Signs guide the search: c>0 → both numbers have the sign of b; c<0 → opposite signs, the bigger one has the sign of b.符号帮你缩小范围：c>0 → 两数同号，且与 b 同号；c<0 → 两数异号，绝对值大的与 b 同号。
  4. Take out a GCF (and a negative leading coefficient) first. If no pair of integers works, the trinomial does not factor over the integers.先提公因式（首项为负也先提出负号）。如果找不到这样一对整数，这个三项式在整数范围内不能分解。

🎯 Test tip考试提示
Write the product–sum pair in the margin before you write any brackets, then expand the middle term to check. Most lost marks are sign errors that a 5-second check would catch.写括号之前先在旁边写出「积—和」那对数，写完再展开中间项检查。大多数失分都是符号错误，5 秒钟的检查就能发现。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Write a, b, c with their signs. First look for a GCF (and a negative leading coefficient) and take it out.带符号写出 a、b、c。先看有没有公因式（首项为负也算），有就先提出来。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Two brackets that multiply back to the original. "Factor fully" means nothing inside can be factored again.要两个括号，相乘能还原原式。「完全分解」意味着括号里不能再分解。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    A small sign table: product ac positive → same signs (the sign of b); negative → opposite signs, the bigger one takes the sign of b.画一个小符号表：积 ac 为正 → 同号（与 b 同号）；为负 → 异号，绝对值大的与 b 同号。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    If a=1, find two numbers with product c and sum b. If a=1, product ac and sum b, split the middle term, then group.a=1 时找积为 c、和为 b 的两个数；a=1 时找积为 ac、和为 b 的两个数，拆中间项，再分组。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Expand your brackets — at least the middle term (outer + inner). It must match bx exactly, sign included.把括号展开——至少算中间项（外项＋内项）。它必须和 bx 完全一样，包括符号。

🧱 Prerequisite check先修检查

4 quick Grade 9–10 questions, about 3 minutes. Miss one and a 5-minute repair card opens right there; fix it, then carry on.4 道九、十年级小题，约 3 分钟。错了就在原地打开 5 分钟补救卡，补好再继续。

1Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘
Work out (−9)+4.计算 (−9)+4。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Negative numbers: add, subtract, multiply负数的加减乘 (Grade年级 7–9)
Subtracting a negative is adding: 5−(−3)=5+3=8. Same signs multiply to +, different signs to −. Squaring a negative is positive: (−4)2=16, but −42=−16 (the square happens first).减去负数等于加上它：5−(−3)=5+3=8。同号相乘得正，异号相乘得负。负数的平方是正的：(−4)2=16，但 −42=−16（先算平方）。
  1. −7−(−2): change "minus negative" to "plus": −7+2=−5.−7−(−2)：把「减负」改成「加」：−7+2=−5。
  2. (−3)(−5)=15 (same signs), (−3)(5)=−15 (different signs).(−3)(−5)=15（同号），(−3)(5)=−15（异号）。
  3. Plugging in x=−3 to x2: write brackets first, (−3)2=9.把 x=−3 代入 x2：先加括号，(−3)2=9。
Try one:试一道：
Evaluate b2−4ac when a=2, b=−3, c=−1.当 a=2, b=−3, c=−1 时，求 b2−4ac。

2Multiplying binomials, (a+b)2二项式相乘与 (a+b)2
Expand (2x+3)(x−4).展开 (2x+3)(x−4)。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Multiplying binomials, (a+b)2二项式相乘与 (a+b)2 (Grade年级 9–10)
Every term times every term (FOIL): (x+3)(x−5)=x2−5x+3x−15=x2−2x−15. A square has a middle term: (x+3)2=x2+6x+9, never x2+9.每一项乘每一项：(x+3)(x−5)=x2−5x+3x−15=x2−2x−15。平方会有中间项：(x+3)2=x2+6x+9，绝不是 x2+9。
  1. (x−4)2=(x−4)(x−4)=x2−4x−4x+16=x2−8x+16.(x−4)2=(x−4)(x−4)=x2−4x−4x+16=x2−8x+16。
  2. Shortcut: (a±b)2=a2±2ab+b2 — middle term =2× first × last.捷径：(a±b)2=a2±2ab+b2——中间项＝2×首项×末项。
Try one:试一道：
Expand (2x−3)2.展开 (2x−3)2。

3Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）
Factor x2+7x+10.分解因式 x2+7x+10。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和） (Grade年级 10)
Take out a common factor first. Then find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b: x2−x−12 → −4 and 3 → (x−4)(x+3). Check by expanding.先提公因式。再找两个数，相乘等于 c、相加等于 b：x2−x−12 → −4 和 3 → (x−4)(x+3)。展开检验。
  1. x2+7x+10: 2×5=10, 2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5).x2+7x+10：2×5=10，2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5)。
  2. If c<0 the two numbers have different signs; if c>0 they share the sign of b.若 c<0，两个数异号；若 c>0，两个数与 b 同号。
Try one:试一道：
Factor x2−2x−15.分解 x2−2x−15。

4Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号）
Expand −3(x−5).去括号：−3(x−5)。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Distributing (and the minus sign)去括号（尤其是负号） (Grade年级 8–9)
Multiply EVERY term inside the bracket: 3(x−4)=3x−12. A minus in front changes every sign inside: −(x−5)=−x+5, and 2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1.括号里的每一项都要乘到：3(x−4)=3x−12。前面是负号时，括号里每一项都变号：−(x−5)=−x+5，2−(x+3)=2−x−3=−x−1。
  1. −2(3x−1)=−6x+2 (the −1 becomes +2).−2(3x−1)=−6x+2（−1 变成 +2）。
  2. x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6.x(x+2)−3(x+2)=x2+2x−3x−6=x2−x−6。
Try one:试一道：
Expand and simplify 4x−(2x−7).去括号并化简 4x−(2x−7)。

🪜 Worked → fill in → your turn例题 → 填空 → 自己做

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

① Worked example — read it完整例题——看懂
Factor 5x2+13x+6.分解因式 5x2+13x+6。
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    a=5, b=13, c=6. No common factor. ac=30.a=5，b=13，c=6。没有公因式。ac=30。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Two brackets like ( x+ )( x+ ) that multiply back to 5x2+13x+6.要两个形如 ( x+ )( x+ ) 的括号，相乘得 5x2+13x+6。
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Product +30, sum +13: both numbers positive. Pairs of 30: 1,30; 2,15; 3,10; 5,6. The sum 13 is 3+10.积 +30，和 +13：两个数都为正。30 的因数对：1,30；2,15；3,10；5,6。和为 13 的是 3+10。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Split the middle term: 5x2+10x+3x+6.拆中间项：5x2+10x+3x+6。
  5. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Group: 5x(x+2)+3(x+2)=(x+2)(5x+3).分组：5x(x+2)+3(x+2)=(x+2)(5x+3)。
  6. ✅ Check检验
    Middle term: outer 3x + inner 10x =13x ✓.中间项：外项 3x＋内项 10x＝13x ✓。
✔
(x+2)(5x+3)
