📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • Variable = what is asked; equation from area, Pythagoras or "height = value".设所求为未知数；由面积、勾股定理或「高度 = 某值」列方程。
  • A frame or path of width x adds 2x to each dimension.宽为 x 的边框或小路，使每个方向的长度都增加 2x。
  • Solve fully, then reject roots that make no sense (say why); keep both if both fit.先完整解出，再舍去不合理的根（写出理由）；两个都合理就都保留。

💡 A picture frame, a path around a garden, a ball in the air — the equation is the easy part once you know which method to grab and which answer to throw away.相框、花园四周的小路、空中的球——只要知道该用哪种方法、该舍掉哪个根，方程本身就不难。

  1. Pick the fastest method: a ready-made square → square roots; D is a perfect square → factor; a=1 with even b → complete the square; anything else → quadratic formula; decimals or a picture wanted → graph and read the x-intercepts.选最快的方法：有现成平方 → 开平方；D 是完全平方数 → 分解；a=1 且 b 为偶数 → 配方；其他情况 → 求根公式；要小数或有图 → 画图读 x 截距。
  2. Word problems: (1) let the variable be what you are asked for; (2) write the equation from the words (area, Pythagoras, height = value); (3) solve; (4) reject roots that make no sense (negative length or time).应用题：(1) 设所求量为未知数；(2) 根据题意列方程（面积、勾股定理、高度等于某值）；(3) 解方程；(4) 舍去不合实际的根（负的长度或时间）。
  3. Both roots can be valid: a ball passes a height once going up and once coming down.两个根也可能都有效：球上升时经过某高度一次，下落时又经过一次。

🎯 Test tip考试提示
Check D first — 10 seconds tells you whether to factor or reach for the formula. In word problems, always end with a sentence and units; marks are often lost for "x=4" with no "the frame is 4 cm wide".先看 D——10 秒钟就知道该分解还是用公式。应用题最后一定写一句带单位的话；只写「x=4」而不写「相框宽 4 cm」常常被扣分。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Read it twice and underline every number with what it describes. Let the variable be the thing asked for, then write every other quantity using it (width w, length w+5).读两遍，把每个数字和它描述的东西划出来。设所求的量为未知数，再用它表示其他量（宽 w，长 w+5）。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Which quantity, in which unit? One value, a pair of numbers, or every time something happens (a ball can pass a height twice)?求哪个量、什么单位？要一个值、一对数，还是「所有时刻」（球可能两次经过同一高度）？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Sketch it: a rectangle with the border labelled x on BOTH sides, a right triangle with its three sides labelled, or a height-vs-time arc with a line at the target height.画个图：矩形四周的边框「两侧」都标上 x；直角三角形三边都标好；或者画高度—时间的弧线，再画一条目标高度的水平线。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Write ONE equation from ONE fact: area = length × width, a2+b2=c2, or h(t)= the height. Move everything to one side; if D is a perfect square, factor, otherwise use the formula.用「一个事实」列「一个方程」：面积 = 长 × 宽、a2+b2=c2，或 h(t)= 某高度。全部移到一边；D 是完全平方数就分解，否则用公式。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Test each root against the WORDS: negative lengths and times go, with a reason. Put the kept answer back into the story, then finish with a sentence and units.每个根都拿回「题目原文」检验：负的长度、时间要写理由舍去。把留下的答案代回情境验算，最后写一句带单位的话。

1Multiplying binomials, (a+b)2二项式相乘与 (a+b)2
Expand (6+2x)(4+2x).展开 (6+2x)(4+2x)。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Multiplying binomials, (a+b)2二项式相乘与 (a+b)2 (Grade年级 9–10)
Every term times every term (FOIL): (x+3)(x−5)=x2−5x+3x−15=x2−2x−15. A square has a middle term: (x+3)2=x2+6x+9, never x2+9.每一项乘每一项：(x+3)(x−5)=x2−5x+3x−15=x2−2x−15。平方会有中间项：(x+3)2=x2+6x+9，绝不是 x2+9。
  1. (x−4)2=(x−4)(x−4)=x2−4x−4x+16=x2−8x+16.(x−4)2=(x−4)(x−4)=x2−4x−4x+16=x2−8x+16。
  2. Shortcut: (a±b)2=a2±2ab+b2 — middle term =2× first × last.捷径：(a±b)2=a2±2ab+b2——中间项＝2×首项×末项。

2Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和）
Solve x2+5x−24=0.解 x2+5x−24=0。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Factoring x2+bx+c (product and sum)分解 x2+bx+c（积与和） (Grade年级 10)
Take out a common factor first. Then find two numbers that MULTIPLY to c and ADD to b: x2−x−12 → −4 and 3 → (x−4)(x+3). Check by expanding.先提公因式。再找两个数，相乘等于 c、相加等于 b：x2−x−12 → −4 和 3 → (x−4)(x+3)。展开检验。
  1. x2+7x+10: 2×5=10, 2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5).x2+7x+10：2×5=10，2+5=7 → (x+2)(x+5)。
  2. If c<0 the two numbers have different signs; if c>0 they share the sign of b.若 c<0，两个数异号；若 c>0，两个数与 b 同号。
Try one:试一道：
Factor x2−2x−15.分解 x2−2x−15。

3SOH CAH TOA and Pythagoras直角三角形：SOH CAH TOA 与勾股定理
A right triangle has legs 9 cm and 12 cm. How long is the hypotenuse?直角三角形两条直角边是 9 cm 和 12 cm，斜边多长？

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · SOH CAH TOA and Pythagoras直角三角形：SOH CAH TOA 与勾股定理 (Grade年级 10)
In a RIGHT triangle, from angle θ: sinθ=hypopp​, cosθ=hypadj​, tanθ=adjopp​. The hypotenuse is opposite the right angle; a2+b2=c2. Calculator in DEGREE mode.在直角三角形中，从角 θ 看：sinθ=斜边对边​，cosθ=斜边邻边​，tanθ=邻边对边​。斜边对着直角；a2+b2=c2。计算器设为角度（DEG）模式。
  1. Legs 3 and 4: hypotenuse 9+16​=5; sinθ=53​ for the angle opposite 3.两直角边 3 和 4：斜边 9+16​=5；对着 3 的角 sinθ=53​。
  2. Find an angle: θ=sin−1(0.6)≈36.9∘.求角：θ=sin−1(0.6)≈36.9∘。

4Solving a linear equation解一元一次方程
A rectangle is w cm wide and 4 cm longer than it is wide. Its perimeter is 36 cm. Find w.一个长方形宽 w cm，长比宽多 4 cm，周长 36 cm。求 w。

🩹 5-minute repair5 分钟补一补 · Solving a linear equation解一元一次方程 (Grade年级 8–9)
Do the same thing to both sides. Clear brackets, collect x on one side and numbers on the other, then divide. With fractions, multiply every term by the common denominator first.两边做同样的事。先去括号，把含 x 的项移到一边、数字移到另一边，最后除以系数。有分数时先把每一项都乘以公分母。
  1. 3(x−2)=x+8 → 3x−6=x+8 → 2x=14 → x=7.3(x−2)=x+8 → 3x−6=x+8 → 2x=14 → x=7。
  2. 2x​+3x​=5: multiply every term by 6 → 3x+2x=30 → x=6.2x​+3x​=5：每项乘 6 → 3x+2x=30 → x=6。
  3. Check by substituting: 3(7−2)=15 and 7+8=15 ✓.代回检验：3(7−2)=15，7+8=15 ✓。

🪜 Worked → fill in → your turn例题 → 填空 → 自己做

Three versions of one idea: read a full solution, then finish a half-done one, then do one yourself with hints you open one at a time.同一个思路的三个版本：先看完整解答，再补全半成品，最后自己做——提示一次只开一个。

① Worked example — read it完整例题——看懂
A 9 m by 5 m pool has a deck of uniform width all around it. Pool and deck together cover 117 m². How wide is the deck?一个 9 m × 5 m 的泳池四周铺了等宽的木平台，泳池加平台共 117 m²。平台多宽？
  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Let x = the deck width in metres. Numbers: pool 9 by 5, total 117.设平台宽 x 米。已知：泳池 9 × 5，总面积 117。
  2. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Sketch: the deck is on both ends and both sides, so the outer rectangle is (9+2x) by (5+2x).画图：两端、两侧都有平台，所以外面的大矩形是 (9+2x)×(5+2x)。
  3. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Area fact: (9+2x)(5+2x)=117, so 4x2+28x+45=117, so 4x2+28x−72=0. Divide by 4: x2+7x−18=0.面积关系：(9+2x)(5+2x)=117，即 4x2+28x+45=117，即 4x2+28x−72=0。除以 4：x2+7x−18=0。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    D=49+72=121=112, a perfect square, so factor: (x+9)(x−2)=0, x=2 or x=−9.D=49+72=121=112 是完全平方数，所以分解：(x+9)(x−2)=0，x=2 或 x=−9。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    A width cannot be −9, so reject it. Check: 13×9=117 ✓. The deck is 2 m wide.宽度不能是 −9，舍去。验算：13×9=117 ✓。平台宽 2 m。
✔

Two cyclists leave a junction near Kelowna at the same time, one heading east and one heading north. The eastbound cyclist is 5 km/h faster. After 2 hours they are 50 km apart in a straight line. Find both speeds and explain why a second root is rejected.两名骑行者同时从基洛纳附近的一个路口出发，一个向东、一个向北，向东的比向北的每小时快 5 km。2 小时后两人直线距离 50 km。求两人的速度，并说明为什么要舍去另一个根。
Let the northbound speed be v. Distances after 2 h are 2v and 2(v+5).设向北速度为 v。2 小时后的距离是 2v 和 2(v+5)。
Northbound 15 km/h, eastbound 20 km/h. The root v=−20 is rejected because a speed cannot be negative.向北 15 km/h，向东 20 km/h。根 v=−20 要舍去，因为速度不能为负。
  1. (2v)2+(2v+10)2=502⇒8v2+40v+100=2500⇒v2+5v−300=0.(2v)2+(2v+10)2=502⇒8v2+40v+100=2500⇒v2+5v−300=0。
  2. (v+20)(v−15)=0, so v=15 or v=−20.(v+20)(v−15)=0，所以 v=15 或 v=−20。
  3. A speed cannot be negative, so reject −20. Northbound 15 km/h, eastbound 20 km/h. Check: legs 30 and 40, 302+402=2500 ✓.速度不能为负，舍去 −20。向北 15 km/h，向东 20 km/h。验算：两边 30 和 40，302+402=2500 ✓。
