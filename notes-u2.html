Simplifying & ordering radicals根式化简与比较大小

  • Mixed radical → simplest form: pull out the LARGEST perfect-square factor. 72​=36⋅2​=62​. For cube roots, pull out perfect cubes: 354​=327⋅2​=332​.化成最简根式：提出最大的完全平方因数。72​=36⋅2​=62​。立方根就提完全立方因数：354​=327⋅2​=332​。
  • Mixed → entire: square the outside number and multiply it in. 35​=9⋅5​=45​. For index n, raise it to the nth power.最简根式 → 整根式：把根号外的数平方后乘进去。35​=9⋅5​=45​。根指数为 n 时就乘 n 次方。
  • To order radicals, write them all as entire radicals with the same index and compare what is inside.比较根式大小：全部化成同一根指数的整根式，比较根号里的数。
  • With variables, an even index needs a non-negative radicand: 18x3​=3x2x​ only makes sense for x≥0. State the restriction.含字母时，偶次根号里必须非负：18x3​=3x2x​ 只在 x≥0 时有意义，要写出限制条件。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Not using the LARGEST square: 72​=218​ is not finished, since 18​=32​ still simplifies. Check the leftover radicand has no square factor.没用最大的平方因数：72​=218​ 还没化完，18​=32​ 还能化简。最后检查根号里不能再有平方因数。
  • Forgetting to square when moving in: 35​=45​, not 15​.挪进根号时忘了平方：35​=45​，不是 15​。
  • Splitting a sum: 9+16​=5, not 3+4. The product rule works for × only.把和拆开：9+16​=5，不是 3+4。乘法法则只对乘法成立。

🎯 Ordering and "simplify fully" questions are almost always non-calculator. Convert everything to entire radicals for ordering, and always ask "can the leftover radicand still be divided by 4, 9, 16 or 25?" before you stop.比较大小和「完全化简」题几乎都不许用计算器。比较时全部化成整根式；化简完再问一句：「剩下的根号内还能被 4、9、16、25 整除吗？」

Operations with radicals根式的四则运算

  • Add/subtract only LIKE radicals (same index, same radicand) — like collecting like terms: 32​+52​=82​. Simplify every radical first; unlike-looking ones often become like.只有同类根式（根指数相同、被开方数相同）才能加减——和合并同类项一样：32​+52​=82​。先把每个根式化简，看起来不同类的常常会变成同类。
  • Multiply: outside × outside, inside × inside: (23​)(56​)=1018​=302​. Brackets are expanded exactly like polynomials (distribute / FOIL).乘法：根号外乘根号外，根号内乘根号内：(23​)(56​)=1018​=302​。带括号的就像多项式一样展开（分配律 / FOIL）。
  • Rationalize a monomial denominator: multiply top and bottom by the radical: 3​6​⋅3​3​​=23​.分母是单项根式时有理化：分子分母同乘这个根式：3​6​⋅3​3​​=23​。
  • Binomial denominator: multiply by its conjugate (a+b​ ↔ a−b​). Difference of squares kills the root: (a+b​)(a−b​)=a2−b.分母是二项式时，乘以它的共轭式（a+b​ ↔ a−b​）。平方差公式消掉根号：(a+b​)(a−b​)=a2−b。

⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Adding unlike radicals: 2​+3​=5​. If the radicands differ after simplifying, leave them.把不同类根式相加：2​+3​=5​。化简后被开方数不同，就保持原样。
  • Multiplying only the bottom by the conjugate. Top and bottom must both be multiplied, or the value changes.只给分母乘共轭式。分子分母必须同乘，否则式子的值变了。
  • Squaring a binomial wrong: (3−2​)2=9−62​+2, not 9+2. Do not forget the middle term.二项式平方出错：(3−2​)2=9−62​+2，不是 9+2。别漏中间项。
  • Cancelling across a sum: 26+43​​=3+23​ (divide both terms), not 3+43​.只约掉一部分：26+43​​=3+23​（两项都要除），不是 3+43​。

🎯 Every "simplify" answer must have no radical in the denominator and no radicand with a square factor. A quick decimal check of the original vs your answer catches almost every sign slip.「化简」的最终答案分母不能有根号，被开方数不能有平方因数。用小数对比原式和答案，几乎能抓住所有符号错误。

Radical equations & extraneous roots根式方程与增根

  • Four steps: (1) state restrictions (radicand ≥0 for even index); (2) ISOLATE the radical; (3) raise both sides to the index (square, cube…); (4) solve and CHECK every answer in the ORIGINAL equation.四步：(1) 写限制条件（偶次根号内 ≥0）；(2) 把根式单独移到一边；(3) 两边同时乘方（平方、立方……）；(4) 解出后把每个答案代回原方程检验。
  • Squaring loses the sign: 2=−2 is false, but 22=(−2)2 is true. A root that solves the squared equation but not the original is an extraneous root — reject it.平方会丢掉符号：2=−2 不成立，但 22=(−2)2 成立。满足平方后方程、却不满足原方程的根叫增根，要舍去。
  • A principal square root is never negative. So …​=x−3 also needs x−3≥0, and x​=−4 has no solution at all.算术平方根永远非负。所以 …​=x−3 还要求 x−3≥0，而 x​=−4 根本无解。
  • Graphically, the solutions are the x-coordinates where y= left side and y= right side intersect. Extraneous roots simply do not show up as intersections.从图象看，解就是 y=左边 与 y=右边 两个图象交点的横坐标。增根在图上不会出现交点。

Squaring step and the hidden condition平方这一步及隐含条件
⚠️ Traps陷阱
  • Squaring term by term: (x​+1)2 is x+2x​+1, not x+1. Isolate the radical BEFORE squaring so this never happens.逐项平方：(x​+1)2=x+2x​+1，不是 x+1。先把根式单独移到一边再平方，就不会出这种错。
  • Skipping the check. Every root from a squared equation must be substituted into the ORIGINAL equation; an extraneous root left in the answer usually costs the mark.不检验。平方后得到的每个根都必须代回原方程；答案里留着增根通常会扣分。
  • Forgetting to square the coefficient: 2x+4​ squared is 4(x+4), not 2(x+4).忘了把系数平方：2x+4​ 平方是 4(x+4)，不是 2(x+4)。
  • Solving x​=−4 to get x=16. A square root cannot equal a negative number: no solution.把 x​=−4 解成 x=16。算术平方根不可能等于负数：无解。

🎯 Tests almost always build the question so one root is extraneous. Write "Check:" with both substitutions and a ✓/✗ — that line is often worth a full mark on its own.考题几乎总会设计成有一个增根。写上「检验：」并把两个根都代入、标 ✓/✗——这一行往往单独就值一分。
