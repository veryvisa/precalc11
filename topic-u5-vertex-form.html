📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • y=a(x−p)2+q: vertex (p,q), axis x=p. (x+3)2 means p=−3.y=a(x−p)2+q：顶点 (p,q)，对称轴 x=p。(x+3)2 表示 p=−3。
  • ∣a∣>1 narrower, ∣a∣<1 wider, a<0 flips. From the vertex: over 1, 2, 3 → up a, 4a, 9a.∣a∣>1 变窄，∣a∣<1 变宽，a<0 翻转。从顶点出发：右移 1、2、3 → 上升 a、4a、9a。
  • x-intercepts: a,q opposite signs → 2; q=0 → 1; same signs → 0.x 截距个数：a,q 异号 → 2 个；q=0 → 1 个；同号 → 0 个。

💡 Why memorize dozens of parabolas? Every one of them is just y=x2 stretched, flipped and slid — and vertex form tells you exactly how.何必死记各种抛物线？每一条都只是 y=x2 经过拉伸、翻折、平移得到的——顶点式把怎么变的全写在脸上。

  1. Vertex form: y=a(x−p)2+q. The vertex is (p,q), the axis is x=p, and the max/min value is q.顶点式：y=a(x−p)2+q。顶点是 (p,q)，对称轴是 x=p，最值是 q。
  2. p slides the graph left/right — opposite to the sign you see: (x−3)2 moves right 3, (x+3)2 moves left 3. q slides up/down with its own sign.p 让图象左右平移，方向与看到的符号相反：(x−3)2 向右 3，(x+3)2 向左 3。q 上下平移，方向与符号一致。
  3. a is the shape: ∣a∣>1 narrower (vertical stretch), 0<∣a∣<1 wider, a<0 reflected in the x-axis. Step pattern from the vertex: over 1 up a, over 2 up 4a, over 3 up 9a.a 决定形状：∣a∣>1 变窄（竖直拉伸），0<∣a∣<1 变宽，a<0 关于 x 轴翻折。从顶点出发的步长规律：横走 1 竖走 a，横走 2 竖走 4a，横走 3 竖走 9a。
  4. Number of x-intercepts without solving: if a and q have opposite signs → 2; q=0 → 1; same signs → 0.不用解方程就知道 x 截距个数：a 与 q 异号 → 2 个；q=0 → 1 个；同号 → 0 个。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Read a, p and q. Rewrite the bracket as (x−p) so the sign of p is honest: (x+3) means p=−3.读出 a、p、q。把括号写成 (x−p) 的样子，p 的符号就不会看错：(x+3) 表示 p=−3。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Which one is wanted: the vertex, axis, max or min, range, the transformations, the number of x-intercepts, or the equation itself (find a)?要的是哪一样：顶点、对称轴、最值、值域、变换过程、x 截距个数，还是方程本身（求 a）？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Plot the vertex, draw the axis x=p as a dashed line, then step out 1, 2, 3 units and go up a, 4a, 9a on both sides.先描顶点，用虚线画对称轴 x=p，再从顶点横走 1、2、3，竖走 a、4a、9a，两边都画。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    To describe: a stretches or flips, p moves left or right, q moves up or down. To find the equation: write y=a(x−p)2+q with the vertex filled in, substitute the OTHER point, solve for a.描述图象：a 管伸缩和翻折，p 管左右，q 管上下。求方程：把顶点填进 y=a(x−p)2+q，代入「另一个」点，解出 a。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Put x=p into your equation: you must get q. Put the other point in: it must work. Sense check: a point below the vertex means a<0.把 x=p 代入方程，必须得到 q。把另一个点代入，也必须成立。合理性：点在顶点下方，a 就小于 0。
