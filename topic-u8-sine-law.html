📌 Key points要点

⭐ Must know必须记住
  • sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​; side a is opposite angle A.sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​；边 a 对角 A。
  • Needs a complete pair (side + opposite angle): AAS, ASA, SSA. SAS or SSS → cosine law.需要一组完整的「边＋对角」：AAS、ASA、SSA 可用。SAS、SSS → 用余弦定理。
  • Unknown on top: sides on top for a side, sines on top for an angle.未知量放分子：求边时边在上，求角时正弦在上。

💡 Two fire lookout towers 10 km apart each spot the same smoke. They know only their angles — yet they can compute exactly how far away the fire is. No right angle needed.相距 10 km 的两座防火瞭望塔同时看到同一股烟。他们只知道各自的观测角，却能精确算出火场有多远——不需要任何直角。

  1. In any triangle, each side divided by the sine of its opposite angle gives the same number: sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​. Lowercase a is the side opposite angle A.在任何三角形中，每条边除以它对角的正弦，得数都相同：sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​。小写 a 是角 A 的对边。
  2. Use it when you know a complete pair (a side AND its opposite angle) plus one more side or angle: AAS, ASA, or SSA.当你知道一组完整的「边—对角」，再加一条边或一个角时使用：AAS、ASA 或 SSA。
  3. Finding a side: put the unknown side on top, sinBb​=sinAa​. Finding an angle: put sines on top, bsinB​=asinA​. The angles always add to 180∘ — use that for the third angle.求边：未知边放分子，sinBb​=sinAa​。求角：正弦放分子，bsinB​=asinA​。三角之和为 180∘，第三个角用它求。

🧮 Key formulas关键公式

sinAa​=sinBb​=sinCc​
Sine law (finding sides)正弦定理（求边）
asinA​=bsinB​=csinC​
Sine law (finding angles)正弦定理（求角）
🎯 Test tip考试提示
Always sketch and label the triangle, and circle the complete side–angle pair before writing any equation. In context questions, the sine law usually finds a slanted side first; a right-triangle step then gives the height or width.先画图标字母，在列式前圈出那一组完整的「边—对角」。实际问题中，正弦定理通常先求出一条斜边，再用直角三角形求高度或宽度。

🧭 How to get started怎么入手

Ask yourself these five, in order, before writing anything. Every worked example on this page is labelled with them.动笔之前，按顺序问自己这五个问题。本页每道例题的步骤都标了它们。

  1. 🔎 Given已知
    Label the triangle: side a across from angle A, and so on. Find a complete pair (a side AND its opposite angle). If two angles are known, the third is 180∘ minus them.先标三角形：边 a 在角 A 的对面，依此类推。找一组完整的「边—对角」。若已知两个角，第三个角是 180∘ 减去它们。
  2. 🎯 Asked求什么
    Which side or angle, and how to round. A side (cm, m, km) or an angle (degrees)?求哪条边或哪个角？保留几位？求的是边（cm、m、km）还是角（度）？
  3. ✏️ Picture it画出来
    Sketch it roughly to scale. The biggest angle faces the longest side: use that to predict your answer before you calculate.大致按比例画草图。最大的角对着最长的边：计算之前先用它预估答案。
  4. 🧰 Tool用什么工具
    Unknown on top: sinBb​=sinAa​ for a side, bsinB​=asinA​ for an angle. Calculator in degrees; keep full values until the end.未知量放分子：求边用 sinBb​=sinAa​，求角用 bsinB​=asinA​。计算器用角度制，中间值全部保留到最后。
  5. ✅ Check检验
    Do the sides come in the same order as their opposite angles? Do the angles add to 180∘? Any negative length means radian mode.边的大小顺序和对角的顺序一致吗？三角之和是 180∘ 吗？出现负的边长，说明计算器在弧度制。

In △ABC, ∠A=35∘, ∠B=80∘ and c=12. Find a to the nearest tenth.在 △ABC 中，∠A=35∘，∠B=80∘，c=12。求 a，精确到 0.1。

4Calculator in radian mode计算器在弧度制
In △ABC, ∠A=50∘, ∠B=70∘ and a=8. Find b to the nearest tenth.在 △ABC 中，∠A=50∘，∠B=70∘，a=8。求 b，精确到 0.1。

🧠 The set-up is right, but the calculator read 70 and 50 as radians. Sines of angles between 0∘ and 180∘ are always positive, so a negative value is the warning sign.式子是对的，但计算器把 70 和 50 当成了弧度。0∘ 到 180∘ 之间的角正弦都是正的，出现负数就是警报。

🔧 In degrees: 0.76608(0.9397)​≈9.8.用角度制：0.76608(0.9397)​≈9.8。

✔ b≈9.8

5Rounding the sine too early正弦值过早取整
In △ABC, a=14, b=10 and ∠A=65∘. Find ∠B to the nearest tenth.在 △ABC 中，a=14，b=10，∠A=65∘。求 ∠B，精确到 0.1°。

🧠 Rounding 0.6474 to 0.65 looks harmless, but sin−1 magnifies it: the angle moves by 0.2∘, which changes the tenths digit.把 0.6474 取成 0.65 看似无害，但 sin−1 会放大误差：角度差了 0.2∘，十分位就变了。

🔧 Keep the full value: sinB≈0.647363, so B≈40.3∘. (Only one triangle: b<a, so B<A.)保留完整值：sinB≈0.647363，所以 B≈40.3∘。（只有一个三角形：b<a，所以 B<A。）

✔ ∠B≈40.3∘

⚠️ Quick list速查

  • Pairing a side with the wrong angle. Label first: side a sits across from angle A, never touching it.边和角配错。先标好：边 a 在角 A 的对面，不和它相接。
  • Trying the sine law with SAS or SSS. Without a complete side–angle pair it cannot start — use the cosine law.已知 SAS 或 SSS 还用正弦定理。没有完整的「边—对角」就开不了头——要用余弦定理。
  • Rounding sinB early (e.g. to 0.7) and getting 44.4∘ instead of 44.8∘. Keep the full value in the calculator.过早把 sinB 取整（如取 0.7），结果得 44.4∘ 而不是 44.8∘。计算器里保留完整数值。
